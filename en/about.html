About the Project

Hypnosis from Scratch is an open educational project dedicated to practical hypnosis. The course is based on the Jerry Kain (Kayfabe) method — a system of instant inductions, shock techniques, and rapid hypnosis methods.

Course Foundation

The course is built on the Jerry Kain (Kayfabe) method — a system of rapid hypnotic inductions that combines shock inductions, instant techniques, and confusion methods.

At its core is the classic Dave Elman induction (deep somnambulism in 4–5 minutes) and Kayfabe rapid inductions.

Jerry Kain is an American hypnotist and creator of the Kayfabe system. His method is characterized by clear structure, no esoteric elements, and a focus on fast, reproducible results.

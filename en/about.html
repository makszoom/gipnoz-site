About the Project

Hypnosis from Scratch is an open educational project featuring Gerald Kein's original certification program recorded at the Omni Hypnosis Training Center in Florida. Original English audio, 19 video lessons, all free.

Course Foundation

This course is Gerald Kein's original hypnosis certification curriculum, recorded live at the Omni Hypnosis Training Center. Kein was a direct student of Dave Elman and spent over 30 years teaching and refining the Elman method.

The program covers the full spectrum: from the Elman induction, Chevreul pendulum, and somnambulism to advanced therapy techniques — age regression, forgiveness therapy, pain control, and past life regression.

Gerald Kein was one of the most influential hypnosis instructors of the 20th century. His training is known for its hands-on approach, real case studies, and focus on clinical results rather than theory.

About This Lesson

A professional hypnotist needs multiple induction methods. The Elman induction works for most people but not all. Rapid inductions (30-60 seconds) and instant inductions (1-3 seconds) are essential tools for analytical clients, busy professionals, and experienced subjects.

When to Use Each Type

Elman induction: first sessions, anxious clients, anyone who needs gradual trust-building.

Rapid induction: returning clients, second+ sessions, subjects who already know they are hypnotizable.

Instant induction: analytical clients (bypasses their critical factor before they can analyze), emergency situations, demonstrations.

Rapid Induction: Handshake Interrupt

(1) Shake the subject's hand normally. (2) Mid-shake, pause and intensify eye contact. (3) Say "Take a deep breath." (4) As they inhale, release their hand and say "Sleep." (5) Guide them into the chair. The cognitive overload of the interrupted handshake bypasses the critical factor.

Rapid Induction: Arm Pull

(1) Ask the subject to stand. (2) "Extend your arm. I'm going to pull you forward. When I pull, close your eyes and let yourself fall into the trance." (3) Pull firmly — as they fall, say "Sleep now." (4) Catch and guide into the chair. Works because the brain cannot simultaneously process falling and resisting hypnosis.

Instant Induction: Eye Closure Overload

(1) "Look at my thumb." (2) Move thumb toward their face — they naturally follow with their eyes. (3) When eyes cross slightly, snap fingers and say "Close your eyes — deep sleep."

Personality Matching

Analytical/logical clients: use instant inductions (they can't analyze what happened). Emotional/creative clients: use Elman or progressive relaxation. Test by asking a few questions before choosing your method.

🧠 Key Takeaway: Having one induction is like having one tool. Build a toolkit of 3-5 inductions and learn to match them to the client.

📝 Practice: Rapid & Instant Inductions

Practice each induction until the movements become automatic. Drill each variant 20 times.

Variant 1: Handshake Induction

Shake hands. Pause. "Take a deep breath." Pull slightly as they inhale. Release. "Sleep." Guide to chair.

Variant 2: Arm Pull Induction

"Stand up. Extend your arm. I'll pull you into trance. When I pull — close your eyes, let go, and sleep." Pull firmly, catch, guide down.

Variant 3: Eye Fixation Instant

"Look at my thumb." Move toward their nose. When eyes cross: "Close your eyes — sleep!"

About This Lesson

This lesson marks the transition from beginner to advanced work. Kein teaches that a professional hypnotist must have multiple induction methods — not just the Elman induction — because not every client will respond to the same approach. The advanced toolkit includes rapid inductions (30-60 seconds) and instant inductions (1-3 seconds).

Kein addresses the fear that even experienced hypnotists feel when attempting instant inductions — including himself. He shares a story of returning from vacation and instinctively switching from an instant induction to the Elman induction at the last second, mentally calling himself a coward. The lesson: you must challenge yourself and push past the fear. These inductions are not unprofessional — they are essential tools.

When to Use Instant Inductions

Instant inductions are ideal for analytical clients — people who overthink every suggestion. They work by bypassing the critical factor so quickly that the conscious mind doesn't have time to engage resistance. They're also perfect for late-afternoon clients who are already mentally fatigued: the more tired someone is, the shorter the induction should be.

Instant inductions are NOT for everyone. They require good judgment to select the right candidate. A nervous first-time client needs a gentler approach. But with the right person, an instant induction can produce deep somnambulism in seconds.

Overcoming the Fear

Kein admits that after decades of practice, instant inductions still challenge him. The key is to practice relentlessly and to push yourself every session. If you find yourself defaulting to the Elman induction because it's comfortable, you're not growing. Dedicate a portion of every session to a fast or instant induction — even if it fails, the experience is valuable.

The Vacation Effect

Kein tells a story about returning from a week-long vacation. His first client was a perfect candidate for an instant induction — analytical, focused, eager. At the last moment, Kein defaulted to the Elman induction. While he delivered the induction, part of his mind was screaming "Coward!" The lesson: instant inductions require constant practice, especially after breaks. Don't let skill atrophy.

🧠 Key Takeaway: A hypnotist with only one induction is like a carpenter with only one hammer. Learn instant inductions, practice them daily, and push through the fear. Your clients — especially analytical ones — will thank you.

📝 Practice: Instant Induction

Practice the rapid handshake induction 20 times today. The movements should become automatic, requiring no conscious thought.

Handshake Induction

"Shake my hand. Look me in the eyes. [During handshake, pause and intensify eye contact] Take a deep breath. [Pull hand slightly, then release] Sleep!"

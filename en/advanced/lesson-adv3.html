About This Lesson

When a subject shows strong emotion during a session — anger, tears, fear — that emotion is a doorway to the root cause. This lesson teaches how to recognize emotional signals and use spontaneous regression to access and resolve the originating event.

Recognizing Emotional Signals

During pre-talk or session, if a subject suddenly becomes angry, tearful, or withdrawn, note what topic was being discussed. That topic is the key. Make a mental note: "We were discussing X when the emotion appeared." Return to X during regression.

Spontaneous Regression Protocol

(1) When emotion appears: "That feeling is important. Close your eyes. Let that feeling grow. Where in your body do you feel it? Good. Now let your mind go back to the first time you felt this. You don't need to know what it is — just let it come." (2) Guide them through the memory without interpretation. (3) When complete: "Now let that image fade. The feeling is released. You are safe."

Deathbed Therapy Protocol

For unresolved grief: (1) Induce somnambulism. (2) Guide to the moment of death or last interaction. (3) Allow them to say everything unsaid. (4) Guide through the goodbye. (5) Install understanding: "They are at peace. You are allowed to continue living."

Empty Chair Technique

For unfinished business with a living person: (1) Place an empty chair. (2) "Your [father/mother/partner] is sitting in that chair. Tell them what you never told them." (3) Switch chairs: "Now be them. What would they say back?" (4) Continue until resolution.

🧠 Key Takeaway: Emotion is not the enemy of therapy — it's the signal that points to the root cause. Train yourself to notice emotional shifts and use them as entry points.

📝 Practice: Emotional Release Techniques

Practice each emotional release technique. Pay close attention to emotional shifts in your subject and note what topic preceded any change.

Variant 1: Spontaneous Regression Prompt

"That feeling — close your eyes. Let it grow. Where is it in your body? Now go back to the first time you felt this. Let the memory come. Describe what you see."

Variant 2: Deathbed Protocol

"You're with them again. Say what you never said. Take your time. [Pause.] Now hear their response. They are at peace. You can let go."

Variant 3: Emotional Release Somatic

"Focus on the tension in your chest/throat/stomach. Breathe into it. On the exhale, let a little go. Each breath releases more. Keep going until it's empty."

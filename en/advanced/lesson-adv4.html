About This Lesson

This is the longest lesson in the course (nearly 2 hours), covering two major topics: the business side of hypnosis and the corridor technique for age regression. Kein teaches that building a practice requires the same dedication as learning hypnosis — and provides specific, actionable strategies.

The second half of the lesson introduces the Corridor Technique for age regression — a powerful visualization method where the subject walks down a corridor of doors, each representing a year of their life. When they stop at a door, they enter the room and re-experience the significant events stored there.

Part 1: Building Your Practice

Kein's marketing strategy is simple: brochures, business cards, and footwork. Print professional brochures (tri-fold) with three business cards attached to each. Then walk 5 miles a day, visiting every business on one side of the street, then the other. Walk into each establishment, hand a brochure to a living human, say "I just opened my practice in the neighborhood — here's my brochure!" and leave. Do NOT try to sell. Just distribute.

The psychology: people who don't need hypnosis will pass the brochure to someone who does. "My friend Stella in shipping needs to lose weight — she might want this." The brochures circulate. Kein also recommends adding a "10% off first session" coupon to track which brochures produce results. Then follow up with doctor referrals using the letter templates from B09.

Part 2: The Corridor Technique

The age regression technique: guide the subject into deep somnambulism, then ask them to imagine a long corridor with many doors. Each door represents a year of their life. On the count of one, they begin walking slowly down the corridor, looking at each door. When they feel a pull toward a particular door, they signal by raising their finger.

At the chosen door, they enter a room with a pedestal and two buttons: green and red. Pressing green projects a significant memory from that year onto the wall. The subject describes what they see. When Kein judges that enough has been revealed, he guides them to press the red button, which makes the memory disappear. This continues until the root memory is identified and processed.

🧠 Key Takeaway: Two halves of the same coin: you need clients (marketing) and skills to help them (age regression). The 5-mile walk method works because it's personal and consistent. The corridor technique works because it gives the subconscious a structured way to reveal what needs healing.

📝 Practice: The Corridor

Read through the corridor regression script until you can visualize it clearly. Practice guiding a partner through the first three steps: corridor, door selection, green button.

Age Regression — Corridor Opening

"You're standing at the entrance of a long corridor. On either side are doors, each one representing a year of your life. The doors get younger as you walk. When you feel a pull toward any door — a curiosity, a strange feeling — stop and raise your finger. We'll enter that room together. Behind the green button is a memory. Behind the red button is release."

Adv5 — Forgiveness Therapy

~110 minutes

🎬 Video will be added after upload to YouTube

About This Lesson

Forgiveness therapy is one of the most powerful therapeutic tools in hypnosis. The premise: until a person forgives those who have harmed them, they remain emotionally controlled by them. The anger and resentment keep the wound open. Forgiveness is not about excusing the behavior — it's about freeing the self from the burden.

This lesson covers the full forgiveness protocol: establishing readiness through the forgiveness decision, the step-by-step forgiveness process under hypnosis, handling cases where forgiveness appears impossible, and post-forgiveness integration to install new patterns.

The Forgiveness Decision

Before doing forgiveness work, obtain conscious consent from the client. Frame forgiveness as self-liberation, not moral obligation. Use this script to present the decision:

"I want you to take a moment and think about the person who has caused you pain. Bring their face to mind. Now ask yourself this question, honestly. Are you ready to forgive? Not because what they did was okay — it may never be okay. Not because they deserve your forgiveness — they may not. But because you deserve to be free. Forgiveness is not for them. It is for you. It is you choosing to put down a weight you were never meant to carry. Are you ready to take your power back? Are you ready to forgive — for yourself?"

Approximately 95% of clients say yes when asked this way. If they say no, do not push — end the session and work on readiness in future appointments.

The Forgiveness Protocol

Step 1: Achieve deep somnambulism. The subject must be deeply relaxed and receptive to inner work.

Step 2: Guide to the memory of the harm. Ask them to visualize the scene and the person who harmed them.

Step 3: Use the forgiveness release script below.

Step 4: Guide them to feel the release physically — weight lifting from shoulders, tightness releasing, knot dissolving.

Step 5: Reinforce the new state of freedom and empowerment.

"See the person who harmed you standing before you now. They are right there in front of you. Look at them. You do not have to like them. You do not have to agree with what they did. But look at them and say these words. Say them silently in your mind: 'I forgive you. I release this. I take my power back.' Say it again. Feel the truth of it. Now notice what happens in your body. Feel the weight lifting from your shoulders. Notice the tightness in your chest beginning to release. The knot in your stomach is dissolving. Breathe deeply and feel the freedom entering your body. They no longer have power over your emotions. You have taken your power back. From this moment on, you are free."

When Forgiveness Is Impossible

For extreme trauma — such as rape, torture, or genocide — some clients genuinely cannot forgive. Pushing them will cause harm and erode trust. Use this containment script instead:

"You do not have to forgive them. That is not required here. Some things are too heavy for that. But here is what you can do. Imagine the person who harmed you standing before you. Now imagine placing them inside a box. A strong, secure box. Close the lid. Seal it. They stay there. They cannot reach you anymore. Now turn around and walk away. Walk away from that box. With every step, you feel lighter. With every step, you leave more of the weight behind. From now on, you decide what enters your life. You decide who has access to your heart and your mind. That box stays behind. You walk forward into freedom."

Post-Forgiveness Integration

After the forgiveness work is complete, install new patterns with this integration script:

"You are no longer defined by what happened. You are defined by who you choose to become from this moment. Your past no longer controls your future. The person you were — the one who carried that pain — is in the past. The person you are now is free. You have released what was not yours to carry. From this day forward, you walk a new path. Every breath you take fills you with peace. Every step you take moves you toward wholeness. You are free. You are whole. You are healed."

🧠 Key Takeaway: Forgiveness is for the forgiver, not the forgiven. Frame it as self-liberation, not moral obligation. Approximately 95% of clients will choose to forgive when the choice is presented correctly. For the rest, containment offers a path to freedom without forgiveness.

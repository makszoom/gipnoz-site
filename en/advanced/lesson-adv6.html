Adv6 — Past Life Regression

~55 minutes

About This Lesson

Past life regression is a powerful advanced technique where subjects access what they experience as memories from previous lifetimes. Whether these are literal past lives or metaphors created by the subconscious is irrelevant — the therapeutic benefit is real. The subconscious creates whatever framework is needed for healing, and the hypnotist's job is to work within that framework.

This lesson covers the four types of regression, the full regression protocol, how to work with past life memories, and ethical guidelines for practicing past life regression responsibly.

The Four Types of Regression

1. Directed regression. The hypnotist guides the subject to a specific time or place — childhood, a particular event, or a past life that relates to a presenting issue.

2. Spontaneous regression. The subject goes into a past life without prompting. This often happens during general regression work when the current-life source is not sufficient for healing.

3. Recreational regression. The subject goes to a past life out of curiosity rather than therapeutic need. This is the hardest type to manage because there is no therapeutic anchor — no symptom or issue to resolve.

4. Symptom regression. The subject goes to the origin of a physical symptom that has no organic cause. If the symptom did not originate in this lifetime, the regression may naturally lead to a past life.

The Regression Protocol

Step 1: Achieve deep somnambulism. Past life work requires a deep, stable trance.

Step 2: Use the Regression Opening script below to guide the subject back to the source.

Step 3: When they arrive, ask open questions — do not lead. Let the subject describe their experience in their own words.

Step 4: Guide through the experience without leading. Ask: "What happens next?" "What do you feel?"

Step 5: Identify the moment the pattern was created — the specific event or decision that generated the symptom or issue.

Step 6: Process and release. Guide through the death experience if applicable, the between-life state, and integrate the healing into their current life.

"Now I want you to go back. Go back to the source of this [symptom/feeling]. Let your subconscious mind guide you. If it requires another time in this life, you will go there. If it requires another place, you will go there. If it requires another life entirely, you will go there. Follow the thread. Follow it all the way back to the beginning. When you arrive, take a look around. Describe what you see. What year is it? Where are you? What are you wearing? Who is with you? What is happening? Take your time. There is no rush. Just describe everything you are experiencing."

Past Life Processing Script

Once the subject is in the past life experience, guide them through the processing and release:

"You are experiencing that moment again. You are an observer now — safe, detached, present. Observe what is happening. What happened in this moment? What did you feel? What did you learn from this experience? This moment shaped you. It created a pattern that has followed you. But now you understand it. Now you see it clearly. And now — let it go. Take a deep breath. That life is over. It has been over. The people in that life, the places, the pain — they are all in the past. You are here, now, in this life, in this body, in this room. You are safe. You are free. The pattern is broken. The healing is complete."

Symptom-to-Source Bridge Script

When working with a physical symptom that may trace back to a past life origin, use this bridging script:

"Focus your attention on the [physical symptom]. Feel it in your body. Notice its location, its shape, its temperature. Now ask your subconscious: when did this begin? Let the feeling take you back. Follow it like a thread through time. Go back to the first time you ever felt this sensation. Let yourself drift back, further and further. If that takes you to another time in this life, that is fine. If that takes you to another life entirely, that is also fine. Go to the moment it started. The exact moment. When you arrive, describe what you see. What is happening? How old are you? Where are you? What is the very first thing that created this feeling in your body?"

Working with Past Life Memories

Do not challenge whether the memory is "real." Accept the subject's experience as presented. Guide them through the narrative: birth, key events, the moment the pattern was created, death, the between-life state, and integration into the current life. At each stage, reinforce that the past life is over, that they are safe in the present, and that the healing is real.

Ethical Guidelines

1. Do not make metaphysical claims. State clearly: "I don't know if this is a real past life or a metaphor your mind created. What matters is that the healing is real."

2. Do not implant suggestions about past lives. Let the content emerge from the subject's subconscious spontaneously.

3. If a subject regresses spontaneously, handle it professionally — do not act surprised. Maintain the therapeutic frame and guide the experience calmly.

🧠 Key Takeaway: Past life regression works regardless of your beliefs about reincarnation. The healing is in the processing, not the metaphysics. Whether the content is literal or symbolic, the subconscious uses it to release patterns that no longer serve the client.

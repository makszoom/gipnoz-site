About This Lesson

Past life regression is one of the most fascinating — and controversial — applications of hypnosis. Kein doesn't take a position on whether past lives are literally real. What matters is that when a client regresses to what they experience as a past life, and the symptom resolves, the therapy is effective regardless of the metaphysical explanation.

Kein demonstrates through a remarkable case: a man who couldn't move his neck more than a few degrees in either direction since birth. Conventional medicine found nothing physically wrong. Psychiatrists couldn't help. In a past life regression, the man experienced being an Irish horse thief who was hanged, resulting in a broken neck — and the soul carried that trauma into the next life. When the memory was processed, the neck stiffness resolved.

The Stiff Neck Case

A woman called Kein about her father, who had seen every specialist imaginable for his rigid neck. From birth, he could barely turn his head. There was no organic cause — the muscles worked fine, but something in his mind wouldn't let them. Kein agreed to see him.

During the regression, the father spontaneously entered a past life as an Irish horse thief. He described the town preparing to hang him — and insisted he was a "good boy" who just visited the ladies while their husbands worked. The hanging went wrong; the rope was rotten, and he died by strangulation rather than a clean break. The trauma of that death carried into his current life as neck rigidity.

Processing Karmic Symptoms

Kein guided him through the death experience, then through the period between lives, then into the current life as a healthy infant. At each stage, he reinforced that the past life was over, that the soul had chosen this new life, and that the body could now release the old pattern. The neck stiffness resolved over the following days.

Working with Past Life Memories

Kein's approach: if a client regresses to a past life during a session, treat it as real for therapeutic purposes. Guide them through the experience, help them identify the source of the current symptom, resolve the trauma in the past life context, and integrate the healing into the present. Whether it's literal or metaphorical doesn't matter — the symptom relief is real.

🧠 Key Takeaway: Past life regression is a powerful therapeutic tool regardless of your beliefs about reincarnation. The subconscious creates whatever framework is needed for healing. If a past life emerges, work with it. The proof is in the result, not the explanation.

📝 Practice: Past Life Protocol

Study the past life regression structure: deep somnambulism → regression to source of symptom → past life experience → death transition → between lives → new birth → integration.

Past Life Regression Opening

"You are going back... back to the source of this physical symptom. If it requires experiencing another time, another place, another life — you will go there. There is nothing to fear. You are simply an observer. When you arrive at the moment that created this pattern, you will know. Describe what you see. What year is it? Where are you? What is happening to your body?"

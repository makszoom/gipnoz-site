Recommended hypnosis books, Orman McGill, Elman's Hypnotherapy. How hypnosis evolved from 1950s to today.

Love as the greatest bypass, TV advertising, power of the mind, the pretending game, eye fixation.

Referral letters to doctors, patient follow-up, intake forms, handling the 'I tried hypnosis before' objection.

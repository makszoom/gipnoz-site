About This Lesson

This lesson contains Kein's most famous live demonstration — the "pretending game" that results in instant anesthesia. But before he gets to that, he lays out the single most important concept in all of hypnosis: the critical factor.

The critical factor is your mind's filter — it evaluates incoming suggestions and rejects anything it doesn't accept. Every hypnotist's job is to bypass this filter. Once bypassed, the subconscious accepts suggestions directly. Kein shows that the best bypass isn't a technique — it's an emotion.

The Used Car Dealer Who Made Millions

Kein was once hired as a consultant to evaluate a TV commercial for a used car dealership. The target audience: blue-collar men aged 18–22 watching football. The ad featured a salesman in a Wild West outfit shouting about hot dogs, burgers, and financing. But the real bypass was standing next to him — a seven-foot-tall former NBA player named Platt Person, wearing a bright yellow chicken costume, silently bobbing in the background.

The audience was so distracted by the absurd chicken that their critical factor never processed the sales pitch. The dealership's business grew 120% the following month. Other dealers tried copying the idea — dancing bears, pink elephants — but none replicated the success. The original had something the copies didn't: genuine bypass of the critical factor.

Love — The Ultimate Bypass

What's the single greatest bypass of the critical factor? Love. When you're truly in love, your critical factor is "somewhere past Pluto." You hang on every word the other person says. Every advertisement that uses sex is trying to borrow a tiny fraction of that emotional bypass. Love is the master key.

The Pretending Game — Live Demonstration

Kein calls a volunteer named Karell to the front. He asks her to do something she was great at as a child: pretend. He tells her he'll ask her to close her eyes and pretend they're glued shut — as if someone poured superglue on her eyelids. Pretend with the full force of that little girl's imagination. Then he works on her arm while she's "pretending" — and drives a needle through her skin without her feeling a thing.

The audience gasps. Karell is shocked. She felt nothing — not even pressure. Kein explains exactly what happened, step by step.

The Anatomy of the Technique

Step 1 — Bypass the critical factor. The instruction to "pretend your eyes are glued shut" is a command the critical factor can't resist testing. When Karell tried to open her eyes and couldn't (because she was pretending so hard), her critical factor stepped aside: "I can't open them — let's see what happens next."

Step 2 — Acceptable selective thinking. Kein told her: "Nothing I do here will bother or disturb you in any way." The word nothing is emphasized — it's a hypnotic command delivered under the radar. The critical factor is already out of the way, so the suggestion goes straight to the subconscious.

Step 3 — Physical demonstration. The result speaks for itself. Complete anesthesia in less than two minutes, no formal induction, no relaxation.

Emergency Hypnosis

Kein used this exact technique as a volunteer in the ER. In two years, they had three cases of complete arm amputation where the patient felt nothing — using only this method, no drugs. The key is bypassing the critical factor before the patient has time to panic. In a crisis, the critical factor is already weakened by shock — making it the perfect moment for an instant bypass.

🧠 Key Takeaway: The critical factor is the gatekeeper. Don't fight it — bypass it. The pretending game is one of the fastest ways to do this. Once the critical factor steps aside, the subconscious accepts suggestions directly — even suggestions for complete anesthesia.

📝 Practice: The Pretending Game

Try this with a willing partner. No needles — just test eye catalepsy.

Eye Catalepsy via Pretending

"Close your eyes. Now — remember how you used to pretend as a child? How real it felt? I want you to pretend, with that same force, that your eyes are glued shut. Like there's superglue on your eyelids. Pretend so hard that when you try to open them, they simply won't move. That's it — keep pretending. Your eyes are fixed. Now notice — your critical mind has stepped aside to watch what happens. That's all I needed."

If their eyes stayed closed when they tried to open them, you've successfully bypassed the critical factor. The hypnosis has already begun.

About This Lesson

This lesson is built around one of the most famous stories in hypnosis — the tale of Walter Sicort, an elderly hypnotist from New Jersey who accidentally discovered a trance level so deep that he couldn't bring his subject back. But Kein doesn't tell it for entertainment. The story illustrates a real phenomenon: there are levels below the coma state, and they behave differently than anything taught in standard hypnosis training.

Kein also shares a remarkable follow-up — an experiment at a Las Vegas conference where this deep state was used for apparent healing, suggesting it may have therapeutic properties that conventional hypnosis doesn't reach.

The Levels of Trance

Kein briefly outlines the standard trance levels: light (ideomotor responses), medium (catalepsy, partial amnesia), and deep somnambulism (full amnesia, anesthesia, hallucinations). Below somnambulism lies the coma state — and below that, according to Walter Sicort's work, there's a level so deep that conventional emergence techniques don't work.

The Walter Sicort Story

Walter Sicort spent years perfecting a single progressive relaxation induction, adjusting one word at a time, until he could predictably drop people into extremely deep trance. One day, a 13-year-old girl came in for help with exam anxiety. Walter used his new, tweaked induction — and the girl dropped into a state he'd never seen before. Her breathing slowed to once per minute. Her skin turned gray. She looked, in Kein's words, "like a corpse."

Walter couldn't bring her out. Standard countdowns, emergency protocols, even threats — nothing worked. He splashed water on her face. Nothing. He lifted her by the armpits and shook her. Nothing. He blew on her eyeballs (which had rolled back into her skull). Nothing. In a panic, he reached for the phone to call an ambulance — and then noticed her skin color returning. She came back on her own, 22 minutes later. When asked why she hadn't opened her eyes when he counted to five, she said, "I did open them!" She had experienced massive time distortion — what felt like seconds to her was 22 minutes in real time. She also had no memory of the incident.

The Healing State

At a hypnosis conference in Las Vegas, someone proposed an experiment: put Walter's assistant Sarah into this deep state, then physically connect her hand to a woman with severe hearing loss (who wore powerful hearing aids). If this state had healing properties, perhaps the connection would transfer something. Sarah went under. Their hands were bound together. The room went quiet. Suddenly, the woman with hearing loss turned beet red and began sweating profusely — describing an intense "hot needle" sensation. What exactly happened? No one knows. But the phenomenon was real and observable. Kein shares this story not as proof, but as a glimpse of how much we still don't know about the deeper levels of hypnosis.

What This Means for You

You are unlikely to accidentally drop someone into this state. It requires a very specific, years-refined induction. But the lesson is important: if someone doesn't come out of trance when you count, don't panic. They will come out on their own. Wait. The body knows how to return. Panicking and shaking them only makes things worse — for both of you.

🧠 Key Takeaway: Deep trance states exist beyond what most textbooks describe. If a subject doesn't respond to emergence cues, stay calm — they will return naturally. Never panic. And never leave a suggestion you haven't explicitly cancelled.

📝 Practice: Emergence Protocol

Memorize this simple emergence sequence. Use it every time you bring someone out of trance:

Safe Emergence

"In a moment I'm going to count from one to five. On the count of five, you will open your eyes, fully alert, feeling refreshed and relaxed. One — beginning to come back. Two — more aware of the room around you. Three — taking a deep breath, feeling energy return to your body. Four — your eyes are starting to feel ready to open. Five — eyes open, wide awake, feeling wonderful."

If they don't respond to five, simply repeat the count with more emphasis. They will come out. If not — wait 30 seconds and try again. Do not panic.

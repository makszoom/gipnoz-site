About This Lesson

Deepening is the process of moving from light trance to the somnambulistic state required for therapeutic work. Multiple methods exist because no single technique works for every subject. Build a toolkit of approaches and develop the judgment to choose the right one.

Counting Techniques — Adjusted to Breathing

The most reliable deepening method is counting synchronized with the subject's breathing. Count on the peak of their inhale, so the number lands during the exhale — when they naturally relax. Adapt speed to their breathing rhythm: fast breathers get faster counting, slow breathers get slower. If the counting and breathing are out of sync, the deepening won't take hold.

"Breathe in deeply. As you inhale, I will say a number. As you exhale, let go of all tension. Ten... [on inhale peak] ...and more relaxed [on exhale]. Nine... deeper. Eight... sinking deeper. Seven... every muscle letting go. Six... further down. Five... more peaceful. Four... completely at ease. Three... deeper still. Two... almost there. One... at the deepest level. Zero... total relaxation."

"With every breath you take, you go deeper. I will count each exhale. Exhale one — let go of your shoulders. Exhale two — let go of your chest. Exhale three — let go of your stomach. Exhale four — let go of your legs. Exhale five — let go of everything. You are completely relaxed."

Hallucination Techniques

Guide the subject to visualize a place. Three standard scripts are provided below. The key is to establish that the location is safe before the session begins — never place a subject in a situation that triggers anxiety. These are guided visualizations that bypass the critical factor through sensory immersion.

"Imagine you're at the top of a beautiful staircase with ten steps going down. Each step takes you deeper into relaxation. Ten — step down and feel your body letting go. Nine — further down, your mind is becoming quiet. Eight — each step blocks out more of the outside world. Seven — your breathing is slowing. Six — deeper and deeper. Five — you feel peaceful. Four — completely safe. Three — almost at the bottom. Two — one more step. One — at the deepest, most peaceful place. Zero — total release."

"You're walking on a warm, beautiful beach. Feel the sand beneath your feet. The waves come in with each inhale and go out with each exhale. Breathe in... the wave rolls in. Breathe out... the wave rolls back, carrying all your tension with it. The sun is warm on your skin. The sound of the ocean is peaceful. With every wave, you sink deeper and deeper into relaxation."

"You're floating on a soft, white cloud. The cloud is thick and comfortable, supporting your entire body. With each breath, the cloud gets thicker, softer, and you sink deeper into it. You feel weightless, peaceful, completely safe. Nothing can disturb you here. The cloud carries you higher... deeper... more relaxed than you've ever been."

Post-Induction Deepening

After the subject is already in trance, use this direct deepening technique to double their level of relaxation with each count.

"I'm going to count to three. On three, you'll double your relaxation. One... deeper. Two... even deeper. Three — double it. Good. Now I'll do it again — on three, double it again. One... deeper still. Two... sinking further. Three — double it completely. Every time I count to three, your relaxation doubles, and you go deeper than ever before."

The Pyramid Technique (Stacking)

After one induction, move to a second deeper level, then a third. Each layer gives the subject permission to go further. For example: start with eye catalepsy, add progressive relaxation, then add a countdown. Each layer drives the subject deeper.

"You're already deeply relaxed. Now I'm going to help you go deeper still. When I count to three, every muscle will relax twice as much. One — feel the relaxation spreading from your head down to your toes. Two — every muscle, every nerve, every fiber is letting go. Three — twice as relaxed as before. Good. Now we go deeper. Again — one, two, three — even deeper. And again — one, two, three — the deepest level yet."

Stack multiple inductions: start with eye catalepsy, add progressive relaxation, then add a countdown. Each layer drives the subject deeper.

🧠 Key Takeaway: Deepening is not a single trick — it's a skill of reading your subject and choosing the right method. When in doubt, default to counting synchronized with breathing.

📝 Practice: Deepening Techniques

Practice the following deepening variants. Have a partner go into a light trance, then deepen them using each technique.

Variant 1: Counting Deepening

Count from 10 down to 1, synchronized with your partner's breathing. Each number takes them deeper.

"I'll count from 10 to 1. Each number takes you deeper. Breathe in as I say the number, breathe out as you relax into it. Ten — breathe in... now exhale and let go. Nine — breathe in... deeper. Eight — breathe in... every muscle releasing. Seven — further down. Six — deeper still. Five — completely at ease. Four — sinking deeper. Three — almost there. Two — one more breath. One — at the deepest level. Zero — total and complete relaxation."

Variant 2: Staircase Deepening

Guide your partner down a mental staircase, using each step as a deepening cue.

"Picture a staircase with 10 steps going down. You're at the top. Ten — step down, more relaxed. Nine — further down, your body feels heavier. Eight — each step blocks out more of the outside world. Seven — your mind is becoming quiet. Six — deeper and deeper. Five — you feel peaceful. Four — completely safe. Three — almost at the bottom. Two — one more step. One — at the bottom, completely at peace. Zero — resting in the deepest relaxation."

Variant 3: Float Technique

Use this gentle floating imagery for subjects who respond better to sensation-based deepening.

"Imagine you're floating on a soft, white cloud. The cloud is thick and comfortable, supporting every part of your body. With each exhale, the cloud gets thicker, softer, and you sink deeper into it. You feel completely weightless. You're safe, supported, and every breath takes you further into this state of peace. Nothing can disturb you here. Just floating... drifting... deeper and deeper."

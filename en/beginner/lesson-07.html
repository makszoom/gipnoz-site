About This Lesson

Deepening is the process of moving from light trance to the somnambulistic state required for therapeutic work. Multiple methods exist because no single technique works for every subject. Build a toolkit of approaches and develop the judgment to choose the right one.

Counting Techniques — Adjusted to Breathing

The most reliable deepening method is counting synchronized with the subject's breathing. Count on the peak of their inhale, so the number lands during the exhale — when they naturally relax. Example: "Ten [on inhale peak]... more relaxed [on exhale]... nine [inhale]... deeper [exhale]..." Adapt speed to their breathing rhythm: fast breathers get faster counting, slow breathers get slower. If the counting and breathing are out of sync, the deepening won't take hold.

Hallucination Techniques

Guide the subject to visualize a place. Three standard scripts:

Staircase: "Imagine you're at the top of a staircase. Each step down takes you deeper. 10-9-8..."

Beach: "You're walking on a warm beach. The waves come in with each inhale and go out with each exhale."

Clouds: "You're floating on a soft cloud. With each breath, the cloud gets thicker, softer, takes you higher... deeper... more relaxed."

The key is to establish that the location is safe before the session begins — never place a subject in a situation that triggers anxiety. These are guided visualizations that bypass the critical factor through sensory immersion.

Post-Induction Deepening

After the subject is already in trance: "I'm going to count to three. On three, you'll double your relaxation. One... deeper. Two... even deeper. Three — double it. Good. Now I'll do it again — on three, double it again."

The Pyramid Technique (Stacking)

After one induction, move to a second deeper level, then a third. Each layer gives the subject permission to go further: "You're already deeply relaxed. Now I'm going to help you go deeper still. When I count to three, every muscle will relax twice as much." For example: start with eye catalepsy, add progressive relaxation, then add a countdown. Each layer drives the subject deeper.

🧠 Key Takeaway: Deepening is not a single trick — it's a skill of reading your subject and choosing the right method. When in doubt, default to counting synchronized with breathing.

📝 Practice: Deepening Techniques

Practice the following deepening variants. Have a partner go into a light trance, then deepen them using each technique.

Variant 1: Counting Deepening

"I'll count from 10 to 1. Each number takes you deeper. Breathe in as I say the number, breathe out as you relax into it. 10 — breathe in... now exhale and let go. 9 — breathe in... deeper..."

Variant 2: Staircase Deepening

"Picture a staircase with 10 steps going down. You're at the top. 10 — step down, more relaxed. 9 — further down, your body feels heavier. 8 — each step blocks out more of the outside world. 7-6-5 — deeper and deeper. 4-3-2-1 — at the bottom, completely at peace."

Variant 3: Float Technique

"Imagine you're floating on a cloud. With each exhale, the cloud gets thicker, softer, and you sink deeper into it. You're safe, supported, and every breath takes you further into this state."

About This Lesson

The Elman induction is the core technique of this entire course. Kein learned it directly from Dave Elman's tradition and has refined it over decades of teaching. In this lesson, he demonstrates the complete induction live with a student named Ben — showing exactly what to say, how to test for results, and what to do when the subject doesn't respond as expected.

The Elman induction is famous for producing deep somnambulism in 4-5 minutes when done correctly. It follows a precise sequence: physical relaxation (eye catalepsy > hand drop), mental relaxation (reverse counting), and deepening (fractionation).

Step 1: Eye Catalepsy

The induction begins with eye catalepsy. Kein tells Ben to close his eyes, relax his eyelids until they "stop working," and then test them. When Ben tries to open his eyes and they don't respond, the first test is passed. This is the foundation — if the eyes don't lock, the induction doesn't proceed.

Step 2: Hand Drop Test

Kein then lifts Ben's hand by the wrist and instructs him not to help. The hand should feel so relaxed that when released, it drops like a "wet rag." Ben initially helps — his muscles engage. Kein corrects him patiently: "Don't help me lift. Let me do the lifting." After several attempts, Ben finally releases control. This is a critical teaching moment — many beginners fail at this step because the subject is still trying to cooperate consciously.

Step 3: Reverse Counting (Mind Relaxation)

With the body relaxed, Kein moves to the mind. He asks Ben to count backward from 100, saying "more relaxed" after each number. "100 — more relaxed. 99 — more relaxed..." The instruction includes the idea that as an engineer, numbers control Ben — and this is his chance to let go of that control. Within a few numbers, Ben's counting slows, then stops. His mind has relaxed. The induction is complete.

The Teaching Moment

Kein's handling of Ben's resistance at the hand drop stage is the real lesson. Most instructors would move on. Kein doesn't — he repeats the test until Ben gets it right. "You're still helping. Let me do the lifting. There — now you're getting it!" The audience sees that success comes from persistence, not from a perfect first attempt.

🧠 Key Takeaway: The Elman induction is a sequence, not a script. The sequence is: relax the body (eye catalepsy), test the body (hand drop), relax the mind (reverse counting). Do not skip tests. Do not proceed past a failed test. Repeat until the subject responds correctly. That's the entire secret.

📝 Practice: Elman Induction

Memorize the three-step sequence: eye catalepsy → hand drop → mental relaxation. Practice each step separately before combining them.

Elman Induction — Key Phrases

"Close your eyes. Focus on your eyelids. Relax them until they stop working. Test them — try to open them. They don't work. Good. Now let that relaxation spread through your whole body... I'm going to lift your hand. Don't help me. Let it be completely relaxed... When I drop it, let it fall like a wet rag and go twice as deep... Now I want you to count backward from 100. With each number, your mind becomes twice as relaxed. 100 — more relaxed. 99 — more relaxed..."

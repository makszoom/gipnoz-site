About This Lesson

Knowing how to hypnotize someone is only half the battle — you also need clients. Kein devotes this lesson to the business side of hypnosis, sharing the exact systems he used to build a thriving practice. Unlike generic marketing advice, these methods are specific to the hypnosis profession: how to get doctors to refer patients to you, how to write follow-up letters, and how to handle the intake process.

The core strategy: make yourself indispensable to medical professionals by solving problems they can't solve, then document your success so they keep sending you patients.

Doctor Referral Letters

Kein provides exact templates for referral correspondence. When a doctor sends a patient, you send a thank-you letter confirming the appointment. After the session, you send a progress report: "I'm happy to inform you that Jane Doe has successfully quit smoking and reports being smoke-free for 14 days. She is delighted with the results." This makes the doctor look good — and ensures they'll keep referring.

The key insight: doctors are busy and competitive. They want their patients to stay loyal. When you frame hypnosis as a service that enhances patient loyalty, doctors become eager to refer.

Handling 'I Tried Hypnosis Before'

Many potential clients say, "I tried hypnosis before and it didn't work." Kein's intake form is designed to catch this early. He changed the question from "Have you been hypnotized before?" to "Has anyone tried to hypnotize you before?" — revealing failed attempts that the client didn't even recognize as hypnosis. The fix is simple: explain what went wrong (usually poor induction, insufficient depth) and promise a different approach.

Intake Forms

A well-designed intake form does half the therapeutic work. It should ask about the problem, previous attempts, medical conditions, medications, and expectations. It signals professionalism and gives you the information you need before the session begins.

🧠 Key Takeaway: Your success as a hypnotist depends as much on marketing as on technique. Build relationships with doctors, use professional intake forms, and always follow up. A single successful referral can bring dozens more.

📝 Practice: Write a Referral Letter

Draft a template referral letter to a doctor. Include: thank-you for the referral, expected appointment date, and a promise to follow up with results.

Referral Letter Template

"Dear Dr. [Name], thank you for referring [Patient] to our practice. I've scheduled an appointment for [date]. I will keep you informed of progress and will follow up after the session is complete. I've enclosed several brochures for your reference. Thank you for your trust."

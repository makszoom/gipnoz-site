B09 — Building a Practice: Referrals & Marketing

~40 minutes

🎬 Video will be added after upload to YouTube

About This Lesson

A successful hypnosis practice requires both skill and clients. This lesson covers the specific marketing systems that work for hypnosis professionals: medical referrals, patient correspondence, intake procedures, and handling common objections.

Doctor Referral Letters

Identify physicians in your area. Write a professional introduction letter describing your services. After each successful case, send a follow-up letter thanking the doctor for their referral and reporting the patient's progress. Doctors refer when they trust you with their patients.

The key insight: doctors are busy and competitive. They want their patients to stay loyal. When you frame hypnosis as a service that enhances patient loyalty and solves problems they cannot address, doctors become eager to refer.

"Dear Dr. [Name], I treated your patient [Name] for [condition]. They responded well and report [result]. Thank you for your referral."

Handling 'I Tried Hypnosis Before'

Most subjects who say this tried a poor practitioner. Ask about their experience — you'll often find they used only progressive relaxation without achieving trance. Your pre-talk should directly address this concern.

"Who was it? What approach did they use?"

"You may have tried hypnosis before and felt it didn't work. That's usually because the hypnotist didn't achieve the right depth. We'll do it differently today."

Intake Forms & Contraindications

Before any session, establish: (1) Medical history — heart conditions, diabetes, epilepsy, medications. (2) Previous hypnosis experience. (3) Current medications (some suppress trance). (4) Reason for visit — what specific outcome do they want?

For heart patients: require a medical referral. For diabetics: monitor blood sugar (hypnosis lowers it). For epileptics: avoid flashing lights; standard inductions are safe. A well-designed intake form signals professionalism and gives you the information you need before the session begins.

Client Follow-Up System

Keep records: date, induction used, trance depth (1-5 scale), scripts used, results. Schedule follow-up calls. Build a referral loop: satisfied clients refer friends and family.

🧠 Key Takeaway: Medical referrals are the most reliable source of clients. Build trust with doctors, document results, and communicate professionally. Your reputation is your marketing.

📝 Practice: Referral & Intake Tools

Practice the following correspondence and client handling scripts.

Variant 1: Referral Letter Template

"Dear Dr. [Name], thank you for referring [Patient] for [condition]. They responded well to hypnosis and report [specific improvement]. I've enclosed brochures for your reference. I welcome your continued referrals."

Variant 2: Handling 'It Didn't Work' Objection

"You tried hypnosis before and it didn't take? Tell me about it. [Listen.] What you described isn't real hypnosis — that's just relaxation. Real hypnosis — what we call somnambulism — is a different state entirely. You'll know the difference today."

Variant 3: Intake Question Sequence

(1) "What brings you here today?" (2) "Have you tried hypnosis before?" (3) "Are you under medical care for anything?" (4) "What medications do you take?" (5) "What outcome are you looking for?"

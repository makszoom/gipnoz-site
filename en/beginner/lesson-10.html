About This Lesson

Self-hypnosis allows clients to reinforce suggestions between sessions and gives practitioners a tool for their own mental programming. Two primary methods are covered here: the finger-drop technique and the audio recording method.

The critical rule of self-hypnosis: do not mentally repeat the suggestion while in trance. Mental repetition re-engages the conscious mind and the critical factor. Instead, read the suggestion, enter trance, and let the subconscious absorb it passively.

The Finger Drop Method

Write a suggestion on a card. Read it five times slowly. Raise your right index finger. Drop it and immediately close your eyes — you are now in deep hypnosis. Do NOT repeat the suggestion mentally — that re-engages the conscious mind and critical factor. Stay in trance for approximately 1 minute. Count 1-2-3 and open your eyes.

The suggestion card should be concise — one sentence is ideal. Read it five times slowly, concentrating on each word. After the fifth reading, drop your finger, close your eyes, and enter trance. Do not repeat the suggestion in your mind. Simply relax and let the subconscious absorb it.

Creating Audio Recordings

Record 20-30 seconds of silence at the start (giving time for the subject to enter trance). Then read a deepening script. Leave 1 minute of silence for suggestion absorption. Then count out. Important: the audio should NOT contain the suggestion itself — only the deepening. The suggestion is read BEFORE pressing play.

The One-Minute Rule

Suggestions in self-hypnosis should be 1 minute or less. Longer is not more effective — it engages the analytical mind. One concise, well-formed suggestion absorbed in deep trance is more powerful than 10 minutes of rambling.

Common Mistakes

(1) Repeating the suggestion during trance — don't. (2) Trying too hard to relax — relaxation comes from letting go, not forcing. (3) Setting time limits consciously — instead, trust the recording or your internal clock.

🧠 Key Takeaway: Self-hypnosis is simple but precise: read your suggestion five times, drop your finger, enter trance for one minute, and come out. The key is to NOT consciously repeat the suggestion during trance. Let the subconscious do its work.

📝 Practice: Self-Hypnosis

Write a single-sentence suggestion on a card. Something simple: "I am calm and focused." Practice the full finger-drop sequence five times today.

Self-Hypnosis Script

"Write your suggestion on a card. Sit comfortably. Read it five times slowly. Raise your right index finger. Drop it — and close your eyes. You are now in deep hypnosis. Do not repeat the suggestion. Simply relax. Let your subconscious receive it. After one minute, count from 1 to 3 and open your eyes, fully refreshed."

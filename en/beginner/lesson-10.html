About This Lesson

Self-hypnosis is an essential skill for both practitioner and client. Kein teaches his preferred method — the "finger drop" technique — along with a system for creating self-hypnosis audio recordings that clients can use at home. The lesson also covers how to structure suggestions for maximum effectiveness.

The critical rule of self-hypnosis: do not mentally repeat the suggestion while in trance. Mental repetition re-engages the conscious mind and the critical factor. Instead, read the suggestion, enter trance, and let the subconscious absorb it passively.

The Finger Drop Method

Kein's self-hypnosis protocol: sit comfortably, set a time limit (1 minute is optimal for suggestions), read your suggestion five times from a card held in your left hand (since the right hand is programmed for the finger drop), then raise and drop your right index finger while closing your eyes. The finger drop acts as a trigger to enter deep trance instantly.

The suggestion card should be concise — one sentence is ideal. Read it five times slowly, concentrating on each word. After the fifth reading, drop your finger, close your eyes, and enter trance. Do not repeat the suggestion in your mind. Simply relax and let the subconscious absorb it.

Creating Audio Recordings

Kein recommends making 30-minute cassette tapes (or digital recordings) for self-hypnosis. The recording structure: 20-30 seconds of silence (to allow the listener to enter trance via the finger drop), then a deepening script, then the therapeutic suggestions, then an emergence count. The listener presses play, does their finger drop during the silence, and lets the recording guide them deeper.

The One-Minute Rule

Suggestions given in self-hypnosis are most effective with a 1-minute trance duration. Extended time in trance provides additional relaxation benefits but doesn't improve suggestion absorption. This makes self-hypnosis practical even for busy schedules — you can complete a session in under two minutes.

🧠 Key Takeaway: Self-hypnosis is simple but precise: read your suggestion five times, drop your finger, enter trance for one minute, and come out. The key is to NOT consciously repeat the suggestion during trance. Let the subconscious do its work.

📝 Practice: Self-Hypnosis

Write a single-sentence suggestion on a card. Something simple: "I am calm and focused." Practice the full finger-drop sequence five times today.

Self-Hypnosis Script

"Write your suggestion on a card. Sit comfortably. Read it five times slowly. Raise your right index finger. Drop it — and close your eyes. You are now in deep hypnosis. Do not repeat the suggestion. Simply relax. Let your subconscious receive it. After one minute, count from 1 to 3 and open your eyes, fully refreshed."

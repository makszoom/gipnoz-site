About This Lesson

The coma state is the deepest level of hypnosis that Kein teaches in the beginner-intermediate course. It's below somnambulism and characterized by complete physical catalepsy, spontaneous anesthesia, and time distortion. Kein demonstrates how to recognize, test, and work with this state.

Three specific tests confirm coma: natural anesthesia (test with a pinch), physical catalepsy (the body holds any position without effort), and the "fright command" (telling the subject to open their eyes triggers a visible eye-movement response even if the eyes don't open). Understanding this state is essential for anyone doing deep therapeutic work.

Achieving Coma

Kein teaches the specific induction for reaching coma: extend the Elman induction with additional fractionation and deepening. The subject passes through somnambulism and into a state where their body becomes completely unresponsive while their subconscious remains accessible. Breathing slows dramatically. The subject may look "dead to the world."

Three Tests for Coma

Test 1 — Natural Anesthesia: a firm pinch produces no reaction. The body's pain response is completely absent. Test 2 — Physical Catalepsy: lift the subject's arm and leg into an awkward position. They will hold it indefinitely without muscular effort. Test 3 — The Fright Command: lean close to the subject's face and firmly say "Open your eyes!" If any eye movement occurs (even if the eyes don't open), the subject is in coma. A true coma subject won't respond at all to this test.

Working in Coma

Therapeutic work done in the coma state is extremely powerful. Suggestions given at this level bypass all resistance. However, emergence must be handled carefully — don't bring the subject straight out of coma to full waking awareness. Bring them up to somnambulism first, then to full emergence. This prevents disorientation.

🧠 Key Takeaway: The coma state is the deepest level of hypnosis accessible in this course. It provides complete anesthesia and catalepsis naturally. Use the three tests to verify it, ensure careful emergence (through somnambulism), and never leave a suggestion uncancelled at this depth.

📝 Practice: Coma Tests

Practice the three coma tests only after establishing reliable somnambulism with a partner. Always emerge through somnambulism, never directly from coma.

Coma State Induction

"You're going deeper now. Deeper than you've ever been. Your body is completely relaxed — so relaxed that it no longer needs to respond. Your arms, your legs — they'll stay exactly where I place them. Your body is resting so deeply that it has shut down all unnecessary functions. You are in a state of complete healing rest. Nothing will disturb you. Only my voice reaches you."

B12 — Working with Children & The Coma State

~40 minutes

About This Lesson

Two distinct topics are covered in this lesson. First: working with children in hypnosis — which is simpler than working with adults because children have less critical factor and more imagination. Second: the coma state — the deepest trance level, characterized by spontaneous anesthesia, complete catalepsy, and time distortion.

The coma state is the deepest level of hypnosis covered in this course. It is below somnambulism and characterized by complete physical catalepsy, spontaneous anesthesia, and time distortion. Three specific tests confirm coma. Understanding this state is essential for anyone doing deep therapeutic work.

Working with Children: Key Principles

(1) Use imagination, not relaxation: "Let's play a pretending game." (2) Keep inductions short — children have short attention spans. (3) Use the "Pretending Game": close their eyes and pretend they're glued shut. (4) Children respond to authority and playfulness. They don't fear hypnosis, so they go deep fast.

Child Induction Script

"Let's play a game. Close your eyes. Now pretend they're stuck — like someone put glue on them. Try to open them — you can't! Good. Now pretend your arm is made of rubber — it's so heavy and loose. Keep pretending. You're doing great."

Achieving the Coma State

Start from somnambulism. Use the three-level deepening to guide the subject to the deepest possible state. Breathing slows dramatically. The subject may look "dead to the world."

"I want you to double your relaxation — that's Level A. Now double it again — Level B. Now double it once more — Level C, the basement. You're in the deepest possible state."

Three Tests for Coma

Test 1 — Anesthesia: pinch the skin — no reaction. Test 2 — Catalepsy: lift the arm — it stays in position. Test 3 — Fright command: command the subject to open their eyes — eyes show rapid movement but don't open (or show delayed opening). All three must be present to confirm coma.

"Open your eyes now!"

Working in Coma and Emerging

In coma, the subject is in natural euphoria. They do not want to communicate. Keep suggestions short and direct. For emergence: count 1-5 into somnambulism first, then 1-5 to full wakefulness. Never try to bring someone directly from coma to full awareness — this prevents disorientation.

Ethical Note on Children

Always maintain appropriate boundaries. Child abuse must be reported by law. Use the pretending game, not physical contact.

🧠 Key Takeaway: Two distinct skill sets: children respond to imagination and playfulness, not relaxation. The coma state is the deepest level of hypnosis — use the three tests to verify it, ensure careful emergence through somnambulism, and always maintain ethical boundaries with children.

📝 Practice: Coma Tests

Practice the three coma tests only after establishing reliable somnambulism with a partner. Always emerge through somnambulism, never directly from coma.

Coma State Induction

"You're going deeper now. Deeper than you've ever been. Your body is completely relaxed — so relaxed that it no longer needs to respond. Your arms, your legs — they'll stay exactly where I place them. Your body is resting so deeply that it has shut down all unnecessary functions. You are in a state of complete healing rest. Nothing will disturb you. Only my voice reaches you."

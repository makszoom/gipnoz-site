0.2 — How the Conscious and Subconscious Mind Work

Module 0 • ~20 minutes

🎬 Video will be embedded after YouTube upload

Two Spheres of the Mind

There is a simple model that explains everything: imagine two circles, like spheres nested inside each other. These are the conscious and subconscious mind. They work on different principles, with different functions and tasks. Misunderstanding these functions leads to problems people carry for years.

Conscious Mind — The Logical Processor

The upper level is the logical, analytical mind. It takes the information that's present, grinds it, processes it, and arrives at some decision or conclusion. This part retrieves needed information from the subconscious like a short-term cache. And that's where its functions end.

Most people overestimate their logical mind. They think that if they "understand it in their head," they will be able to change. But deep changes don't happen here. They happen inside — in the subconscious.

Subconscious Mind — The Record of Programs

You can think of the subconscious as a record with certain programs burned into it. A needle reads these programs — and thoughts, beliefs, and feelings arise.

This is where all the stored beliefs live — convictions, fixed ideas, thoughts you believe without a shadow of doubt. A classic example is your name. You know your name; it's stored at the subconscious level. It raises no doubt whatsoever.

🧠 Important: Beliefs in the subconscious trigger emotions — fear, anger, guilt. The emotions themselves are the context, the cocoon around the belief. And the physical sensations they produce (tension, tightness, spasm) show up in the body. All psychosomatic issues come from here.

The Critical Factor — The Main Filter

Between the conscious and subconscious mind lies the critical factor (critical factor of the conscious mind). It's a filter, a firewall, a barrier that compares new information with existing data.

If a new belief matches what's already stored in the subconscious, the critical factor lets it through. If it doesn't match — it blocks it. This is especially true for positive changes. Because the subconscious is lazy — it doesn't want to do the work required to create change.

This is exactly why willpower runs out, affirmations don't work, and people fall back into old habits. You can force your way through the critical factor with effort and repetition — but why break down the door when you can simply open it?

Bypassing the Critical Factor — That Is Hypnosis

Everything that happens in hypnosis is the bypassing of the critical factor. It is temporarily dispersed, neutralized, steps aside and ceases its comparison. And nothing more happens. No magic.

Since in hypnosis you simply move the critical factor out of the way — you hear everything, understand everything, and remain fully aware. There is no loss of control. There is no zombie state.

Official Definition of Hypnosis

Hypnosis is the bypass of the critical factor of the conscious mind and the establishment of acceptable selective thinking.

How Problems Arise

The subconscious links a threat to something that was never a threat. A belief forms that triggered an unpleasant emotion and gets locked into long-term memory with the feeling that "this is a threat." Once that connection is made, the subconscious starts protecting you from what it perceives as a threat — through certain behaviors, compensations.

The main function of the subconscious is protection from threat, real or imagined. This is precisely why people can't just "snap out of it" through willpower — the subconscious considers the old pattern safe and the new one unfamiliar, therefore potentially dangerous.

🧠 Main takeaway: No amount of talking, logic, analysis, or digging into the past at the conscious level will change the luggage stored in the subconscious. You need direct access — through hypnosis.

📝 Try It Yourself

Try to recall a situation where you knew what you needed to do, but couldn't make yourself do it. Sound familiar? That's the critical factor and subconscious programs at work.

Now imagine you could simply "slide aside" that internal filter and give the subconscious a new instruction. Without struggle, without forcing yourself.

That's what hypnosis is. And later in this course, you'll learn how to do it — first with others, then with yourself.

0.3 — Who Hypnosis Is For. Suggestibility Test

Module 0 • ~10 minutes

🎬 Video will be embedded after YouTube upload

Can Everyone Learn Hypnosis?

Yes. Hypnosis is not magic and not a gift. It's a technique that can be learned. Like driving a car or playing the guitar. Some need more practice, others less — but anyone can learn.

The Only Thing That Gets in the Way — Fear

If someone asks you what prevents a person from going into hypnosis, the answer is simple: fear. Fear and misconception, misunderstanding. Everything else is technique.

People are afraid of:

  • losing control
  • looking foolish
  • that hypnosis is "brainwashing"
  • that they won't be able to come out of trance
  • that they'll be made to do something against their will

Each of these fears can be dispelled with a simple explanation. And as soon as a person understands that hypnosis is their own state, not someone else's influence — the fear goes away.

Who Can Be a Hypnotist?

🙋 Beginners with No Experience

Never tried hypnosis? Doesn't matter. The course is built to start from zero. The very first lesson will give you hands-on experience.

🧑‍⚕️ Psychologists and Therapists

Hypnosis is a powerful tool for your practice. Rapid inductions save session time. Ready-made protocols for working with confidence, habits, and stress.

🎭 Stage Performers

Kayfabe instant inductions, group work, phenomena. Techniques that look like magic — because it's a real altered state of consciousness.

📚 Self-Learners

Read Erickson, Bandler, Benjamin? Systematize your knowledge. Understand how it all connects into a working system.

🧪 Test: Check Your Suggestibility

The simplest test — the finger lock. Do it right now:

1. Sit comfortably, straighten your back.

2. Raise your hands in front of you, interlock your fingers.

3. Close your eyes. Imagine your fingers are tree roots that have intertwined deep underground. They are fusing together, intertwining tighter and tighter.

4. Now say to yourself mentally: "My fingers are locked so tightly that I cannot separate them, no matter how hard I try."

5. On the count of 3, open your eyes and try to separate your fingers.

🧠 What did you feel? If your fingers didn't come apart on the first try or you felt resistance — you just experienced a hypnotic phenomenon. Your subconscious accepted the suggestion and carried it out. The stronger the resistance, the higher your suggestibility — which means the easier it will be for you both to hypnotize others and to go into trance yourself.

This test is used as a compliance check (readiness to follow instructions) before inducing hypnosis. If your fingers came apart easily — simply repeat, adding more confidence to your voice and more detail to your visualization.

Why This Is Important to Understand

Hypnosis has no "chosen ones." There are no people who "can't be hypnotized." There are people who are afraid, and people who haven't been told that hypnosis is their own state.

If you can remove the fears and misconceptions — anyone can go into hypnosis. If it doesn't happen, it means there's still a fear you haven't uncovered. In the next module, we'll learn how to work with this through pre-talk and the hypnotic contract.

🧠 Remember: The only thing that prevents a person from going into hypnosis is fear. If you remove the fear — the person will go into trance. If they don't — you missed something.

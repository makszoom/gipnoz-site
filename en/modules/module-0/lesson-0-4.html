How This Course Works

This course is built on the Jerry Kain (Kayfabe) method — a system of rapid hypnotic inductions. It is based on the classic Dave Elman induction (deep somnambulism in minutes) and Kayfabe rapid inductions.

What Makes This Course Different

Most hypnosis courses fall into one of two categories:

1. Pseudo-hypnosis — long relaxation exercises, metaphors, visualizations. The person sits with their eyes closed, but there's no real hypnosis. No checks, no phenomena, no somnambulism.

2. Week-long courses — everything at once: from basics to past life regression. Information evaporates faster than it can stick.

This course is different. One dense intensive where you master the foundational principles, then develop them at your own pace. Systematic, sequential, with checks at every stage.

Course Program

Free Module 0 — Introduction — 4 lessons, 50 min

What hypnosis really is, how the mind works, suggestibility test. You are here.

Module 1 — Basic Skills — 6 lessons, ~2 h

Rapport, progressive relaxation, eyelid catalepsy, hand levitation. First complete induction.

Module 2 — Deepening Trance — 5 lessons, ~1.5 h

Somnambulism, 10→1 stabilization, hidden tests, working with resistance, direct suggestions.

⭐ Key Module 3 — Instant Techniques — 6 lessons, ~2 h

Basic instant induction, shock inductions, confusion techniques. 3 seconds to trance.

Module 4 — Phenomena — 6 lessons, ~2 h

Body catalepsy, amnesia, hallucinations, post-hypnotic suggestions.

Module 5 — Therapy — 5 lessons, ~1.5 h

Working with confidence, beliefs, habits. Full session protocol.

Bonus Module 6 — Advanced Topics — 5 lessons, ~1.5 h

Stage hypnosis, handling objections, ethical boundaries.

How to Learn Most Effectively

  • Watch the videos — all techniques demonstrated with explanations
  • Read the transcripts — to reinforce and revisit complex sections
  • Do the practice — hypnosis can't be learned by reading, it must be done
  • Use the scripts — ready-made induction texts for your first sessions
  • Repeat — every induction works better the fifth time than the first

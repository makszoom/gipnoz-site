Module 1 — Dave Elman Induction

6 lessons • ~2 hours • Free

This is the most important module of the course. You will master the Dave Elman induction — the best technique for rapid somnambulism induction.

The Elman induction is not just another technique. It is a system with built-in checks that allows you to guide a person into deep somnambulism in 4 minutes (or less). It works with almost anyone, provided three conditions are met: absence of fear, willingness to go into trance, and trust in the operator.

All other course modules rely on the somnambulic state you learn to induce here. Without this module, regression, therapy, or work with phenomena are impossible.

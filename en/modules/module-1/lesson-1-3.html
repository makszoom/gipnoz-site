1.3 — Fractionation and Arm Drop Test

Module 1 • ~20 minutes

🎬 Video will be inserted after upload to YouTube

What is Fractionation

Fractionation (from Latin fractio — fragmentation) is a technique where you bring a person out of trance and immediately re-induce it. Each subsequent entry is deeper than the previous one.

The basis is the Bernheim principle: the hypnotic state has inertia — after emergence, a residual induced state remains, and re-entry happens faster and deeper. Each "out-in" cycle is a step to a lower level.

Elman reformulated this principle radically: "Why wait a week for natural deepening when you can bring them out and re-induce in 10 seconds?" Fractionation replaces days and weeks of practice with minutes.

🧠 The essence: Every time a person comes out of trance and goes back in, their threshold of suggestibility lowers. Fractionation is the fastest way to deepen trance without additional rituals.

Triple Fractionation: Open — Close — Deeper

The basic fractionation technique looks like this:

  1. "In a few seconds, I'm going to ask you to open and close your eyes" — warning (conscious mind preparation)
  2. "Open your eyes" — short command
  3. "Close your eyes" — immediately, without pause
  4. "Relax twice as deep" — deepening suggestion

This cycle is repeated 3 times. Why 3? Research shows that three cycles are enough to achieve significant deepening. If the subject is still "on the surface" — you can do a 4th cycle. But 3 is the optimal number for most people.

Important element: when the person opens their eyes, hold your hand in front of their face, blocking their field of vision. This way they only see your palm and don't search for the operator with their gaze. This prevents distraction and maintains focus.

"In a few seconds, I'm going to ask you to open and close your eyes…"

"Open your eyes…" [hand in front of face]

"Close your eyes…"

"And relax twice as deep…"

[cycle repeated 3 times]

Speed — The Key to Success

There should be no pauses between commands. As soon as you say "open" — the person opens their eyes. Half a second later — "close." Another half second — "relax twice as deep."

Why? Because a pause gives the conscious mind an opportunity to "switch on" — to start analyzing, evaluating, doubting. When you act quickly, you "outrun" the analytical mind, and the suggestion goes directly to the subconscious.

The warning ("in a few seconds I'm going to ask you…") — is critically important. It prepares the conscious mind: "a command is coming, don't be alarmed." Without warning, the person might be startled by the sudden shift in state.

🧠 Rule: Your rhythm should be: command — reaction — next command. You don't wait for the person to "get there" — you lead them. If you're waiting, you've stopped being the leader.

Arm Drop Test

After three fractionation cycles, an arm drop test is performed — a check of relaxation depth. It is simultaneously a test and a deepening technique.

Execution technique:

  1. Take the person's hand by the wrist
  2. Lift it a few centimeters above their knee or armrest
  3. Suggest: "Your arm is like a heavy sack of sand… like a wet rag… completely heavy…"
  4. Add: "Don't help me at all. Give me all the weight of your arm."
  5. Release the hand

If the hand drops like a sack — relaxation is deep. If the person is helping (holding the hand up, bracing) — say gently but firmly: "No, no, don't help. Give me all the weight of your arm. Let it fall on its own."

Important: the arm drop test is not just a check. It is also a built-in suggestion. You're not just observing whether the arm is relaxed — you're suggesting it is heavy, thereby deepening the relaxation.

Built-in Feedback and Compliance Check

Every time the person correctly follows a command, you say: "Excellent", "Well done". This is not just praise — it's anchoring. You reinforce the pattern: "I do what the operator says → I get approval → I want to do more."

Feedback on the arm drop test is especially important. The person just "failed" with their arm — and they hear "excellent." This removes any anxiety ("did I relax wrong?") and reinforces the correct behavior.

The command "Don't help me at all" is simultaneously a compliance check. If the person doesn't help — the hypnotic contract is working. If they help — you need to rephrase and achieve non-helping before moving on.

🧠 Important: If a person systematically helps on the arm drop test — it's a signal that the hypnotic contract hasn't been established or there's hidden resistance. Go back to the pre-talk.

📝 Practical Assignment

Goal: Perform fractionation on a partner 3 times in a row with an arm drop test.

  1. Conduct preparation (pre-talk) and progressive relaxation (Lesson 1.2).
  2. Perform triple fractionation: open → close → relax twice as deep (×3).
  3. After the 3rd cycle, perform the arm drop test.
  4. Give built-in feedback: "excellent," "well done."
  5. Repeat the entire cycle (fractionation + arm drop) 3 times in a row on one partner.
  6. Record observations: which cycle did the arm fall most freely? Was there resistance? How did depth change?

🧠 Tip: If you notice your partner's eyes are opening slower or with more delay — that's a sign of deepening. Don't rush — give them time. The speed is for your commands, not for their reactions.

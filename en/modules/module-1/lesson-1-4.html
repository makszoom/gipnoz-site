1.4 — Number Amnesia and the Somnambulism Threshold

Module 1 • ~20 minutes

🎬 Video will be inserted after upload to YouTube

From Physical Relaxation to Mental Relaxation

Up to this point, all techniques worked with the body: progressive muscle relaxation, fractionation, arm drop test. Now comes the key moment — the transition to mental relaxation.

Physical relaxation is only the first layer. Even when the body is completely relaxed, the conscious mind may continue to actively work: analyzing, evaluating, counting. As long as the conscious mind is active, the trance remains shallow.

The goal of this lesson is to "turn off" the internal dialogue. And the tool for this is reverse counting with number amnesia.

🧠 Key idea: As long as a person can count — their conscious mind is active. When numbers disappear — the conscious mind "lets go," and access to the subconscious opens. This is the somnambulism threshold.

Reverse Counting from 100: The Technique

The technique is performed after the person has gone through physical relaxation and fractionation. You give the following instruction:

"Now I want you to start counting… slowly and softly… backward from one hundred…"

"100… deeper and deeper… 99… greater relaxation… 98… with each number deeper and deeper… 97…"

Notice the wording: you're not just asking them to say numbers. Between the numbers, you add the words "greater relaxation", "deeper and deeper". This is critically important.

Why "greater relaxation"? If you just say "count down from 100," the conscious mind will mechanically recite numbers and remain active. Adding suggestions between numbers gives two effects:

  • Time for integration — the pause between numbers allows the suggestion to "settle" in the subconscious
  • Pattern interruption — the conscious mind cannot just automatically recite because a suggestion is inserted between numbers

Number Amnesia Suggestion

After 5–10 counting steps, you begin to embed the amnesia suggestion — the disappearance of numbers:

"…92… and notice how the numbers begin to disappear… 91… some numbers just vanish… 90… they dissolve… 89… you can no longer remember what number came before this one…"

"In a few numbers, they will all be gone… vanished… dissolved… and only silence will remain…"

The suggestion is built on the idea that numbers are an analogue of internal dialogue. As long as a person is counting — they are talking to themselves. When numbers disappear — the internal dialogue stops. Emptiness in the mind — that is somnambulism.

⚠️ What NOT to do: Never say "all numbers are gone." Using the word "numbers" in an affirmative form can bring them back. Say: "they are no longer there," "only silence remains," "emptiness."

Hidden Somnambulism Test

When numbers disappear — this is the first hidden test. The person does not know they are being tested. They simply stop saying numbers because the subconscious has accepted the amnesia suggestion.

If numbers have disappeared — the person has crossed the somnambulism threshold. This is deep trance, where the subconscious accepts suggestions without criticism.

After the pause (silence), get confirmation:

"Are they all gone?" — nod or say "yes".

Confirmation can be verbal ("yes") or non-verbal (nod). Either form is acceptable.

Case: silence instead of an answer. If the person is silent after the question "are they all gone?" — this could be either aphonia (inability to speak) or amnesia (don't remember being asked). How to tell the difference? With aphonia, the person tries to answer but can't — the mouth opens but no sound comes out. With amnesia — just silence and even breathing. In both cases, the test is passed.

If Numbers Don't Disappear

Sometimes the person continues counting despite the suggestion. This is normal, especially for beginner hypnotists. What to do:

  1. Repeat the suggestion: strengthen the wording — "numbers are disappearing faster and faster, they simply cease to exist"
  2. Physical intervention: raise your hand, tap their forehead and say "they're all gone!" — a sharp tactile signal shifts attention
  3. If the person keeps counting — they have seized control. In this case, you need the talk: stop, open your eyes, say: "You're counting consciously. Let go of control. Let your subconscious guide you."

Important: if you see the person clinging to the count — don't push. Go back a step, do another fractionation cycle, and try again. Each time, the threshold will lower.

🧠 Rule: If the person seizes control — it's not a failure. It's information. The talk is not a punishment, but a recalibration. Say it calmly, without emotion, and they'll get back on track.

📝 Practical Assignment

Goal: Perform reverse counting with number amnesia on a partner and record the result of the hidden test.

  1. Conduct full preparation: pre-talk, progressive relaxation, fractionation (Lesson 1.3).
  2. Give the instruction for reverse counting from 100, adding "greater relaxation" between numbers.
  3. Embed the amnesia suggestion after 5–10 numbers.
  4. Record: what number did the partner stop at? How long was the pause before disappearance?
  5. Ask: "Are they all gone?" — note the form of reaction (nod, "yes," silence).
  6. If numbers didn't disappear — apply repeated suggestion or physical intervention.
  7. Repeat with 2 different partners. Compare results.

🧠 Tip: Don't aim for numbers to disappear on the first try. For many people, this is an unfamiliar experience. Your job is not to "make numbers disappear," but to create conditions where the subconscious itself chooses to let go of the count. Patience and calmness are your main tools.

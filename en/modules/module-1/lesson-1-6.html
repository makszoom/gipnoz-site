1.6 — Shortened Versions and Deepening

Module 1 • ~20 minutes

🎬 Video will be inserted after upload to YouTube

Why Shortened Versions Are Needed

The full Elman induction (Lessons 1.1–1.5) takes 15–20 minutes. This is an excellent protocol for the first session, but for repeat sessions it's excessive. Once a person has gone through the full induction at least once, their subconscious knows the way to somnambulism.

Shortened versions are the same steps, but without the "warm-up." You skip stages that are no longer needed and jump straight to the key moments. The person enters trance faster, and trance quality doesn't suffer.

In this lesson: two shortened versions + a deepening technique that works with any version.

🧠 Key idea: Shortening is not simplification. It's optimization. You remove what's no longer needed to focus on what truly works. The faster a person enters somnambulism, the higher their trust in you and the process.

Shortened Version 1 — Counting from 100 Omitted

When to use: after the person has gone through the full version at least once. The subconscious already knows what "number disappearance" means and doesn't need a long count.

Structure:

  1. Pre-talk (shortened: "you already know what to do")
  2. Body scan / progressive relaxation (quick, 1–2 minutes)
  3. Fractionation (one cycle instead of three)
  4. Arm drop test with amnesia: "the light will go out and all numbers will disappear"
  5. Forehead tap: "it all vanishes, vanishes, it's all gone"
  6. Tests: eyelid catalepsy + analgesia (if needed)
  7. Emergence

Full script for version 1:

— Pre-talk —

"You've been in hypnosis before, your subconscious knows the way. Sit comfortably, close your eyes, take a deep breath…"

— Quick body scan —

"Feet relaxing… calves relaxing… thighs… stomach… chest… shoulders… neck… face… Your whole body is relaxed…"

— Fractionation (once) —

"In a moment I'm going to ask you to open your eyes… and close them… Open. Close. Eyes closed, and you relax three times deeper…"

— Arm drop with amnesia —

"I'll take your hand… lift it… and when I release it, it will drop… and the moment it drops the light will go out, and all the numbers will disappear…"

[release the hand — it drops — and immediately a forehead tap]

"TAP! It all vanishes, vanishes, it's all gone… Silence… Emptiness…"

— Check —

"Are they all gone?.. You are in deep hypnosis…"

🧠 Important: The forehead tap should be loud and unexpected enough to "reset" any remaining conscious control, but not frightening. Warn the person in advance: "I'm going to tap your forehead, it's part of the process."

Shortened Version 2 — Without Fractionation

When to use: from the third session onward, or when you see the person has "deflated like a balloon" — meaning they entered deep relaxation already during the body scan.

Structure:

  1. Minimal pre-talk
  2. Body scan (very quick, 30–60 seconds)
  3. Immediate arm drop with amnesia
  4. Test: eyelid catalepsy
  5. Emergence

Fractionation is omitted because the person has already learned to drop into trance without a "pattern interruption." If you see that during the body scan their eyelids have become heavy, their breathing has slowed, their face has relaxed — skip fractionation. They're already there.

Full script for version 2:

— Pre-talk —

"You know this process. Just close your eyes and allow yourself to relax…"

— Body scan (quick) —

"Feet… legs… torso… arms… shoulders… neck… face… Your whole body is completely relaxed…"

— Arm drop with amnesia (no fractionation) —

"I'll take your hand… lift it… and when I release it, you will drop into deep somnambulism… All numbers will disappear, all thoughts will dissolve…"

[release the hand — forehead tap]

"All gone! Emptiness. Silence. You are in deep hypnosis…"

— Check —

"Try to open your eyes… they are sealed shut… when you're sure — say: I am convinced…"

Signs the person has "deflated like a balloon" — you can skip steps:

  • Body has gone limp, posture has lost tension
  • Breathing is deep and even, audible exhalations
  • Face is completely relaxed, jaw has dropped
  • Eyelids don't flutter — they are "glued" on their own
  • Answers questions with a delay or not right away

Trance Deepening and Stabilization

Regardless of which version you use, after entering somnambulism it's useful to perform deepening. It stabilizes the trance and makes it more resistant to external distractions.

Technique: Counting from 10 to 1 with Shoulder Pressure

This is the primary deepening technique in Elman induction:

"Now I'm going to count from 10 down to 1… with each number you will sink deeper…"

"I'll place my hand on your shoulder… at the peak of your inhale I'll press… and that signal will take you deeper…"

[place your hand on the shoulder; wait for an inhale — press at the peak]

"10… deeper…" [shoulder press at peak of inhale]

"9… every exhale takes you deeper…" [press]

"8… any sounds take you deeper…" [press]

"7… the sound of my voice takes you deeper…" [press]

"6… the deeper you go, the better you feel…" [press]

"5… deeper and deeper…" [press]

"4… each moment — even deeper…" [press]

"3… deep, calm trance…" [press]

"2… every relaxation doubles…" [press]

"1… complete somnambulism… silence… emptiness…"

Key Amplifying Phrases

These phrases can be inserted between numbers or used separately to maintain depth:

  • "Every exhale takes you deeper" — links to the natural physiological rhythm
  • "Any sounds take you deeper" — protection from noise: any external sound becomes a deepening trigger
  • "The sound of my voice takes you deeper" — anchors to your voice, strengthens rapport
  • "The deeper you go, the better you feel" — positive reinforcement, removes fear of depth

Why NOT to Use Staircase and Elevator Metaphors

Many beginner hypnotists use metaphors: "imagine you're going down in an elevator," "you're walking down a staircase." This is popular, but Elman has important objections:

  • Phobias: a person may have claustrophobia (elevator) or a fear of heights (stairs). You don't know this in advance, and the metaphor could cause discomfort.
  • Distraction: visualizing a staircase or elevator requires active imagination, which distracts from the trance state. Instead of "sinking," the person is "painting a picture."
  • Redundancy: counting from 10 to 1 with shoulder pressure works directly, without intermediaries. It's pure physiology + suggestion. No metaphors needed.

Rule: the more direct the suggestion, the more effective it is. Don't add visualizations where they aren't needed.

Post-Hypnotic Window — Work After Emergence

As mentioned in Lesson 1.5, after coming out of trance, the person retains heightened suggestibility for 30–90 seconds. In the context of shortened versions, this window becomes even more important.

Why? Because shortened versions are shorter, and you have less time for suggestions inside the trance. The post-hypnotic window allows you to add suggestions after emergence.

What to say in the post-hypnotic window:

  • "Each time it becomes easier and easier to enter trance"
  • "You notice how pleasant it feels to be relaxed"
  • "Your subconscious continues working on [the topic] even after you've opened your eyes"

Say this after the person has opened their eyes, but before they start actively talking and moving. As soon as they speak or move — the window has closed.

When to Use Which Version

Here is a simple guide for choosing the version based on session number:

First session — full version (Lessons 1.1–1.5)

Pre-talk → body scan → fractionation (3×) → counting from 100 with amnesia → eyelid catalepsy → analgesia → emergence. Don't skip anything. The person must go through the entire path from start to finish so the subconscious remembers the "route."

Second session — shortened version 1

Pre-talk (shortened) → quick body scan → fractionation (1×) → arm drop + tap → eyelid catalepsy → emergence. Counting from 100 omitted — the subconscious already knows what "numbers disappeared" means.

Third+ session — shortened version 2 or instant induction

Minimal pre-talk → very quick body scan → arm drop → tap → emergence. Fractionation omitted. The person drops into somnambulism in 1–2 minutes. For the experienced — instant induction (future modules).

🧠 Rule: If after a shortened version the person doesn't enter deep enough trance — go back to the full version for one session. Shortening is a privilege that must be earned. Don't try to shorten until the person has proven they can enter deep somnambulism with the full version.

Summary: Three Versions of the Induction

🔵 Full version (first session, ~20 min)

Pre-talk → Body scan → Fractionation (3×) → Counting from 100 with amnesia → Eyelid catalepsy → Analgesia → Deepening → Emergence

🟢 Shortened version 1 (second session, ~10 min)

Pre-talk (short) → Body scan (quick) → Fractionation (1×) → Arm drop + tap (with amnesia) → Eyelid catalepsy → Emergence

🟠 Shortened version 2 (third+ session, ~5 min)

Pre-talk (min.) → Body scan (very quick) → Arm drop + tap → Eyelid catalepsy → Emergence

📝 Practical Assignment

Goal: Conduct each of the three versions of the induction on the same partner, with intervals of a few days.

  1. Session 1 (full version): perform the full protocol with pre-talk, body scan, triple fractionation, counting from 100, eyelid catalepsy, and analgesia. Time the session.
  2. Session 2 (shortened version 1): after 2–3 days, perform shortened version 1 — skip counting from 100, replace with arm drop + tap. Time the session.
  3. Session 3 (shortened version 2): after another 2–3 days, perform version 2 — skip fractionation. Time the session.
  4. After each session, ask your partner: "How deep was the trance on a scale of 1 to 10?" Compare results.
  5. Add deepening (10–1 count with shoulder pressure) to each version. Note the difference in trance stability.

🧠 Final advice: Shortened versions are not a speed competition. If a person enters deep trance in 15 minutes with the full version — that's great. If they do it in 3 minutes with a shortened version — that's also great. The key is trance quality, not time. Don't chase speed — chase depth.

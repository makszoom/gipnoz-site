Module 2 — Deepening & Resistance Work

5 lessons • ~1.5 hours • Free

You've mastered the Dave Elman induction and can guide a person into somnambulism in 4 minutes. Now you need to learn how to stabilize this state, deepen it, and handle situations when something doesn't go according to plan.

This module covers everything that comes right after induction: how to keep a person below the somnambulism threshold, how to check depth with a second hidden test, what to do if the client resists or doesn't follow directions, and how to start giving your first direct suggestions.

The material is based on the Kayfabe methodology and the master class "Advanced Hypnosis Techniques".

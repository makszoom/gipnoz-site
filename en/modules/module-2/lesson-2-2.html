Module 2 • ~15 minutes

🔍 Two Hidden Tests for Somnambulism

Once you've induced trance and performed stabilization (lesson 2.1), you need to know — is the person really in somnambulism or just deeply relaxed but still holding conscious control?

Asking directly "are you in trance?" doesn't work — it engages the conscious mind and breaks the state. Instead, we use hidden tests — checks that are built into the process and invisible to the client.

Test #1: Number Amnesia (built into Elman)

The first hidden test is already in the Elman protocol — number amnesia. When you say "I will name three random numbers, and you will remember them… 1… 2… 3…" and suggest the person forget one of them. If they are in somnambulism — they really forget. If no forgetting occurs — you're not at the right depth yet.

Test #2: Fractionation 1-2-3 Deeper

The second hidden test is fractionation through counting 1-2-3. This is a more subtle tool that checks how automatically the subconscious responds to your instructions without conscious involvement.

🧠 Important: Tests are not about "catching" the client or proving how great a hypnotist you are. They are for adjusting your work. If the test isn't passed — you haven't failed, you just know you need to work more on depth.

🔢 How the Test Works: 1-2-3 Deeper

The essence of the test is simple: you give the command "when I count to 3, you will open your eyes" — but say it with a special intonation characteristic of fractionation. And on the counts of "1" and "2" you observe the reaction.

How it works:

  • You say: "Now I'm going to count to 3… 1 — deeper… 2 — even deeper… 3 — open your eyes"
  • If the person is in somnambulism: they do not open their eyes on "1" or "2." They wait for "3" because the subconscious took your instruction literally: "open eyes on 3." The reaction is automatic, without conscious evaluation.
  • If the person is NOT in somnambulism: they might open their eyes slightly on "2" or even "1." Why? Because the conscious mind anticipates the command. It thinks, "well, I'll be opening them soon, why wait."

"Now I'm going to count to 3… and on the count of 3 you will open your eyes… 1 — deeper… 2 — even deeper… 3 — open your eyes!"

→ Observe: did the eyes open only on "3" (test passed) or did the eyelids flutter/open earlier (not passed).

🧠 Nuance: The test is called hidden because the client doesn't know they're being tested. They think you're just doing a fractionation cycle — in and out. If they anticipate the eye opening, they don't even realize they "failed" the test — you'll just spend more time stabilizing next time.

⚠️ Limitations of the Test

Despite the 1-2-3 deeper test being widely used in the hypnosis community, this test has limitations. Here's why:

1. The person may tense up trying to "correctly" follow

Some people, upon hearing "you will open your eyes on 3," start consciously controlling the process: "they're testing me, I need to open exactly on 3." This engages the conscious mind — which is exactly what we want to avoid. Instead of deepening, you get a client who is tensely waiting and counting.

2. Can cause irritation if it doesn't work

If the person "fails" the test (opens eyes on 2) and you repeat the attempt over and over — it can cause irritation. The client feels like they "can't do it," that they're a bad subject. This destroys rapport and compliance.

3. Better to rely on the number test

The number amnesia test (built into Elman) is more reliable and less intrusive.

🧠 Understand this: "If I'm already in somnambulism — why test? I already know. If I'm not in somnambulism — the test won't help me, I need to deepen. The fractionation test is a tool for those who aren't sure of their depth. But it's better to learn to feel depth without tests."

🎯 When to Use the Test

Despite its limitations, the fractionation test can be useful in specific situations:

  • When you're unsure of depth: After induction and stabilization, you're not sure — is the person in deep trance or just well-relaxed? The test gives quick feedback.
  • When the person has "floated up": In the middle of a session, you notice depth has decreased (client moves, swallows, changes position). A quick fractionation cycle 1-2-3 will also check how deeply they've returned.
  • When you're learning: For beginning hypnotists, the test helps "calibrate" depth perception. The more tests you run, the more accurately you'll detect somnambulism without tools.
  • When the client is "opaque": Some people don't show external signs of trance. The test is the only way to know if your suggestions are working.

🧠 Advice: Use the test no more than once per session. If you check every 30 seconds, the client will start expecting the test and the conscious mind will engage. One test — got information — adjusted your work.

🔄 What to Do If the Test Fails

The test failed — the client opened their eyes on "2" or even "1." This is not a failure. It's simply information. Here's what to do next:

1. Continue 1-2-3 deeper until stabilization

Don't stop. Continue the fractionation cycle: "1 — deeper… 2 — even deeper… 3 — deeper…" Just remove the eye-opening command. Use fractionation as a deepening tool, not a test. After 3-4 cycles, depth will increase.

"1 — deeper… and with every exhale deeper… 2 — even deeper, twice as deep… 3 — full immersion… and again… 1 — deeper… 2 — even deeper… 3 — the deepest depth…"

2. Return to stabilization from lesson 2.1

Do a reverse count from 5 to 1 with a shoulder press. Add stabilization phrases. When you feel the person has "settled" deeper — try the test once more (one repeat maximum).

3. The Talk (if needed)

If after several deepening attempts the person still won't go into somnambulism — there may be conscious resistance. In this case, you need to conduct The Talk (lesson 2.3) — an open conversation with the client about what's blocking the depth. Sometimes the block is fear, distrust, or misunderstanding of the process.

🧠 Golden rule: Never fixate on one test. If the test fails — you have a dozen other ways to deepen and check. Fractionation, counting, stabilization phrases, hand catalepsy, levitation. The more tools you know, the more flexible your work.

📜 Sample Fractionation Test Script

Below is a full script that combines stabilization (lesson 2.1), the fractionation test, and the response to the result. Use it as a template, adapting it to your style.

Step 1 — Stabilization (after induction):

"10 — deep… 9 — deeper… 8 — even deeper… 7 — with every exhale… 6 — deeper still… 5 — deeper… 4 — deeper… 3 — very deep… 2 — deeper… 1 — full immersion…"

Step 2 — Stabilization phrases:

"Any sounds around you do not bother you at all, they only help you relax deeper… The sound of my voice takes you deeper… The deeper you go, the better you feel… And the better you feel, the deeper you go…"

Step 3 — Fractionation test:

"Now I'm going to count to 3… and on the count of 3 you will open your eyes… 1 — deeper… 2 — even deeper… 3 — open your eyes!"

▸ If the test passed (eyes opened only on 3):

"Good… close your eyes again… and go even deeper… now we're going to work…"

▸ If the test failed (eyes opened on 1 or 2):

"Okay, close your eyes… And again: 1 — deeper… 2 — even deeper… 3 — deeper… And again: 1 — deeper… 2 — deeper… 3 — the deepest depth… Now I'm going to count to 3 one more time, and on the count of 3 you will open your eyes… 1 — deeper… 2 — deeper… 3 — open your eyes!"

📝 Practice Assignment

Goal: Perform a hidden fractionation test on a partner.

  1. Induce trance in your partner using the Elman protocol (Module 1).
  2. Perform stabilization: count 10→1 with shoulder press + stabilization phrases.
  3. Run the test: "on the count of 3 open your eyes — 1 deeper, 2 even deeper, 3 open your eyes."
  4. Record the result: on which count did your partner open their eyes?
  5. If the test failed — repeat fractionation 1-2-3 deeper without opening eyes 2-3 times and try again.
  6. Discuss with your partner: did they feel like they were being tested? Did they feel they "should" open on 3?

🧠 Important: If the test fails — that's normal for the first time. Don't show disappointment. Just say "great, let's try again" and continue deepening. Your calmness is the main success factor.

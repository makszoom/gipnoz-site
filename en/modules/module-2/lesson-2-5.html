2.5 — Post-Hypnotic Window and Session Preparation

Module 2 • ~15 minutes

🎬 Video will be embedded after upload to YouTube

Post-Hypnotic Window: 30-90 Seconds

When you bring a person out of trance, they don't emerge instantly. Hypnosis is not an on/off switch. The critical factor (conscious resistance) closes gradually. This window — 30-90 seconds after emergence — is called the post-hypnotic window.

In this moment, the person is in a unique state: they are already "awake," but their subconscious is still open to suggestions. They hear you, they understand, but they don't filter through the conscious mind the way they would in a normal state.

🧠 Key rule: Do not stop giving suggestions immediately after emergence. The window is open — use it. As soon as the person starts talking, analyzing, looking around — the window has closed.

How to Give Suggestions After Emergence

The technique is simple: you bring the person out of trance, but keep talking in a calm, confident voice. Your suggestions "catch up" to their subconscious while it's still open.

Examples of Post-Hypnotic Suggestions

"…3, 2, 1 — eyes open. You're back. Notice how good you feel…"

"You will notice that this feeling of calm stays with you…"

"With each passing day, it becomes easier and easier for you to enter this state…"

"Your subconscious continues to work, even now that you're back…"

What to emphasize in the post-hypnotic window:

  • Good feeling — "you feel rested, refreshed, full of energy"
  • Positive attitude toward hypnosis — "you enjoyed this state, and next time will be even deeper"
  • Reinforcing results — "what we did today stays with you"
  • Anticipation of the next session — "you're looking forward to the next meeting"

First Session Structure

The first session is the most important. It sets the tone for all subsequent ones. Here is a proven structure:

  1. Diagnosis (information gathering) — the three questions from lesson 2.4. What do you want? How will you know? Why?
  2. Pre-talk (addressing fears) — explain that hypnosis is safe, that the person is in control, that it's a natural state. Dispel myths.
  3. Full Elman induction — from progressive relaxation to deep trance.
  4. Eyelid catalepsy and checks — eyelids locked, arm rises, levitation. Make sure the person is in trance.
  5. Positive suggestions — the first 10-15 minutes of suggestions based on the client's request. Don't try to fix everything in one session.
  6. Emergence — a calm, smooth emergence from trance.
  7. Post-hypnotic window — additional suggestions for 30-90 seconds after emergence.

🧠 Important: The first session is not about "healing." It's about demonstrating that hypnosis works. The client should leave with the feeling: "Wow, this really works. I want more."

Preparing for Regression

Regression (returning to a traumatic event) is a powerful tool, but it requires preparation. Do not start regression on the first session.

  • Somnambulism is mandatory — regression requires a deep stage of trance. If the person is not in somnambulism, regression will be superficial or traumatic.
  • First session — direct suggestion — provides temporary relief and hope. The client sees that hypnosis works and trusts the process.
  • From the second session — regression — once trust is established and trance depth is sufficient, you can move to regressive work.
  • Don't rush — it's better to do 2-3 sessions of direct suggestions than to ruin a regression due to insufficient depth.

🧠 Rule: Speed induces trance. Hesitation — uncertainty. But speed doesn't mean rushing. A professional works quickly and clearly, without unnecessary pauses or hesitation.

Professionalism = Speed

One of the main signs of a professional is speed of work. Speed induces trance. If you hesitate, re-read the script, take long pauses — you broadcast uncertainty. The client feels this and cannot relax.

  • Fast commands — leave no time for analysis. The client simply executes.
  • Confident voice — even if you made a mistake in the script, continue with the same tone. The client doesn't know the script — they won't notice the error.
  • Minimum pauses between steps — transition from induction to check, from check to suggestion — in one motion.
  • Practice until automatic — the session structure should be in muscle memory so you don't think about "what's next."

🧠 Advice: Time your first session from start to finish. Aim for 25-30 minutes for a full session (including diagnosis and pre-talk). If it's longer — cut, speed up, practice.

📝 Practice Assignment

Goal: Write out a first session plan on paper, then conduct it.

  1. Write a session plan on a sheet of paper following the structure above (7 steps).
  2. For each step, write 1-2 key phrases — anchor points.
  3. Conduct the session with a partner, timing yourself:
    • Diagnosis + pre-talk: 5 minutes
    • Induction + checks: 10 minutes
    • Suggestions: 10 minutes
    • Emergence + post-hypnotic window: 2 minutes
  4. After the session, ask for feedback: what was unclear? where was there a pause? what could be sped up?

🧠 Success criterion: You should finish within 27 minutes. If longer — practice until you do. Professionalism is speed.

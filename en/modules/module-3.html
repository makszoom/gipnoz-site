Module 3 — Instant & Rapid Inductions

6 lessons • ~1.5 hours • Free

Transition into trance in seconds. After mastering the full Elman induction (Module 1) and learning to stabilize somnambulism (Module 2), you are ready for rapid and instant techniques.

At the core are 4 principles formulated in the Kayfabe methodology: focus/distraction, shock/surprise, the "sleep" command, and instant deepening. Knowing these principles, you can create any instant induction on your own.

The material is based on master class demonstrations: rapid hand induction (Kayfabe style), standing induction with balance, verbal shock, and the "shock of no shock" technique.

This module provides tools for fast work in 3+ sessions and for demonstration contexts.

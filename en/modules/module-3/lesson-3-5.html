Module 3 • 15 min

🔢 Number Amnesia — Demonstration with Liliya

This is a classic phenomenon that Jerry demonstrates on his student Liliya. The technique shows how the subconscious can selectively block information on command.

Execution technique:

  1. Induce deep trance (somnambulism) in the subject using any rapid induction.
  2. At the peak of trance, give the suggestion: "Now I'm going to teach your subconscious to skip one number. When you count from 1 to 10, the number 6 will disappear. You'll say: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10. The number 6 simply doesn't exist for you."
  3. Repeat the suggestion 2–3 times with different wording.
  4. Bring the subject out of trance.
  5. After emergence, say: "Count out loud from 1 to 10" — the subject will skip the number 6.
  6. Remove the suggestion: "Now I'll snap my fingers, and you'll see all numbers again. One, two, three — snap!"
  7. Verify: "Count again" — now the number 6 is back.

Important: amnesia only works if the subject is truly in somnambulism. If there's no trance, the person will simply "play along" with skipping the number to please you. Learn to distinguish a real phenomenon from social compliance.

📜 Script: Number Amnesia

In trance, confident, suggestive voice:

"Your subconscious is about to receive an interesting task... It will learn to skip one number... Just one number out of ten...

When you open your eyes and I ask you to count from 1 to 10... you'll count easily and freely... 1... 2... 3... 4... 5... ... 7... 8... 9... 10...

The number 6 simply won't be there. It will disappear. You'll try to remember which number was skipped — and you won't be able to. The harder you try — the further it slips away...

Even if someone hints at it, you won't believe them. For you, the number 6 doesn't exist. You'll just skip it — and that's all.

Good. Now I'll count to three, and you'll open your eyes, feeling refreshed and rested. One... two... three — eyes open, fully awake."

After emergence: "Count out loud from 1 to 10, just to warm up."

After the demonstration — removal: "Now I'm going to snap my fingers, and the number 6 will return to its place. Snap! That's it, now all numbers are back. Count again."

👤 Name Amnesia

An even more impressive phenomenon — temporary amnesia for one's own name. The person can't remember their name for a few seconds.

Execution technique:

  1. In trance, give the suggestion: "On the count of 3, you won't remember your name."
  2. After emergence, use the count: "One... two... three — what's your name?"
  3. The subject freezes, tries to remember — and can't. The harder they try, the further the name slips away.
  4. After 5–10 seconds, remove the suggestion: "Now you remember."

Why this works: a name is the most automatic information in memory. When it suddenly becomes inaccessible, cognitive dissonance occurs, which confirms the power of the suggestion. The subject becomes convinced: "I really was in hypnosis, since I couldn't remember my own name."

⚠️ Caution: do not prolong name amnesia beyond 10–15 seconds. It may cause anxiety in the subject. Remove the suggestion quickly and confidently.

👁️ Eyelid Catalepsy After Rapid Entry

Eyelid catalepsy is a state where the eyelid muscles temporarily "lock up," and the person cannot open their eyes even when trying. In the classic Elman induction, this is one of the steps. In rapid hypnosis, you check for catalepsy immediately after entry to confirm depth.

Execution technique:

  1. Perform a rapid or instant induction (lessons 3.1–3.4).
  2. Immediately after entry, after 2–3 seconds, say: "Your eyes are tightly closed. You can't open your eyes. Try — your eyes won't open."
  3. If the subject can't open their eyes — catalepsy worked. This confirms somnambulism.
  4. If the eyes open — trance isn't deep enough. Either you didn't enter deeply enough, or the subject is resisting. Deepen and try again.

Removal: "And now your eyes open easily. One... two... three — open your eyes."

Note: in some people, eyelid catalepsy doesn't work even in deep trance — this is an individual trait. Don't insist; move on to other phenomena.

🔄 Post-hypnotic Reinduction

Post-hypnotic reinduction is a suggestion that makes the subject re-enter trance after emergence, upon activation of a specific trigger. This is a powerful tool for multi-session work.

Execution technique:

  1. In trance, give the suggestion: "On the count of 3, you'll open your eyes. When I tug on my earlobe, you'll sink into a trance deeper than now."
  2. Count to three. The subject opens their eyes.
  3. After 5–10 seconds, tug on your earlobe. The subject should instantly re-enter trance, even deeper.
  4. This confirms: (a) the post-hypnotic suggestion works, (b) the subconscious continues to respond to triggers.

Trigger options:

  • Shoulder touch
  • Finger snap
  • A specific word ("relax," "deep")
  • Eye contact of a certain duration

Application: reinduction allows you to enter and exit trance multiple times in one session, deepening the state with each cycle. This accelerates the client's learning to enter trance.

📋 Conditions for Successful Phenomena

Not all subjects and not always will phenomena work. For a successful demonstration, the following conditions are necessary:

  • Absence of fears — the subject must not fear hypnosis or loss of control. Fear blocks the subconscious.
  • Willingness to participate — the subject must want to experience the phenomenon. Coercion or skepticism reduces effectiveness.
  • Trust — the subject trusts the hypnotist. Trust is built before the trance, in conversation.
  • Somnambulism (confirmed) — phenomena only work in deep trance. Check eyelid catalepsy or ideomotor signals before suggesting a phenomenon.
  • Desire to "play" — the person must be ready to engage in the game, the experiment. Overly serious, controlling subjects rarely demonstrate clean phenomena.

If the phenomenon didn't work — don't insist and don't get upset. Simply say: "Great, next time it'll work. For now, let's just work with relaxation." This preserves trust and doesn't create pressure.

📝 Practice Assignment

Perform number amnesia:

  1. Induce trance in your partner using any rapid induction from lessons 3.1–3.4.
  2. Check trance depth (eyelid catalepsy).
  3. Give the suggestion to skip the number 6 when counting from 1 to 10 (use the script above).
  4. Bring them out of trance.
  5. Ask them to count out loud from 1 to 10.
  6. If the number is skipped — record the success. If not — analyze: was there somnambulism? Was there trust?
  7. Remove the suggestion with a finger snap.

Task 2. Post-hypnotic Reinduction

With the same partner (or another), practice reinduction. Suggest that on the count of 3, eyes will open, and upon touching the earlobe, trance will return deeper. Test it.

Task 3. Journal

Record which phenomena worked and which didn't. What influenced the outcome? Trance depth? Subject's mood? Your confidence?

Module 4 — Demonstrating Phenomena

6 lessons • ~1.5 hours • Free

Hypnotic phenomena — catalepsy, analgesia, amnesia, hallucinations — are not "magic" but verifiable manifestations of somnambulism. They are only possible in deep hypnosis and only if the person willingly participates.

This module covers everything related to phenomena: how to induce them, when to use them (therapy vs demonstration), and why in a therapeutic context you must not perform open tests.

Key rule: if you know the person is in somnambulism, any phenomenon will work. The only reason for refusal is that the person does not want to "play along" or has a hidden fear.

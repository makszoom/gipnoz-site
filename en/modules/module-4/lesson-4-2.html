4.2 — Catalepsy and Analgesia

Module 4 • 15 min

🎬 Video will be inserted after upload to YouTube

🧊 What Is Catalepsy

Catalepsy is a hypnotic phenomenon in which muscles are either completely relaxed or completely tensed in a fixed position. It is the simplest and most reliable way to demonstrate to a person that hypnosis is a real state.

There are three levels of catalepsy, tied to trance depth:

  • Light trance: catalepsy of small muscles — eyelids
  • Medium trance: catalepsy of large muscle groups — arm, leg, full body
  • Somnambulism: any catalepsy is accessible

👁️ Eyelid Catalepsy (Review from M1)

You already know this technique from Module 1. In the context of phenomena, it is used as an overt test — a demonstration to the client themselves.

Key phrase: "The harder you try to open them, the tighter they close."

Reinforcement phrase: "When you're convinced, say: I am convinced" — this gives the client's conscious mind irrefutable feedback.

💪 Arm Catalepsy (Arm Rigidity)

Technique: "Your arm is becoming hard as a steel rod. It won't bend. Try to bend it — and feel that you can't."

Important: after testing, release the catalepsy with a suggestion to relax. Do not leave the person in a frozen state.

🩺 Analgesia (Pain Suppression Test)

From the transcript, demonstration with Inna:

Analgesia Script

"In a few seconds, I'm going to touch your arm. You'll know that I'm touching your arm, but nothing I do will cause you the slightest discomfort at all. You simply know I'm here, and there is no discomfort at all."

The operator pinches the skin on the wrist with their fingertips. Firmly. Not with the nails.

Inna: "I felt that you were pinching me, but I knew you were going to do that. It's all fine."

🎯 Why Catalepsy Is So Important

Hypnosis doesn't feel like anything. A person may think they are not in hypnosis, even when they are in deep somnambulism. Catalepsy provides irrefutable experience: the person tries to open their eyes — and can't. Or tries to bend their arm — and can't. This convinces the conscious mind better than any words.

"That's why I love doing eyelid catalepsy. The person says: 'I can't open my eyes!' I say: 'Exactly. You're in hypnosis.'"

📝 Practice Assignment

  1. Put your partner into somnambulism (Elman).
  2. Perform eyelid catalepsy: "try to open — the harder you try, the tighter they close."
  3. Perform arm catalepsy: suggest rigidity, test it.
  4. Perform an analgesia test: give the suggestion, pinch the skin on the wrist.
  5. Ask: "What did you feel?"
  6. Record the results.

4.3 — Amnesia: Types and Techniques

Module 4 • 15 min

🎬 Video will be inserted after upload to YouTube

🧠 What Is Hypnotic Amnesia

Hypnotic amnesia is a temporary blocking of access to specific information. The person cannot remember something they know perfectly well. It is not memory loss — it is a temporary block that can be lifted on command.

Key fact: amnesia is only possible in somnambulism. If a person cannot forget something at your suggestion — they are not in somnambulism.

That is precisely why numbers disappearing while counting back from 100 is a covert test for somnambulism.

🔢 Number Amnesia (Covert Test)

Built into the Elman induction. The person counts back from 100, adding "deeper relaxation." After a few numbers, they "disappear" — the person stops saying them because there is nothing to say.

The person does not know they are being tested. This makes the test "covert" — the patient cannot "play along" or "fear failure."

"Remember, this is a covert test for somnambulism. The person doesn't know, has no idea where they are. But I know they are in somnambulism."

📛 Name Amnesia (Overt Test)

After you have confirmed somnambulism (with a covert test), you can do overt tests.

Name Amnesia Script

"In a few seconds, I will count to three. On the count of three, you will open your eyes. You won't remember what your name is. The harder you try to remember, the more the name escapes you. Just for a little while. Wish for it to happen. One, two, three. What is your name?.. Don't remember? That's normal. Now I'll snap my fingers — and the name will come back."

Always have a return command (snap, touch).

⚖️ Aphasia vs Amnesia

It's important not to confuse these states:

  • Aphasia: the person cannot speak due to deep relaxation of the jaw. They want to answer but physically cannot.
  • Amnesia: the person can speak but has nothing to say — the information has disappeared from consciousness.

How to tell the difference: ask "Is it all gone?" If the person nods — it's amnesia. If they are silent — it may be aphasia. Get verbal or non-verbal confirmation.

⚠️ What NOT to Do

"Never say 'all the numbers are gone.' Never. Because as soon as you say 'numbers' — they come back."

The subconscious takes words literally. Say: "Everything is gone. Disappeared. Dissolved. There is nothing."

📝 Practice Assignment

  1. Put your partner into somnambulism.
  2. Perform a covert test: counting back from 100, numbers disappearing.
  3. After confirmation — name amnesia (overt test).
  4. Lift the block: "Snap — the name came back."
  5. Repeat with another person.
  6. Record who reacted how.

4.5 — Hallucinations with Open and Closed Eyes

Module 4 • 15 min

🎬 Video will be inserted after upload to YouTube

👁️ Overview of Hypnotic Hallucinations

Hypnotic hallucinations are perceptual phenomena that occur exclusively under the influence of suggestion in a trance state. They fall into two fundamental types:

  • Positive hallucinations — the subject sees, hears, or feels something that does not exist in objective reality.
  • Negative hallucinations — the subject does not see, hear, or feel something that does exist in reality.

In this lesson, we will cover both types, with special attention to how they work with open and closed eyes. The difference is fundamental: with closed eyes, the subconscious projects images onto the "inner screen"; with open eyes, the perception of reality itself is altered.

✨ Positive Hallucinations with Closed Eyes

The subject is in trance with eyes closed. The subconscious is free from external distractions and can project vivid, detailed images. This is not just imagination — it is full-fledged seeing.

From the transcript [49:42–50:49]: The operator suggests: "Imagine that a red ball appears before your inner vision. It becomes brighter and brighter, more and more real. You see its shades, the highlights on its surface. It's so real that you can reach out and touch it." The subject in trance describes the ball in minute detail — even though objectively the ball does not exist.

Features of working with closed eyes:

  • Easier to induce — no need to compete with real visual stimuli
  • Can be very vivid, up to full sensory reality
  • The subject can interact with the images (touch, smell)
  • Well suited for therapeutic work (resource imagery, inner child)

Script for closed eyes: "Now, with your eyes closed, you see the space in front of you. On this dark background, a light begins to appear. At first it's just a glow, but gradually it forms into an image. You see... (name the object). Look at it carefully — what shape, color, texture does it have?"

🌟 Positive Hallucinations with Open Eyes

The subject opens their eyes while remaining in deep trance. The suggestion is overlaid on real visual perception — and alters it.

From the transcript [96:06–97:17]: The operator suggests to the subject looking at numbers on a sheet of paper: "Now these numbers will start moving. They are changing places. The number 9 becomes 6, the number 3 becomes 8. Do you see how they float across the paper?" The subject, with open eyes, looks at the stationary sheet and describes how the numbers move, change, flip.

Features of working with open eyes:

  • Harder to induce — the brain receives real visual signals that need to be "rewritten"
  • Requires deeper trance (somnambulism 5+)
  • The effect can be unpredictable — it manifests differently in different subjects
  • An incredibly spectacular phenomenon for demonstration

Script for open eyes: "Now you will open your eyes, but remain in this deep state. Look at this sheet of paper. There are numbers written on it. Look at them carefully... They are starting to move. The numbers are changing places. They are dancing before your eyes. Describe what you see."

🕳️ Negative Hallucinations

A negative hallucination is "not seeing" a real object or person. It is not that the subject "looks away" or "closes their eyes" — they look directly at the object, but their brain does not process that information.

From the transcript [354:30–356:32]: A classic demonstration: "You will not see me. You will open your eyes and look straight at me, but you will not register me. For you, I do not exist." The subject opens their eyes, looks directly at the operator, but acts as if they are not there — looks through them, does not react to movements, does not hear words addressed to them.

Notably: The operator does this immediately after a rapid induction, without lengthy deepening. This speaks to their virtuoso level of skill and the fact that for some subjects, trance depth is achieved instantly.

Comparison with positive hallucinations: According to some scales of hypnotic depth, negative hallucinations require deeper somnambulism than positive ones. Why? Because a positive hallucination "adds" information to what is already being processed — the brain finds it easier to accept an additional stimulus. A negative hallucination "cuts out" an existing stimulus — the brain actively suppresses the processing of real data, which requires deeper trance.

❓ Conditions for Hallucinations

For the successful induction of any type of hallucination, two conditions are necessary:

  1. Somnambulism (deep trance): minimum level — 5 on the Davis-Husband scale. Without this, hallucinations will either not occur or will be weak and unstable. Test depth with catalepsy and amnesia checks before attempting hallucinations.
  2. Desire to play (playfulness): the subject must be willing to "give in" to the game. Hypnotic hallucinations are not an assault on consciousness but a co-creation between hypnotist and subject. If the subject is skeptical or afraid — hallucinations will not work. An atmosphere of trust and curiosity must be established.

Additional success factors:

  • High suggestibility of the subject (if the subject easily enters trance — hallucinations will be more vivid)
  • Developed imagination (visuals find it easier, auditory learners — auditory hallucinations)
  • Previous experience with hypnosis (hallucinations become more vivid with each session)
  • Absence of blocking beliefs ("hypnosis is nonsense," "hallucinations only happen to crazy people")

⚠️ Caution: Not for Therapy

Critical warning from Jerry Kein: hypnotic hallucinations (both positive and negative) are NOT intended for therapeutic work with clients who have psychological trauma or disorders.

Why:

  • Hallucinations interfere with the basic processes of reality perception. In a person with an unstable psyche, this can trigger a psychotic episode.
  • A negative hallucination of "not seeing the operator" may be perceived by the subconscious as "I am disappearing" or "I do not exist" — a powerful destabilizing suggestion.
  • Hallucinations can "stick" — the subject may continue to see/not see objects after emerging from trance if the suggestion was not given carefully enough.

Where to use:

  • On stage for demonstrating the capabilities of hypnosis
  • For educational purposes with mentally healthy volunteers
  • For scientific experiments and research
  • DO NOT USE: in psychotherapy, when working with anxiety disorders, PTSD, depression, psychotic conditions

If your goal is to help a client, use direct suggestion (Module 2) or regression (Lesson 4.6), not hallucinations.

📝 Practice Assignment

Task 1. Positive hallucination with closed eyes

Put your partner into deep trance. Suggest that a red ball appears on the "inner screen." Ask them to describe it: color, size, texture, sensations when touching. Record how detailed the description was.

Task 2. Positive hallucination with open eyes (numbers)

In deep trance, have the subject open their eyes. Show them a sheet with numbers written on it (1 2 3 4 5). Suggest that the numbers are "dancing," changing places, flipping over. Ask: "What do you see?" Record the reaction. If it doesn't work — try again at a deeper level of trance.

Task 3. Negative hallucination — "not seeing an object"

In deep trance, place a cup in front of the subject. Suggest: "When you open your eyes, you will see everything except this cup. The cup does not exist. There is emptiness there." Open their eyes. Ask: "What do you see on the table?" If the subject lists everything except the cup — the hallucination worked. Restore perception: "Now the cup appears again. You see it."

Task 4. Negative hallucination — "not seeing the operator" (caution)

Note: This task is FOR ADVANCED users ONLY. Put your partner into deep trance. Suggest: "When you open your eyes, I will be invisible to you. You will look right through me. I do not exist for you." Open their eyes. Stand still in front of the subject. Ask in a familiar voice: "Who is in this room?" If the subject does not mention you — the hallucination worked. Be sure to restore perception: "Now I become visible again. You see me. Everything is normal."

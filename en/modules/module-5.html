Module 5 — Client Work: Direct Suggestion

5 lessons • ~1.5 hours • Free

You know how to induce somnambulism (M1), stabilize it (M2), perform rapid inductions (M3), and demonstrate phenomena (M4). Now — what to do with a client in hypnosis.

This module covers the system of direct suggestion: three diagnostic questions, four language constructions, the law of compounding, and the key distinction — when direct suggestion works and when regression is needed.

This is the foundation for any therapeutic work. Without regression (a separate 9-hour master class), but with a clear first-session structure and suggestion protocol.

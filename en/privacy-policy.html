Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Calm Anchor browser extension.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Calm Anchor does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on remote servers. All data is stored locally in your browser.

2. Local Storage

The extension uses browser localStorage to remember your preferences (e.g., Instant or Guided mode) and optional anchor strength ratings. This data never leaves your device.

3. Optional Email

If you choose to provide an email address to receive the full 3-minute technique, it is stored locally and is not transmitted to any server.

4. No Third Parties

We do not share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties.

5. Changes

We may update this policy as the extension evolves. Changes will be reflected on this page.

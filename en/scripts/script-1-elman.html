Elman Key Somnambulism

1. Complete Dave Elman Induction

⚡ Full protocol: from pre-talk to somnambulism

When to Use

This script is the complete Dave Elman induction protocol, designed for the first session with a new subject. It includes all stages: from pre-talk and hypnotic contract to exit from somnambulism with a post-hypnotic window.

Use it when:

  • You're working with someone for the first time — the full protocol builds maximum compliance
  • You need to reach somnambulism (deep trance with amnesia)
  • You want to demonstrate the reality of hypnotic phenomena to the client
  • You're practicing the classic Elman sequence "from start to finish"

⏱ Duration: 15–25 minutes. Pace — calm, not rushed, with pauses between phases.

📜 Complete Induction Script

PHASE 1 — Pre-talk (Hypnotic Contract and Preparation)

Speak confidently, calmly, without rushing. This is the foundation of the entire session.

"Make yourself comfortable in the chair. Place your feet flat on the floor, soles fully touching the ground. Rest your hands loosely on your lap or on the armrests. Don't cross them. Take a deep breath… and on the exhale, just allow yourself to begin relaxing."

"Before we start, I want to clarify a few things. Hypnosis is not magic and not loss of control. It is a state of deep focus and relaxation in which your subconscious becomes more receptive to suggestions."

"I can help you if you follow my instructions. If you don't follow my instructions — I can't help you. It's that simple."

"I want your permission for three things. First: during hypnosis, I will address you casually — it helps relaxation and removes formality. Is that okay?" (wait for a response)

"Second: during the session, I may lightly touch your hand, shoulder, or forehead to reinforce relaxation. Is that okay?" (wait for a response)

"And third: if you feel uncomfortable or want to stop — just raise your hand or say 'stop.' Agreed?" (wait for a response)

"Great. Now close your eyes if it's comfortable. If not — just look at a fixed point in front of you."

— Compliance check: ask them to take a deep breath; if they do it without question — you're ready —

"Take a deep breath… and on the exhale, let your body relax. Another deep breath… and on the exhale, let go of all thoughts, all worries of the day. With each exhale, you sink deeper into a state of peace."

PHASE 2 — Eyelid Catalepsy (First Wedge into the Subconscious)

Elman starts not with body relaxation, but with a phenomenon. Relaxing the eyelids to the point where they won't open is the first step that bypasses the critical factor.

"Now focus all your attention on your eyelids… just feel them… how light they are right now… And begin to relax them deeper and deeper…"

"Relax your eyelids as deeply as you possibly can — so deeply that they won't open…"

"Good… Now, keeping that deep relaxation in your eyelids, test them — make sure they won't open… Move your eyebrows… wrinkle your forehead… Feel that your eyelids stay relaxed and closed…"

"Great. If they do open — just close them again and continue relaxing them even deeper…"

— Wait until the eyelids lock. If the client opens their eyes: "You've just proved they open. Now relax them to the point where they won't open." —

PHASE 3 — Body Scan (Wave of Relaxation Through the Body)

As soon as the eyelids are locked, immediately, without pause, move to the wave of relaxation. Use the momentum of the first phenomenon.

"Great… Now take that feeling of deep relaxation from your eyelids and fill your entire body with it. Send it like a wave — from the top of your head to the tips of your fingers and toes."

"Fill every cell… every muscle… Let it flow downward — through your face… neck… shoulders… arms… chest… stomach… back… hips… legs… all the way to your feet…"

"Relax your right arm… from shoulder to fingertips… forearm… wrist… hand… each finger…"

"Relax your left arm… from shoulder down… forearm… wrist… hand… fingers… both arms heavy, relaxed, still…"

"Relax your chest and stomach… each exhale lets go deeper… your breathing becomes even and calm…"

"Relax your back — from neck to lower back… each vertebra… all the muscles of your back, soft and warm…"

"Relax your right leg… thigh… knee… calf… foot… toes…"

"Relax your left leg… thigh… knee… calf… foot… toes… both legs resting, heavy, warm…"

"Your whole body is completely relaxed. From head to toe. No tension. Only deep peace."

"Every muscle in your body is now relaxed. You feel warmth spreading through your body. The relaxation deepens with every exhale…"

PHASE 4 — Fractionation (3 Open-Close Cycles)

Fractionation is the key element of Elman. Each "open — close" cycle deepens trance twofold. Work fast, without pauses between commands.

Hold your hand in front of the client's face, blocking their field of view.

— Cycle 1 —

"In a few seconds, I'll ask you to open and close your eyes…"

"Open your eyes…" (hand in front of face)

"Close your eyes…"

"And relax twice as deep…"

— Cycle 2 —

"Open your eyes…"

"Close your eyes…"

"And relax twice as deep… Even deeper than last time…"

— Cycle 3 —

"Open your eyes…"

"Close your eyes…"

"And relax twice as deep… Complete, absolute relaxation…"

"Excellent…"

PHASE 5 — Arm Drop Test

A test of relaxation depth and simultaneously a deepening technique. If the arm drops like a sack — relaxation is deep.

"Now I'm going to take your right arm…"

(take the arm by the wrist, lift it a few inches above the knee)

"Your arm is like a heavy sack of sand… like a wet rag… completely heavy… Don't help me at all. Give me all the weight of your arm…"

(release the arm)

"Great. Your arm is completely relaxed… And this relaxation spreads through your whole body — even deeper…"

— If the client helps (holds the arm up): "No, no — don't help. Give me all the weight. Let it drop by itself." Repeat until the arm drops freely. —

PHASE 6 — Count Down from 100 with Number Amnesia

Transition from physical relaxation to mental relaxation. Numbers disappear — internal dialogue stops. This is the threshold of somnambulism.

"Now I want you to start counting… slowly and softly… backward from one hundred…"

"100… deeper and deeper…"

"99… more relaxation…"

"98… with each number deeper…"

"97… deeper and deeper…"

"96… complete relaxation…"

"95… and notice how numbers begin to disappear…"

"94… some numbers simply vanish…"

"93… they dissolve…"

"92… you can no longer remember what number came before this…"

"91… they disappear faster and faster…"

"90… in a few numbers, they will all vanish… disappear… dissolve… and only silence will remain…"

"The numbers are disappearing… fading away… they're gone… only emptiness… silence…"

— Wait for silence. If the client keeps counting — repeat the suggestion. If they keep counting consciously — do The Talk. —

(after the pause)

"Is it all gone?" (wait for a nod or "yes")

"Good. You are in deep hypnosis. You have crossed the somnambulism threshold. Your subconscious is now open."

PHASE 7 — Hand Catalepsy (Hand Catalepsy Test)

Hand catalepsy check. Demonstrates to the client that their body is under the control of the subconscious.

"Now I'm going to take your left arm and lift it…"

(lift the client's arm by the wrist and position it vertically, palm facing away from them)

"This arm is becoming light… very light… like a balloon… it rises by itself… and freezes in this position…

"It is locked in the air… as if an invisible force is holding it… it won't come down… completely still…"

"Now try to lower it… try… but it stays in place… it doesn't obey your conscious commands… only my voice can bring it back down…"

(pause 3–5 seconds)

"Good. Stop trying. Now I will touch your arm — and it will gently lower…"

(touch the arm — lower it)

"And this relaxation spreads through your whole body, even deeper…"

PHASE 8 — Eyelid Catalepsy (Demonstration for the Client)

Final check. The client sees for themselves that hypnosis is real: they cannot open their eyes even though they genuinely try. This is a paradoxical suggestion.

"Now bring your attention to your eyelids… they begin to flutter slightly and squeeze tighter…"

"I will count from five to one, and with each number they will squeeze tighter…"

(touch the client's forehead with your finger)

"5… they are squeezing…"

"4… squeezing tighter…"

"3… as if glued shut with superglue…"

"2… eyelids are sealed shut…"

"1… your eyes are completely closed, and you cannot open them…"

"Want it to happen… allow it to happen…"

"Now try to open them…" (quickly and firmly) "…but they only squeeze tighter… the harder you try, the tighter they are closed…"

"When you're convinced you can't open them — say: I am convinced."

— Wait for "I am convinced" or a nod —

"Good. Stop trying. Just let them stay closed… and relax even deeper…"

PHASE 9 — Deepening (Count 10→1 with Shoulder Press)

Stabilizing trance before completion. Synchronization with breath.

"Now I will count from ten down to one. With each number you will sink deeper…"

"I will place my hand on your shoulder… at the peak of your inhale, I will press… and this signal will take you deeper…"

(place your hand on the shoulder)

"10… deeper…" (press on shoulder at peak inhale)

"9… each exhale sends you deeper…" (press)

"8… any sounds send you deeper…" (press)

"7… the sound of my voice sends you deeper…" (press)

"6… the deeper you go, the better you feel…" (press)

"5… deeper and deeper…" (press)

"4… each moment — even deeper…" (press)

"3… deep, peaceful trance…" (press)

"2… every relaxation doubles…" (press)

"1… complete somnambulism… silence… emptiness…"

"You are here. You hear my voice. And you are in a state of deep, healing peace."

PHASE 10 — Post-Hypnotic Suggestions and Exit

Exit with a post-hypnotic window. Never use the words "wake up."

"And now I will give your subconscious a few suggestions that will stay with you…"

"Each time it will be easier and easier for you to enter this state…"

"Each time you hear my voice in this same calm tone, you will instantly return to this deep relaxation…"

"And you will notice that with each day, you feel calmer, more confident, better…"

"Now I will count from one to three. On the count of three, but not before, open your eyes — and notice how good you feel…"

"1… begin to return… become aware of your body… feel the energy coming back…"

"2… feel the room, the sounds around you… your fingers and toes begin to move…"

"3 — open your eyes… deep breath… how do you feel?"

PHASE 11 — Post-Hypnotic Window (30–90 Seconds After Exit)

After exit, the person retains increased suggestibility for about 30–90 seconds. Use this window to reinforce the positive experience, while the conscious mind is just starting to return.

"Notice how pleasant this deep relaxation feels…"

"Your subconscious continues to absorb everything we did today…"

"And each time, entering trance will become easier and easier…"

— After this phrase, give the client time to come back. Offer water. Ask about impressions. Discuss the eyelid catalepsy experience — this strengthens belief in hypnosis. —

💡 Important Notes

  • Pace: Between phases — pause 2–3 seconds. Inside a phase — continuous flow, especially between commands during fractionation.
  • Feedback: After each completed step, say "great," "good," "well done." This anchors correct behavior.
  • Hand on shoulder: Keep your hand on the client's shoulder throughout the session — it's a feedback channel: through shoulder warmth you feel trance depth.
  • Forbidden words: Never use "under hypnosis," "wake up," "try," "hope."
  • Ab-reaction: If the client starts crying or trembling — maintain a calm tone, say "everything is disappearing, focus on your breathing." Do not touch the client during an ab-reaction.

Basic Relaxation For beginners

1. Progressive Relaxation

🧘 Basic scripts

When to Use

This script is the ideal choice for a person's first experience with hypnosis. Progressive relaxation works with almost everyone, requires no special preparation, and delivers predictable results.

Use it when:

  • The subject has never been hypnotized — gentle and safe immersion
  • The person is tense, anxious, or skeptical — relaxation breaks down barriers
  • You need to demonstrate "classic hypnosis" without special effects
  • You are working with therapeutic goals — stress relief, anxiety, insomnia

⏱ Duration: 15–20 minutes. Speak slowly, calmly, and pause between paragraphs.

📜 Induction Script

Preliminary Setup

Make yourself comfortable in the chair. Place your hands on your lap or on the armrests. Put your feet on the floor, don't cross them. Close your eyes if you're comfortable. If not, just gaze at a single point in front of you.

Take a deep breath... and as you exhale, let your body begin to relax. Good. Another deep breath... and as you exhale, release all the tension of this day, all thoughts, all worries. With each exhale, you sink deeper into a state of calm.

Now I will talk about different parts of your body. And when I speak of them — they relax. Completely, deeply, fully. Your task is simply to listen to my voice and allow this to happen.

Beginning of Relaxation — Head and Face

Start with your face. Feel the muscles of your forehead. Relax your forehead... let all the wrinkles smooth out. Relax your eyebrows... eyelids... let them become heavy, very heavy.

Relax your cheeks... relax your jaw — let your teeth separate slightly, don't clench them. Relax your lips... your whole face becomes soft, relaxed, calm.

Warmth spreads across your face... a pleasant warmth of relaxation. And this warmth begins to move downward.

Neck and Shoulders

Now relax your neck. The neck often holds a lot of tension, but right now you can let it go. Allow the muscles of your neck to become soft... relaxed... let your head rest comfortably, without effort.

Bring your attention to your shoulders. Relax your shoulders... just let them drop. Drop them down and back. Feel the tension leaving your shoulders, flowing down your arms.

With each exhale, your shoulders drop even lower... relax even more. This pleasant feeling of letting go.

Arms and Hands

Now relax your right arm. From the shoulder to the fingertips. Relax your forearm... relax your wrist... relax your hand... each finger... let your fingers curl slightly, relaxed, calm.

And now your left arm. From the shoulder — relaxation. Down the forearm... wrist... hand... fingers... your whole left arm is heavy and relaxed. Both arms are resting, heavy, calm, still.

Warmth flows through your arms, warming them from within. This is the warmth of relaxation.

Chest, Abdomen, and Breathing

Bring your attention to your chest and abdomen. Take a breath... and feel the air filling your lungs... and as you exhale, relax your ribcage... release all the muscles of your abdomen.

Your breathing becomes deeper and more even. With each inhale, you fill with calmness. With each exhale, you release the remaining tension.

Breathe easily, freely, rhythmically. Your abdomen gently rises on the inhale and falls on the exhale. Complete, deep relaxation in the chest and abdomen.

Back and Lower Back

Now relax your back. The upper part of your back — between the shoulder blades. Feel the muscles of your back becoming soft... warm... relaxed.

Relax your lower back. If there is any tension there — let it melt away. Imagine a warm light washing over your lower back, warming it, relaxing each vertebra.

Your whole back — from the neck to the tailbone — is relaxed, warm, calm. You feel the chair supporting you, taking all your weight.

Legs and Feet

Relax your right leg. Thigh... knee... calf... shin... ankle... foot... toes... your whole right leg is heavy, relaxed, still.

Relax your left leg. Thigh... knee... calf... foot... toes... both legs are resting, heavy, warm, completely relaxed.

Feel the warmth rising from your feet up through your legs, warming, relaxing, soothing.

Deepening

Now your entire body is relaxed. From head to toe. Every muscle, every nerve, every organ — in a state of deep rest.

You are calm. You are safe. Nothing disturbs you. Only my voice and your breathing.

And now I will begin to count from ten down to one. With each number, you will sink twice as deep. Into a state of complete, absolute peace.

Ten... deep relaxation throughout the body.

Nine... sink deeper.

Eight... feel your mind becoming calm.

Seven... all thoughts fade, melt, disappear.

Six... deeper and deeper.

Five... you are halfway to the deepest state.

Four... three... two... deeper with every moment.

One... complete, absolute relaxation. You are in a deep trance.

You are here. You hear my voice. And you are in a state of deep, healing peace. Enjoy this moment — it is only for you.

💡 Tip

Don't rush. The pauses between paragraphs are the key to this script's success. Give the person 5–10 seconds for each body area. Watch the subject's breathing: when it becomes steady and deep, you're on the right track. If you want to use this script for self-hypnosis, record it with your own voice and listen before sleep.

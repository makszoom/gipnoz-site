Phenomenon Hallucination Complex

11. Negative Hallucination

🔬 Hypnosis phenomena

🕯️ When to Use

Negative hallucination — suggesting not to see a real object. A classic stage hypnosis phenomenon that always leaves a strong impression on audiences.

When to use: after achieving the somnambulistic stage (deep trance with catalepsy, amnesia, and positive hallucinations). Use to demonstrate trance depth or as an advanced-level suggestibility test.

Important: good rapport is required, and the subject must have already demonstrated the ability for positive hallucinations. Do not attempt this script in a light trance — it will lead to resistance and suggestion failure.

Script: Inducing Negative Hallucination

— You are now in a very deep state. Your subconscious is completely open to suggestion. With every word I speak, you sink deeper, into the most secure, safe place within yourself.

— Imagine that you are standing in an empty room. A large, bright room. Look around — the walls are painted a soft gray. The floor is smooth, light-colored. Nothing extra. Just you and the emptiness.

— Now on the count of "three" you will open your eyes, but remain in this deep trance. Your body will be awake, but your subconscious will remain fully under hypnosis. One... Two... Three. Open your eyes.

— You are looking at the room. The same empty room. But now I am going to place this object on the table in front of you. (The hypnotist places a noticeable object on the table — for example, a book or a cup.)

— You see the table. You see its surface. But there is one interesting thing. Your subconscious has decided that some things simply do not exist. And that's perfectly normal. Because your brain is now set in a special way — it can choose what to see and what not to see.

— Listen to me carefully. In a moment, I will say the word "disappear." And when I say it, the object on the table will simply cease to exist for you. You will look directly at the spot where it is, but you won't see it. Your eyes will see an empty space, because your subconscious has removed that image from your perception.

— Ready? Disappear.

— The object is gone. You look at the table and see only an empty surface. There is nothing there. You know that a second ago there was something there, but now — emptiness. The table is completely clear.

— Check for yourself. Look at the table. Slowly move your gaze across the entire surface — left to right. You see only a smooth, empty surface. Nothing extra. That's right. Your subconscious has removed the unnecessary.

— Now I will extend my hand and pass it over the spot where the object was a second ago. You see my hand. It moves through the air. But under it — nothing. Emptiness. A completely empty space.

— Now listen further. When I touch your shoulder and say "return," the object will appear again. You will see it on the table again, as if nothing happened. But until then — it's empty there. And with every breath, this emptiness becomes more natural for you.

— Breathe in... Breathe out... The emptiness on the table is reality. You are certain of this because your eyes see exactly that. You trust your eyes, and they tell you — there is nothing there. And that is correct.

— Now I will pretend to pick up this invisible object. (The hypnotist "takes" the object from the empty spot.) Look — am I holding something in my hand? No. My fingers are closed, but between them — nothing. Air.

— I bring my hand toward you. You can touch my hand. Do you feel my fingers? Yes. But there is nothing between them. Because the object disappeared. Completely. Without a trace.

— Your subconscious has done amazing work. It took an entire object and made it invisible to you. You are not just "trying not to see" — you genuinely do not see it. Because your subconscious has blocked that image at the level of the visual cortex.

— And this is completely normal. In hypnosis, this happens all the time. Your brain can remove from perception any objects that the subconscious considers unnecessary. Right now it obediently followed my command.

— In a few seconds, I will return the object. But remember this feeling — the feeling of control over reality. You have proven that your brain can change what you see. This knowledge will stay with you.

— I touch your shoulder and say: Return. The object is here again. You see it on the table. Clearly, sharply, just as before. Everything is back in its place.

— Look at the object. Now it's here again. You see its color, its shape, its texture. It is real. And you know it was here all along — your subconscious just made it invisible to you. Amazing, isn't it?

— Now close your eyes. Return to your deep, calm state. Breathe in deeply and let this experience settle into your subconscious. You have just experienced one of the most complex hypnotic phenomena — negative hallucination. Your subconscious has proven its incredible power.

— Rest in this state. In a minute, I will bring you out of the trance, counting from three to one. On the count of "one" you will return to full consciousness, rested, calm, and full of energy.

💡 Tip

To enhance the effect, you can use a complication: suggest to the subject that they "do not see" not only the object, but also the hypnotist themselves (e.g., "I will disappear, and you will be talking to an empty chair"). Always complete any negative hallucination with a mandatory return of perception and an anchor for exiting the trance. Never leave a subject with impaired perception — this is unethical and dangerous.

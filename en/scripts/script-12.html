12. Post-Hypnotic Suggestion

🔬 Hypnotic Phenomena

🕯️ When to Use

Post-hypnotic suggestion (PHS) — planting a command in the subconscious that activates after coming out of trance when a specific trigger is encountered (word, gesture, situation). It's the foundation of both stage hypnosis and therapeutic work.

When to apply: when you need the subject to perform an action after hypnosis — in therapy (e.g., "when you hear the count of 'three,' your anxiety will fade away") or on stage (e.g., "when I clap my hands, you will stand up and bow").

Important: PHS must be simple, unambiguous, and not conflict with the subject's values. Execution time — from a few seconds to several days after the suggestion. Test the response immediately after coming out of trance.

Script: Setting a Post-Hypnotic Anchor

— You are in a deep, safe trance. Your subconscious is open and attentive. Every word I speak lands directly in the depths, directly in that part of you that handles actions without conscious involvement.

— And now I want you to picture yourself sitting in that chair after we finish. You're already awake, eyes open, fully conscious. But inside, deep inside, this connection remains — the link to my voice.

— Now listen very carefully. I'm going to give you a simple task. A task you will carry out after you come out of trance. When your eyes open, you will feel completely normal — alert, calm, ordinary. But in your subconscious, one command will be planted.

— Here it is: as soon as I snap my fingers like this — snap — and say the word "awake," you will raise your right hand up, stretch, as if after a long sleep, and say out loud: "Excellent!"

— You will do this automatically. Without thinking. Your hand will rise on its own, as if someone is pulling it upward. You will hear your own voice saying the word, and it will feel completely natural to you.

— Let's rehearse this right now, in trance. Imagine you hear my voice saying: "Awake." Your right hand begins to rise... it rises on its own... and you already hear yourself saying: "Excellent!"

— Good. Your subconscious has memorized this sequence. Snap — the word "awake" — hand up — the word "Excellent!" This chain has been anchored in your subconscious, like a program that will launch automatically.

— And when you come out of trance, you won't remember that I told you this. You'll just sit there, talking with me, feeling great. But at the right moment, your body will do what I told it to. And you won't even be surprised — because it will feel like your own impulse.

— Now I want to strengthen this anchor. Imagine this snap is like a trigger. It sets the mechanism in motion. When you hear "awake," your subconscious instantly activates the entire chain of actions. Instantly. Without hesitation. Without a pause. Your hand flies up, your mouth opens, the word comes out.

— I will repeat the command clearly and slowly. Memorize it at the body level. Snap... "Awake" — your right hand rises on its own, reaches up, and you say: "Excellent!"

— Now I will give you the signal right here, in trance. Snap. "Awake." Your hand rises... higher... even higher... and you say: "Excellent!" Well done. Your subconscious has locked in the anchor one hundred percent.

— In a minute, I will bring you out of trance. You will open your eyes, stretch, feel refreshed. We'll talk about something ordinary. Then I will test the anchor — I'll give the signal. And your subconscious will execute the command clearly, quickly, and naturally.

— Bringing you out. On the count of "three" — full awakening. One — energy returns to your body. Two — you feel lightness throughout your body, clarity of mind. Three — eyes open! Fully awake.

PHS Test: Give the subject a minute to adjust. Talk about something neutral. Then, as if casually, snap your fingers and say clearly: "Awake." Observe the reaction. If the hand didn't rise — don't insist, repeat the installation in the next session with deeper trance.

If the subject performed the command — praise them and say: "Good, now relax, it was just a subconscious joke." Then you can either remove the anchor or leave it for further work. In therapy, PHS is kept active for the required duration (e.g., one month of daily repetitions of the new behavior).

💡 Tip

Always test PHS immediately after the session. If the anchor didn't fire — it's not a failure, but a signal of insufficient trance depth or weak rapport. Deepen the trance and repeat the installation more emotionally and specifically. For reliability, set the anchor on 3 different signals: auditory (word), visual (gesture), and contextual (situation). For therapeutic purposes, use PHS to reinforce new behavior: "Every morning when you brush your teeth, you feel confidence in the day ahead."

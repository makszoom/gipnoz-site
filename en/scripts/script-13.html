13. Confidence and Resources

💊 Therapeutic Scripts

🕯️ When to Use

The resource protocol is one of the most powerful therapeutic techniques in hypnosis. The subject doesn't receive something from the outside — they activate their own internal resource: the memory of a state of maximum confidence, strength, and calm.

When to apply: before an important event (exam, job interview, performance), when working with low self-esteem, social anxiety, indecisiveness. Can be used as a standalone session or as preparation before deeper therapeutic work.

Important: don't impose a specific memory — let the subject's subconscious choose the moment of peak confidence itself. If the subject can't recall one, suggest they "construct" an ideal image of themselves.

Script: Resource State Protocol for Confidence

— Sit comfortably, close your eyes. Take three deep breaths. With each exhale, allow tension to leave your body. Shoulders drop. Jaw relaxes. You are entering a state of peace.

— Good. Now I will ask your subconscious to go on a journey. A journey through your own life. There, deep in your memory, a moment is stored — a moment when you felt absolutely confident.

— It could be any moment in your life. Maybe you aced a difficult exam and knew every answer. Or you performed in front of a large audience and felt on top of the world. Or you helped someone in a tough situation and knew you were doing the right thing. Or maybe it's just a moment from childhood — when you were running through a meadow, the wind in your face, and you felt: "I can do anything."

— Your subconscious already knows what moment this is. It's already beginning to bring it up from the depths of memory. You might feel a slight change in your body — warmth, vibration, a feeling of lightness. The resource is rising to the surface.

— Now allow this memory to become brighter. See the place where you were. Notice the details: the light, the colors, the objects around you. Hear the sounds. Feel the air on your skin. Breathe in the scent of that moment.

— And most importantly — feel what you felt then. That feeling of confidence. Where is it in your body? In your chest? In your belly? In your shoulders? Notice that place. Feel its warmth. Its strength. Its steadiness.

— Now I will touch your shoulder. And when I do, the feeling of confidence will triple. You will feel a wave of warmth and strength pass through your entire body. (Touch the subject's shoulder.)

— Yes. There it is. This state — your inner resource. It was always inside you, you just didn't always know how to connect to it. Now your subconscious has learned to activate it on cue.

— Now imagine this feeling of confidence is like a bright golden light inside your chest. It pulses to the rhythm of your heartbeat. With each heartbeat it becomes brighter, stronger, more stable.

— And now I want you to mentally overlay this light onto the situation where you need confidence. Imagine yourself in that situation — whether it's an exam, a conversation, or a performance — and allow the golden light to fill the entire space around you.

— Look at yourself from the outside. The person standing there — that's you. And you see how they breathe evenly and calmly. Their shoulders are back. Their voice sounds clear and confident. They know what they're saying. They know what they're doing. They know their worth.

— Take a deep breath. And when you exhale, allow this image of your confident self to merge with you. To become part of your reality. Step into it. Feel how confidence becomes your skin, your posture, your breath.

— Now exhale. And open your eyes, if you like. Look at me. You see the world a little differently now — through the lens of this confidence. Wiggle your fingers and toes. Feel your body strong and centered.

— Remember this state. Remember how you're sitting right now — back straight, breath even, gaze clear. This state can be called up at any moment. Just take a deep breath, remember that place in your chest where the golden light lives, and confidence will return.

— You were always capable of this. Hypnosis just helped you find the key to a door that was already open. Now you know the way.

— Let's lock it in. I will touch your shoulder one more time — the same gesture — and say "resource." And this word will become an anchor. When you need confidence, you will touch your shoulder, say "resource" to yourself, and take a deep breath. Try it right now. Touch your shoulder. "Resource." Breathe in. Can you feel it? There it is.

— Good. Now slowly return to your normal state. On the count of "three" — full awakening. One — you feel your body, light and filled. Two — thoughts become clear, calm. Three — eyes open. You're back. Rested, confident, filled with resource.

Homework for the client: Throughout the day, at the first signs of uncertainty, use the anchor gesture (shoulder touch + inhale + the word "resource"). The more you practice, the faster the anchor fires. After 7–10 days, the anchor becomes automatic.

💡 Tip

This protocol can be strengthened with the "resource stacking" method: after anchoring the first resource memory, return the subject to trance and ask the subconscious to find another moment of confidence, then merge both feelings into one. Three to four such layers create a state of "absolute confidence" that lasts for months. Works great before public speaking — do the session 24 hours before the event.

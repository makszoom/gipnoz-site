15. Stress and Anxiety Relief

💊 Therapeutic Scripts

🕯️ When to Use

This protocol combines deep relaxation with elements of age regression to find and neutralize the root cause of anxiety. Unlike simple relaxation, it works with the root of the problem, not just the symptoms.

When to apply: for chronic stress, generalized anxiety, panic attacks (only after consulting a physician!), stress-related insomnia, psychosomatic manifestations of anxiety (lump in the throat, neck tension, stomach pain).

Important: this script requires the hypnotist to have experience with regressive techniques. If the subject has severe psychological trauma — refer them to a qualified psychotherapist. Hypnosis is a tool, not a replacement for psychiatric care.

Script: Deep Relaxation with Anxiety Neutralization

— Get as comfortable as possible. Close your eyes. Place your hands on your knees or armrests — so they are completely relaxed. Don't cross your legs. Allow the floor to hold you, the chair to embrace you, the space to take care of you.

— Take a deep breath... and on the exhale, release everything you've been holding today. All the thoughts, all the worries, all the weight of the day. Exhale them. Right now. Let them go with the air.

— One more breath in — and on the exhale, I want you to feel how your head relaxes. Your forehead becomes smooth, even. Your eyes rest in their sockets. Your jaw drops — your teeth stop clenching. Your tongue rests softly on the floor of your mouth. Your whole face softens.

— Relaxation flows down your neck, taking over your shoulders. Your shoulders drop — by themselves, easily, without effort. As if someone took off a heavy backpack you've been carrying all day and didn't even notice.

— Warmth spreads through your chest. Your heart beats evenly, calmly, rhythmically. Each beat is like a wave washing away tension. Your breath becomes deep, slow, rhythmic. You hear your own breathing — and it's the only sound that matters.

— Now allow relaxation to move into your belly. Soft, warm, calm belly. All your internal organs relax, releasing tension that has built up over years. You might feel a gentle warmth in your solar plexus — this is the center of peace.

— Relaxation flows further — into your hips, into your legs. Heavy, warm legs. Down into your feet. Your whole body — like warm sand on a beach after a long day. Still, heavy, completely at peace.

— Now that your body is completely relaxed, I will ask your subconscious to go on a journey. A journey back in time — to the very first moment when you felt this anxiety. Don't relive it — just watch it like a scene in a theater, from the audience.

— You are sitting in a comfortable chair in a dark theater. On the stage — a scene from your past. That moment when anxiety first appeared. You see yourself — small, confused. You see the circumstances. You see what exactly became the trigger.

— And now, as an adult, wise person, you can give that younger self what they didn't have back then. Support. Understanding. Protection. You can step onto the stage, stand beside your past self, and say: "It's okay. I've got this. I'm here now, and I'll take care of you."

— Feel how that old anxiety begins to melt. It was a child's reaction to a situation the child couldn't control. But now you're an adult. You have resources, knowledge, strength. That situation is in the past. It no longer has power over you.

— Now imagine that anxiety is a dark cloud inside your chest. With each inhale, golden light — the resource of calm, adult strength — enters this cloud. With each exhale, the cloud becomes smaller, lighter, more transparent. Breathe in — the light arrives. Breathe out — the darkness leaves. Breathe in — breathe out... the cloud becomes small, bright, harmless.

— Now imagine that in place of this cloud, an anchor appears. A crystal clear, calm, warm sphere in the center of your chest. This is your new state — the state of peace. It's always with you. Any moment you feel anxiety approaching, you can take a deep breath, direct your attention to the center of your chest, and return to this calm.

— Your subconscious has locked in the new pattern. The anxiety trigger is now connected to the resource of calm. Once it activated anxiety — now it activates peace. The reprogramming has happened at a deep level.

— I will give you a simple signal — three slow claps. When you hear three slow claps after the session, your body will automatically take a deep breath, and a wave of calm will pass from the crown of your head to the soles of your feet. You can use this signal for yourself — just mentally say "one... two... three" and take three deep breaths, just like that.

— And now — rest. Enjoy this complete silence inside. There's nothing you need to do. Nowhere you need to go. There is only this moment — a moment of complete peace. Allow yourself to stay in it as long as you need.

— In a few minutes, I will bring you out of trance. You will return light, rested, as if you've slept a full night. All the heaviness that was in your body is gone. All the anxiety that was in your mind has dissolved. You're starting with a clean slate.

— Bringing you out. On the count of "four" — full awakening. One — your breath becomes more shallow, lighter. Two — you feel your fingers and toes, a slight tingling — energy is returning. Three — stretch your whole body, like after the best sleep of your life. Four — eyes open. You're back. Calm, fresh, filled with stillness.

After the session: Give the subject a glass of water. Don't start discussing right away — allow 2–3 minutes of silence. If the subject cried during the regressive part — that's normal, give them time. Ask what they remember, but don't insist on details. Schedule the next session in 5–7 days: for chronic anxiety, 4–6 sessions are usually required.

💡 Tip

When working with anxiety, it's important to distinguish between clinical anxiety (requires a psychiatrist) and situational stress (hypnotherapy is effective). For panic attacks, never use regression to trauma in the first session — start with 2–3 sessions of pure relaxation and resource anchoring, and only then, when the subject feels safe, move to regressive elements. At the first signs of strong emotional discomfort — immediately switch to resource work and bring the subject out of trance.

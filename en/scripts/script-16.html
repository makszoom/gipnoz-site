16. Somnambulism Stabilization

🌀 Module 2 — Trance Deepening

When to Use

Right after the person has entered somnambulism (e.g., after the Elman induction). Stabilization locks in the depth and prevents the conscious mind from "surfacing."

Also use during a session if you notice depth decreasing: the client fidgets, swallows, changes position.

⏱ Duration: 3–5 minutes. Speak calmly, with 3–5 second pauses between numbers.

📜 Full Stabilization Script

Step 1 — Countdown from 10 to 1 with Shoulder Press

I will now count from 10 to 1… and with each number, with each exhale, you will sink deeper and deeper…

[short pause, wait for the client's natural inhale — at the peak of the inhale, press on the shoulder]

10 — deep… [press on the shoulder at peak of inhale] and with the exhale — even deeper…

9 — deeper… [press] with each exhale, deeper…

8 — twice as deep… [press]…

7 — deeper and deeper… [press]…

6 — always deeper… [press]…

5 — halfway there… [press] and with each exhale — deeper…

4 — deep inside… [press]…

3 — very deep… [press]…

2 — almost at the very bottom… [press]…

1 — full submersion… [press] you are at the deepest depth…

[Pause 3–5 seconds, let the person "settle" at this depth]

And now, at this depth, you can hear me clearly… and every word I speak sinks you even deeper…

Step 2 — Four Stabilization Phrases

Phrase 1 — Noise isolation:

Any sounds around you don't disturb you at all, they only help you relax deeper.

Phrase 2 — Voice anchoring:

The sound of my voice sinks you deeper.

Phrase 3 — Depth-wellbeing link:

The deeper you go, the better you feel.

Phrase 4 — Positive loop:

And the better you feel, the deeper you go.

Step 3 — Natural Weaving (example)

Don't read all four phrases in a list. Weave them into natural speech. Here's an example:

You feel how the sound of my voice sinks you deeper… and the deeper you go, the better you feel… and the better you feel, the deeper… and any sounds around only help…

Step 4 — Positive Loop (reinforcement)

The deeper you relax
↓
the better you feel
↓
the better you feel
↓
the deeper you go

This loop makes deepening automatic. Once you've set the loop, you don't need to repeat suggestions — it works on its own.

💡 Important

  • Don't rush. Between numbers, pause 3–5 seconds so the subconscious has time to "digest" each suggestion.
  • Syncing with breath: each number is spoken on the exhale. Press on the shoulder at the peak of the inhale.
  • Don't use staircase or elevator metaphors. Counting from 10 to 1 works better than any metaphor because it doesn't require visualization.
  • If depth decreases — return to the count, but from 5 to 1, not from 10. That's usually enough.

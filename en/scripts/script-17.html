17. Pre-talk: Hypnotic Contract

🧠 Module 1 — Basic Skills

When to Use

Pre-talk is the conversation before the induction. It's conducted with every new client before the first session. Without pre-talk, hypnosis doesn't work: the client's subconscious doesn't know whether to trust the operator and puts up blocks.

This script is a set of ready-made speech modules. You can combine them depending on the situation.

⏱ Duration: 5–10 minutes. Speak calmly, confidently, without rushing.

📜 Pre-talk Script

1. Body Positioning (first 30 seconds)

Show the client how to sit. Don't command — suggest.

"Make yourself comfortable… place both feet flat on the floor, completely. Rest your hands on your knees or armrests — so they are not crossed. Keep your back straight but without tension. You can tilt your head slightly forward or rest it on the headrest."

"Good. When you're ready — just close your eyes and take a deep breath."

🧠 Observation: the client followed the request to change position — this is already the first compliance test.

2. Hypnotic Contract (key block)

"Now I'll explain how we'll work together. Hypnosis is not magic and not my power over you. It's cooperation. There's one important condition."

"I can help you if you follow my instructions. If you don't follow my instructions — I won't be able to help you."

"This means: I'm responsible for your state and safety. And you're responsible for doing what I say. Agreed?"

Wait for verbal or non-verbal agreement (nod, "yes").

3. Three Permissions

Get three explicit permissions before starting the induction. This is the foundation of compliance.

For first-name address: "Do you mind if I address you by your first name? In hypnosis, it helps relaxation and removes unnecessary barriers."

For touch: "During the process, I may lightly touch your hand, shoulder, or forehead — this enhances relaxation. Is that okay?"

For a signal: "If you feel uncomfortable or want to stop — just raise your hand or say 'stop.' That's your control. Agreed?"

🧠 Paradox: it's precisely the feeling of control that allows the client to relax more deeply.

4. Four Forbidden Words — Instructions to Self

This block you say mentally. Never say these words aloud in the client's presence.

🚫 "Under hypnosis" → say "in the hypnotic state," "in a state of relaxation."

🚫 "Wake up" → say "return to your normal state," "open your eyes."

🚫 "Try" → say "you will…," "this is happening…"

🚫 "Hope" → say "this will work," "this is happening right now."

Rule: words of doubt and uncertainty kill the trance. Words of certainty and specificity deepen it.

5. What Hypnosis Is (explanation to the client)

"Hypnosis is a state of deep focus and relaxation. You will hear everything I say. You can come out of this state at any moment if you want. But if you simply follow my voice — you will experience a very pleasant, deep relaxation."

"No loss of consciousness. No magic. Just your subconscious becoming more receptive to suggestions — those that are beneficial to you."

"Everything I say is for your benefit. Your subconscious will only accept suggestions that align with your values. It is your protector."

6. Hypnotic Voice — Calibration

Before starting the induction, calibrate your speech. This is not part of the dialogue with the client — it's your internal preparation.

• Tempo: slightly slower than usual, but natural.

• Pauses: 2–3 seconds between key phrases.

• Volume: a quiet voice makes the client listen more carefully, narrowing attention.

• Intonation: descending at the end of each suggestive phrase — works like a command.

• Congruence: if you say "relaxation" — your voice sounds relaxed.

🧠 Exercise: read this script aloud at half your normal speed. Record it. It should sound like a calm, confident voice.

7. Compliance Check (before induction)

Ask the client to do a simple action. If they do it without resistance — compliance is established.

"Please take a deep breath… and on the exhale, let your shoulders drop. Good."

"Now raise your right hand to shoulder level… and lower it. Excellent."

If the client performs without hesitation — you're ready for induction. If they ask "why?" or hesitate — return to the hypnotic contract.

8. Abreaction — Action Protocol

If during the session the client has an emotional outburst (crying, trembling, anxiety), follow this protocol. Don't panic — abreaction means the subconscious is actively working.

Step 1. Maintain a calm, steady tone. Your voice is the anchor of safety.

Step 2. Don't touch the client — unexpected touch can intensify the reaction.

Step 3. Use the phrase: "Everything you're feeling is simply disappearing. Focus on your breathing. Breathe in and out. With each exhale, it all goes away."

Repeat in an even tone until the emotion subsides. Abreaction is not a failure. After it, rapport becomes stronger and trance deeper.

9. Physiological Signs of Trance (cheat sheet)

During induction, observe these signs. They are objective and don't depend on the client's words.

• 🔴 Flushed cheeks — blood rush, a reliable sign of relaxation

• 👁️ Eyelash flutter — small twitches of closed eyelids

• 💧 Eye moistening — teariness without crying

• 🔥 Body heating — the client says they feel hot

• ↔️ Horizontal eye movement — REM-like activity under the eyelids

See these signs — your technique is working, deepen the state. Don't see them — return to the contract and compliance.

💡 Tip

Don't move to trance induction until you have compliance. Better to spend 10 minutes on pre-talk than to fail a session in the first minute. The hypnotic contract is the foundation. If the foundation is weak — the whole building will collapse.

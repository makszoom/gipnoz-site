18. Fractionation

🧠 Module 1 — Basic Skills

When to Use

Fractionation (from Latin fractio — splitting) is a technique for deepening trance through repeated cycles of emerging and re-entering. Applied after pre-talk and progressive relaxation, before the countdown from 100 with number amnesia.

Principle: each subsequent entry is deeper than the previous one. Three cycles are enough to achieve significant deepening.

⏱ Duration: 2–3 minutes. Speed of commands is a key success factor.

📜 Induction Script

Preparation — Position and Compliance Check

"You're already relaxed. Your body is resting. Now I'm going to do something that will deepen your relaxation several times over."

"Are your feet flat on the floor? Good. Are your hands resting freely on your knees? Excellent. Take a deep breath… and on the exhale, allow yourself to relax even more."

🧠 Important: before fractionation, make sure the client is already in a light state of relaxation (after body scan).

Warning (required)

"In a few seconds, I'll ask you to open and close your eyes. Don't be alarmed — it's part of the process. You'll simply open your eyes, see my palm, and close them again."

Hold your open palm 20–30 cm in front of the client's face, blocking their field of vision. They should see only your palm.

Cycle 1

"Open your eyes…" [0.5 sec pause]

"Close your eyes…" [0.5 sec pause]

"And relax twice as deep…"

Give 2–3 seconds for integration of the suggestion.

Cycle 2

"Again… open your eyes…"

"Close your eyes…"

"And relax twice as deep again… deeper than last time…"

Cycle 3

"And for the last time… open your eyes…"

"Close your eyes…"

"And drop into deep, complete relaxation… twice as deep as ever before…"

🧠 Key: there should be no pauses between commands. "Open" → reaction → "close" → reaction → "relax." You lead, you don't wait. Speed outpaces the analytical mind.

Arm Drop Test

"Now I'm going to take your hand…"

Take the client's hand by the wrist, lift it 5–10 cm above their knee or armrest.

"Your hand is like a heavy sack of sand… like a wet rag… completely heavy…"

"Don't help me in any way. Give me all the weight of your hand."

Release the hand. Observe: drops like a sack (deep relaxation) or lingers (helping, need to repeat suggestion).

If helping: "No, no — don't help. Give me all the weight of your hand. Let it fall by itself." Repeat.

"Excellent! Good job."

🧠 The "excellent" feedback is anchoring. You're reinforcing the pattern: "I do what the operator says → I get approval → I want to do more." Even if the person "failed" the hand drop — say "excellent."

Repeat Cycle (optional — for deepening)

If needed, repeat the full cycle: fractionation (3× open-close) → arm drop test. Up to 3 full cycles total.

"Now we'll do it again… even deeper…"

"In a few seconds, I'll ask you to open and close your eyes again…"

"Open… close… relax twice as deep…"

"Open… close… even deeper…"

"Open… close… and drop to the deepest level…"

"I'll take your hand again… lift it… don't help… give me the weight…"

Release. Say "excellent."

Transition to the Next Stage

"You are in deep relaxation. Your body is resting. Now I will ask you to direct your attention inward…"

"Deep breath in… and on the exhale — even deeper…"

After the arm drop test, you can proceed to the reverse count from 100 with number amnesia (script-19) or directly to deepening with the 10→1 count.

💡 Tip

Speed is the key to success. Between "open" and "close" — half a second. Between "close" and "relax" — another half second. If you pause, the conscious mind will "turn on" and start analyzing. Your rhythm: command → reaction → next command. You don't wait — you lead.

Keep your hand in front of the client's face when they open their eyes — this blocks their field of vision and prevents distraction.

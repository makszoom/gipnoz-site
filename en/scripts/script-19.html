19. Arm Drop + Number Amnesia

🧠 Module 1 — Basic Skills

When to Use

After physical relaxation (body scan) and fractionation comes the key moment — the transition to mental relaxation. As long as the conscious mind is actively counting, the internal dialogue continues. The task of this technique is to "turn off" the internal dialogue through number amnesia.

When numbers disappear — the conscious mind releases control, and access to the subconscious opens. This is the threshold of somnambulism.

⏱ Duration: 3–5 minutes. Speak slowly, with pauses between numbers.

📜 Induction Script

Transition from Physical to Mental Relaxation

"Your body is completely relaxed. Every muscle is resting. But your mind… your mind is still active. Inside you, a conversation is going on — thoughts, evaluations, comments."

"Now we'll let go of the mind too. Allow thoughts to dissolve. And the tool for this — numbers."

"I'll ask you to begin counting… slowly and gently… backwards from one hundred."

"You'll say the number, and between numbers, add the words 'deeper relaxation.' Listen to my voice — I'll guide you."

🧠 Pauses between numbers are a key element. They prevent the conscious mind from mechanically listing and give time for the suggestion to "settle" in the subconscious.

Reverse Count from 100 with Relaxation Suggestions

"Begin… 100… deeper and deeper…"

"99… deeper relaxation…"

"98… with each number, deeper…"

"97… complete, deep relaxation…"

"96… sink deeper…"

"95… each exhale carries you deeper…"

"94… all thoughts begin to disappear…"

"93… deeper and deeper…"

"92… deeper relaxation…"

"91… you're sinking into a state of peace…"

After 5–10 numbers, start weaving in the amnesia suggestion.

Number Amnesia Suggestion

"90… and notice how the numbers start to disappear…"

"89… some numbers simply vanish…"

"88… they dissolve, melt, disappear…"

"87… you can no longer remember what number came before…"

"86… numbers disappear faster and faster…"

"85… they simply cease to exist…"

"84… in a few numbers, they will all disappear… vanish… dissolve…"

"83… and only silence will remain…"

"82… only emptiness… only peace…"

"81… numbers are fading… fewer and fewer numbers…"

"80… they're gone… only silence…"

Here you fall silent. If the person continues calling out numbers — continue the suggestion. If they've fallen silent — pause for 5–10 seconds.

⚠️ Never say "all numbers are gone." The word "numbers" in affirmative form brings them back. Say: "they're gone," "only silence remains."

Hidden Somnambulism Test

After the pause (silence), get confirmation. Speak calmly, without changing tone.

"Are they all gone?.."

Wait for a response: nod, "yes," or silence. Any form of confirmation — test passed.

"Good. You are in deep hypnosis."

Option: Arm Drop + Forehead Tap (for shortened version)

Use this option when number amnesia has already been practiced and the person is familiar with the feeling of numbers disappearing (second and subsequent sessions).

"Now I'll take your hand… lift it up…"

Take the hand by the wrist, lift above the knee.

"And when I let go — it will drop. And in the moment it falls, the light will go out and all the numbers will disappear."

Release the hand. As soon as it falls — immediately tap the forehead with your palm. The tap should be loud and unexpected, but not frightening.

"TAP! Everything disappears… disappears… everything is gone."

"Silence… Emptiness…"

"Are they all gone?.. You are in deep hypnosis."

🧠 Warn the person beforehand: "I will tap, it's part of the process." The tap "resets" the remnants of conscious control.

If Numbers Don't Disappear — The Talk

If the person keeps counting despite the suggestion — they've taken back control. Use this sequence.

Step 1 — repeat suggestion: "Numbers are disappearing faster and faster. They simply cease to exist. With every moment, fewer remain."

Step 2 — physical intervention: Lift your hand, tap the forehead: "They all vanish!"

Step 3 — The Talk: "Open your eyes for a second. You're counting consciously. You're controlling the process. Release control. Allow your subconscious to lead you. Close your eyes and let's try again."

If it didn't work — return to fractionation (script-18), run another cycle and try again.

💡 Tip

Don't aim for the numbers to disappear on the first try. For many people, this is an unfamiliar experience. Your task is not to "make the numbers disappear" but to create conditions where the subconscious itself chooses to let go of the counting. Patience and calm are your main tools.

If the numbers disappeared — the somnambulism threshold has been crossed. The person is ready for tests: eyelid catalepsy and analgesia (script-20).

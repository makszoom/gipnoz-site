Basic Catalepsy For beginners

2. Hand Catalepsy

🧘 Basic scripts

When to Use

Hand catalepsy is one of the most elegant and reliable inductions in classic hypnosis. It uses a physiological phenomenon: in trance, muscles can become rigid, "wooden," holding a pose without effort.

This script works well when:

  • You want to clearly demonstrate that the person is in trance — the arm doesn't drop
  • You need fast and effective deepening of the state
  • The subject trusts you and is open to physical contact
  • You are practicing hypnosis phenomena — catalepsy opens the door to other phenomena

⏱ Duration: 10–15 minutes. Before starting, get permission to touch the arm and shoulder.

📜 Induction Script

Preparation and Introduction

Make yourself comfortable. Place your hands on your knees, palms up. Put your feet on the floor. Take a deep breath... and as you exhale, close your eyes. Good.

Now I'm going to work with your right arm. You will feel my touch, and that's normal. Just allow yourself to relax with every word I speak.

Take another deep breath... and as you exhale, let go of everything unnecessary. All thoughts. All worries. Just you and my voice.

Initial Relaxation

Begin to relax your body. Feel your face — relax your forehead, eyes, jaw. Let your face become soft and calm.

Relax your neck and shoulders. Allow your shoulders to drop down and back. With each exhale, you sink deeper into a state of peace.

I will count from five to one. With each number, you will relax twice as deeply. Five... deep relaxation begins in your face and neck. Four... relaxation moves down into your shoulders and arms. Three... warmth spreads through your chest and abdomen. Two... relaxation flows down your legs. One... your whole body is relaxed, you are ready.

Arm Lift

Now I am going to take your right hand by the wrist. Don't open your eyes. Just feel.

I gently lift your arm up... higher... higher... until it is in front of you, parallel to the floor. Elbow straight, but not locked. Good.

Notice how your arm already begins to feel different. It feels lighter than it should. Or perhaps heavier. Some unusual sensation.

Suggestion of Rigidity

Your arm begins to change. Your fingers start to go numb... a slight tingling in your fingertips... that's normal. It's a sign that the subconscious is taking control of the arm.

Now I will remove my hand, and your arm will stay suspended in the air. It won't come down. It will become frozen, like wood, like stone. You won't be able to lower it, even if you wanted to. But you won't want to.

I'm letting go of your arm. And it stays in place. It doesn't fall. Feel this strangeness — your arm is hanging in the air by itself. It's as if it has turned to stone.

Your arm is becoming hard, like a steel rod. Try to bend it — it won't work. It's rigid. Try to lower it — it won't work. It's frozen. Your arm is in catalepsy.

Notice how amazing this is: you're not moving your arm, but it doesn't fall. It is obeying something else, not your conscious commands. It is obeying the subconscious.

Deepening Through the Arm

And now this strange, unusual feeling in your arm — it begins to spread. From your arm it flows into your shoulder... into your neck... into your head. Your whole body becomes deeper, calmer.

As if the rigidity in your arm is a key that opens the door to a deeper trance. And with every second, the door opens wider, and you sink deeper.

This state can only happen in deep hypnosis. And since it has happened — it means you are already there. You are in a deep trance. Enjoy this state.

Lowering the Arm with Deepening

Now I will take your wrist again. And as I begin to lower it — every centimeter it descends will take you twice as deep.

I lower your arm... a little... and you sink deeper... Lowering it further... and even deeper... Your arm slowly returns to your knee... and when it touches your leg — you fall into the deepest, most peaceful state imaginable.

Your arm is on your knee. You are in a deep trance. You are here, you hear me, and you are completely safe.

Now I will count from one to three, and on the count of three you will open your eyes, feeling rested and renewed. One... slowly return... two... feel your body, your fingers and toes... three — eyes open, you are fully here, refreshed and alert.

💡 Tip

Hand catalepsy is an excellent test for trance depth. If after the suggestion you release the arm and it stays suspended — the person is in hypnosis. If the arm drops immediately — pause, repeat the relaxation, and try again. To strengthen the effect, ask the subject to try to lower their arm and "discover" that it won't obey. This breaks down any remaining doubts.

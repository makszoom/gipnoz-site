Catalepsy Analgesia Testing

20. Eyelid Catalepsy and Analgesia

🧠 Module 1 — Basic Skills

When to Use

These tests are performed after the numbers have disappeared — the person has crossed the somnambulism threshold. You know they're in deep trance, but they don't realize it yet. Tests solve two tasks:

  • Demonstration to the client — show that hypnosis really works
  • Confirmation for the operator — objective sign of trance depth

⏱ Duration: 5–7 minutes for both tests + emergence.

📜 Induction Script

Somnambulism Confirmation

"Are the numbers gone?.. Good."

"You are in deep hypnosis. Your subconscious is open right now. You can hear my voice, but everything happening around you is just sounds that don't disturb you."

"Now I'll show you how powerfully your subconscious controls your body. You'll be surprised."

🧠 Key idea: eyelid catalepsy is a light trance test that you give to someone already in deep trance (somnambulism). It MUST work.

Eyelid Catalepsy — Paradoxical Suggestion

"Notice the contact of your closed eyelids… they begin to flutter slightly… and tighten…"

"I will count from 5 to 1, and with each number, your eyelids will squeeze tighter… and tighter…"

Touch the forehead (or bridge of the nose) with your index finger — tactile reinforcement.

"5… they're tightening…"

"4… tightening more and more…"

"3… as if glued shut with superglue…"

"2… your eyelids are sealed shut…"

"1… your eyes are completely closed, and you cannot open them…"

"Want this to happen… allow it to happen…"

"Now try to open them…"

Say the command "try to open them" quickly and confidently, without pause. The conscious mind must not have time to seize control.

"…but they only squeeze tighter… the more you try, the tighter they're closed…"

"When you're convinced you can't open them — say: I am convinced."

Wait for "I am convinced" or a nod. Observe the eyes: lashes flutter, eyelids vibrate, but the eye slit doesn't open. If the slit opens even a little — the suggestion is incomplete.

"Good. Stop trying. Just allow them to stay closed… and relax even deeper…"

🧠 Core phrase to remember: "The harder you try to open them, the tighter they squeeze shut." This is paradoxical suggestion — conscious effort strengthens the subconscious suggestion.

Analgesia Test (pain suppression)

"You are in a deep state. Now I'll check how much your subconscious controls the sensations in your body."

"In this state, nothing I do will cause you the slightest discomfort… You will feel nothing but touch…"

"Your subconscious has complete control over all sensations in your body. And it can switch off any signals of discomfort."

"I'm going to gently pinch the skin on your arm — and you will feel nothing but a light touch… Just a soft pressure…"

Pinch the skin on the back of the wrist between your thumb and index finger. Squeeze with pads of fingers (not nails!), with noticeable but not harmful pressure.

"You only feel a light touch… just soft pressure… no discomfort…"

"When I move my hand away — the touch will disappear, and you'll relax even deeper…"

Remove your hand. Observe the reaction:
✅ No reaction — analgesia accepted, suggestion working.
⚠️ Micro-reaction (slight change in expression) — partially accepted.
❌ Pulls away, winces — not accepted. Don't insist.

Emergence from Trance (count 1 to 3)

"Now I'll bring you out of hypnosis. You will feel rested and full of energy."

"I will count from 1 to 3. On the count of 3, but not before, open your eyes and notice how good you feel."

"1… begin to return… become aware of your body…"

Pause 3–5 seconds.

"2… feel the room, the sounds around you…"

Pause 3–5 seconds.

"3 — open your eyes… deep breath in… how do you feel?"

⚠️ Never say "wake up" or "snap out of it" — those are medical terms. The phrase "but not before" gives the subconscious time for a smooth emergence.

Post-Hypnotic Window (30–90 seconds)

After emerging from trance, the person remains highly suggestible for 30–90 seconds. Use this window for positive suggestions.

"Notice how pleasant the feeling of deep relaxation is…"

"With each time, it will be easier and easier for you to enter this state…"

"Your subconscious continues to work, even with your eyes open…"

Say this after the person has opened their eyes, but before they start actively talking and moving. Once they start speaking or moving — the window has closed.

💡 Tip

For many people, the first eyelid catalepsy is a shock in the best sense. They are genuinely surprised they can't open their eyes. Don't ruin that surprise with unnecessary commentary. Just say: "This is how hypnosis works. Your subconscious is more powerful than you thought."

If analgesia didn't work — don't be discouraged. It means trance depth is insufficient. Return to deepening in the next session. Analgesia is an advanced test.

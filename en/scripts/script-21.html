21. Shortened Induction Versions

🧠 Module 1 — Basic Skills

When to Use

The full Elman induction (lessons 1.1–1.5) takes 15–20 minutes. It's an excellent protocol for the first session, but for repeat sessions it's excessive. Once a person has gone through the full induction at least once, their subconscious knows the way into somnambulism.

Shortened versions are the same steps, but without the "warm-up." You skip stages that are no longer needed. The person enters trance faster, and quality doesn't suffer.

This script contains: two shortened versions + deepening technique with 10→1 count.

⏱ Version 1: ~10 minutes. Version 2: ~5 minutes.

📜 Shortened Version 1 — Count from 100 Skipped

When: Second session. The subconscious already knows what "numbers disappearing" means and doesn't need a long count.

— Pre-talk (shortened)

"You've been in hypnosis before. Your subconscious knows the way. Sit comfortably, close your eyes, take a deep breath…"

— Quick Body Scan

"Feet relaxing… calves relaxing… thighs… belly… chest… shoulders… neck… face… Your whole body is relaxed…"

"With each exhale, you sink deeper…"

— Fractionation (once instead of three times)

"Now I'll ask you to open your eyes… and close them."

"Open…" [hand in front of face]

"Close…"

"Eyes closed, and you relax three times deeper…"

— Arm Drop with Amnesia + Tap

"I'll take your hand… lift it…"

"And when I let go — it will drop. And in the moment it falls, the light will go out and all the numbers will disappear."

Release the hand — it falls — and immediately tap the forehead.

"TAP! Everything disappears… disappears… everything is gone…"

"Silence… Emptiness…"

— Check

"Are they all gone?.."

"You are in deep hypnosis…"

— Eyelid Catalepsy (if needed)

"Notice your eyelids… they're tightening… 5… 4… 3… 2… 1…"

"Try to open them… but they only squeeze tighter… When you're convinced — say: I am convinced."

📜 Shortened Version 2 — Without Fractionation

When: Third session and beyond, or when the person "deflates like a balloon" — deep relaxation already at body scan. Fractionation is skipped because the person has learned to drop into trance without pattern interruption.

— Pre-talk (minimal)

"You know this process. Just close your eyes and allow yourself to relax…"

— Body Scan (very quick, 30–60 seconds)

"Feet… legs… torso… arms… shoulders… neck… face… Your whole body is completely relaxed…"

"Breathing is deep and even… with each exhale, you sink deeper…"

— Arm Drop with Amnesia (without fractionation)

"I'll take your hand… lift it…"

"And when I let go — you will drop into deep somnambulism. All numbers will disappear, all thoughts will dissolve."

Release the hand — tap the forehead.

"Everything is gone! Emptiness. Silence. You are in deep hypnosis."

— Check: Eyelid Catalepsy

"Try to open your eyes… they're sealed shut… when you're convinced — say: I am convinced."

— Emergence (count 1–3)

"1… begin to return…"

"2… feel the room, the sounds…"

"3… open your eyes… deep breath in…"

📜 Deepening with 10→1 Count and Shoulder Press

This deepening technique works with any version of induction. It stabilizes trance and makes it resistant to external interference.

Performed after entering somnambulism (after number amnesia or arm drop + tap).

Preparation

"Now I will count from 10 to 1. With each number, you will sink deeper."

"I'll place my hand on your shoulder. At the peak of your inhale, I'll press — and this signal will take you deeper."

Place your hand on the client's shoulder. Wait for an inhale — press at the peak.

The Count

"10… deeper…" [shoulder press at peak of inhale]

"9… each exhale sinks you deeper…" [press]

"8… any sounds sink you deeper…" [press]

"7… the sound of my voice sinks you deeper…" [press]

"6… the deeper you go, the better you feel…" [press]

"5… deeper and deeper…" [press]

"4… each moment — even deeper…" [press]

"3… deep, calm trance…" [press]

"2… every bit of relaxation doubles…" [press]

"1… full somnambulism… silence… emptiness…"

Key Amplifier Phrases

These phrases can be inserted between numbers or used separately to maintain depth.

🧠 "Each exhale sinks you deeper" — linking to the natural rhythm of breathing.

🧠 "Any sounds sink you deeper" — protection against noise: any external sound becomes a deepening trigger.

🧠 "The sound of my voice sinks you deeper" — anchoring to the voice, strengthening rapport.

🧠 "The deeper you go, the better you feel" — positive reinforcement, removes fear of depth.

💡 Tip

Why NOT to use staircase or elevator metaphors: the person may have claustrophobia or a fear of heights that you don't know about. Moreover, visualizing a staircase requires active imagination work, which distracts from trance. Counting from 10 to 1 with a shoulder press works directly, without intermediaries. The more direct the suggestion — the more effective it is.

Rule: If after the shortened version the person doesn't enter deep enough trance — return to the full version for one session. Shortening is a privilege that must be earned.

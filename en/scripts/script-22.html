Fractionation Test Checking

22. Hidden Fractionation Test

🌀 Module 2 — Trance Deepening

When to Use

After induction and stabilization, when you need to check whether the person is truly in somnambulism or just deeply relaxed. The test is embedded into the process invisibly for the client.

Also use mid-session if depth decreases — a quick 1-2-3 cycle will also check how deep the person returned after fractionation.

Suitable for "opaque" clients who don't show external signs of trance.

⏱ Duration: 1–2 minutes for the test, 3–5 minutes for recovery if it fails.

📜 Full Fractionation Test Script

Step 1 — Stabilization Before the Test

Start only after the person has gone through basic stabilization (10→1 count with shoulder press + stabilization phrases). Make sure your voice sounds confident, rhythm calm.

Transition script:

You are very deep now… at the very deepest depth… and I want to check how deep you can go…

Now I will count to three… and on the count of "three," you will open your eyes…

1 — deeper… [pause 2–3 seconds]

2 — even deeper… [pause 2–3 seconds, observe the eyelids]

3 — open your eyes!

[Observe: eyes opened only at "3" — test passed. Eyelids twitched or opened slightly at "1" or "2" — test not passed.]

Step 2 — If the Test is PASSED (eyes opened only at "3")

Script:

Good… you see how deep you can go…

Close your eyes again… and sink even deeper… twice as deep as just a moment ago…

Now I will count from 3 to 1, and with each number, you will go deeper…

3 — deeper… 2 — even deeper… 1 — full submersion…

And now, at this depth, we will work…

[Proceed to suggestion or main work. The client has confirmed depth — no need to return to the test.]

Step 3 — If the Test is NOT PASSED (eyes opened earlier)

Script for gentle correction:

Good… close your eyes… and just listen to my voice…

Now we'll do the same thing, but without opening your eyes. Just deepening.

1 — deeper… 2 — even deeper… 3 — deeper…

And again: 1 — deeper… 2 — twice as deep… 3 — the deepest depth…

And once more: 1 — deeper… 2 — deeper… 3 — full submersion…

[Do 3–4 fractionation cycles without opening the eyes. Then repeat the test.]

Test repeat (maximum once):

Now I will count to three again… and on the count of "three," you will open your eyes…

1 — deeper… 2 — even deeper… 3 — open your eyes!

▸ If passed — good, close your eyes, continue working.

▸ If not passed again — move to recovery stabilization (Step 4).

Step 4 — Recovery Stabilization (if test failed twice)

If the test fails twice in a row — don't repeat it again. This will cause irritation and damage rapport. Instead:

Return to stabilization script:

It's okay. Close your eyes and just breathe…

Now I will count from 5 to 1, and with each number, you will go deeper…

5 — deep… [press on shoulder at peak of inhale]

4 — deeper… [press]

3 — very deep… [press]

2 — almost at the deepest level… [press]

1 — full submersion… [press]

And now, at this depth… any sounds around only help you relax deeper… the sound of my voice sinks you deeper… the deeper you go, the better you feel… and the better you feel, the deeper you go…

[Repeat stabilization phrases 2–3 times. After this, if needed, conduct The Talk (script 23) — there may be conscious resistance.]

📜 Fractionation as a Deepening Tool (without test)

If you don't want to test, but simply deepen — use the same 1-2-3 count, but without the command to open eyes. Just a deepening cycle:

1 — deeper… and with each exhale, deeper…

2 — even deeper, twice as deep…

3 — full submersion… and again…

1 — deeper… don't think about anything, just listen to my voice…

2 — even deeper… every word I speak sinks you deeper…

3 — the deepest depth…

[Repeat 3–5 cycles. With each cycle, depth increases.]

💡 Important

  • No more than one test per session. If you test every 30 seconds, the client will start expecting the test — the conscious mind will engage.
  • Watch the eyelids. Twitching or opening slightly at "1" or "2" — a sign that the conscious mind is anticipating the command. This signals insufficient depth.
  • Don't show disappointment. If the test fails — your calmness is paramount. "Great, let's try again" is the only correct response.
  • The number amnesia test is more reliable. If in doubt — use the numbers test from the Elman protocol. It is less intrusive.
  • Always deepen after a test. Even if the test passed, give the command "close your eyes and sink even deeper" — this reinforces the result.

23. The Talk: Working with Resistance

🌀 Module 2 — Deepening Trance

When to Use

Use The Talk when the client consciously refuses to follow instructions: opens their eyes before commanded, helps lift their arm instead of allowing it, comments on the process, analyzes aloud, fails to let go of numbers when counting.

Do not confuse this with situations where the client simply didn't understand the instruction (rephrase it instead) or when they are nervous/afraid (pre-talk is needed, not The Talk).

⏱ Duration: 1–3 minutes. After The Talk — resume the session.

📜 Complete The Talk Script

Step 1 — Stop the Session

When you notice resistance, do not try to push through it. Stop. In a firm but calm voice:

Open your eyes. Look at me.

[Pause. Establish direct eye contact. Speak calmly, firmly, without aggression. You are a professional stating facts.]

Step 2 — The Main Part (speak slowly, with pauses)

I can help you. But for that, you need to follow my instructions.

Think about everything you've done before, and how well it worked. It didn't work. Otherwise we wouldn't be here today.

I believe I can help you. I know how this works. But for that, you need to follow my instructions.

Decide.

[The rule of silence: after "Decide" — stay silent. Whoever speaks first loses. The client must make a decision. Give them silence. Do not fill the pause.]

Step 3 — After Client Agreement

If the client nods or says "yes":

Good. Close your eyes. Take a deep breath… and with your exhale, allow yourself to drift into trance…

I will count from 5 down to 1, and with each number you will sink deeper…

5 — deep… 4 — deeper… 3 — very deep… 2 — almost at the deepest level… 1 — complete immersion…

And now you hear only my voice… and every word I speak sends you deeper…

[Continue with stabilisation phrases, then proceed to suggestion.]

Step 4 — If the Client Does Not Agree or Hesitates

If the client is not ready to continue — do not push. End the session professionally:

I understand. Perhaps today is not your day. We can try another time, when you're ready to fully trust the process.

Note what specifically caused the resistance. It might be fear, distrust, or misunderstanding of hypnosis. At the next session, start with a pre-talk that addresses these doubts.

📜 Micro-Script "My Way or Highway" (short version in trance)

If you don't want to bring the client out of trance, use the short version directly in the state — it's gentler but still regains control:

You're here so I can help you. And for that, you need to do what I say.

If you want to do it your way — you've already done that. And it didn't work.

So now — it's my turn.

Just relax and listen to my voice. I'll handle everything else.

[After this phrase, pause for 5–7 seconds, then continue with stabilisation or deepening. Do not comment — just lead forward.]

📜 Rebuilding Rapport After The Talk

After The Talk, it's important to rebuild trust. The client may feel awkward after being "put in their place." Use this script for a gentle return:

I appreciate that you're willing to try something new. That takes courage.

And now, as you allow yourself to simply listen… you'll notice that entering trance becomes easier…

Because you stop controlling… and allow it to happen…

The deeper you relax, the better you feel… and the better you feel, the deeper…

[Transition smoothly into stabilisation phrases and then to suggestion.]

📜 Differential Diagnosis: The Talk or Correction?

Not every resistance requires The Talk. Here is a quick reference:

🔹 Person simply didn't understand — rephrase the instruction. Say it differently, use other words. "Close your eyes when I count to three, not before."

🔹 Person is "helping" — gentle correction directly in trance: "You don't need to help. Just let it happen. The best help is to relax and listen to my voice."

🔹 Person is nervous or afraid — not The Talk, but pre-talk. Remove fear, explain safety, give control.

🔹 Person consciously refuses to follow — does it their own way, ignores your words, comments and analyzes — use The Talk.

Remember: The Talk is for combating resistance. Metaphors and rephrasing are for working with trauma. Differentiate between these situations.

💡 Important

  • Tone of voice is everything. The Talk should sound calm, firm, confident — not angry, not irritated. You are a professional simply stating facts.
  • The rule of silence. After "Decide" — stay silent. Whoever speaks first loses. The pause may last 15–30 seconds. Endure it.
  • Do not apologize. The Talk is not punishment, it's restoring structure. Apologies destroy authority.
  • After The Talk — a new beginning. Do not go back to the moment of resistance. Do not remind them. Just continue the session.
  • If resistance is caused by trauma — do not use The Talk. Use impersonal suggestions, metaphors, give the client the illusion of control.

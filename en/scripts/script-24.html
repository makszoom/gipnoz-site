24. Constructing Direct Suggestions

🌀 Module 2 — Deepening Trance

When to Use

After the person is stabilized in somnambulism (or deep trance), before exiting the session. Direct suggestions are the primary tool for therapeutic work in hypnosis.

Always build suggestions using the client's own words from the preliminary diagnosis. Internet scripts with someone else's words don't work — they don't resonate with that specific person's subconscious.

⏱ Duration: 10–15 minutes. Speak slowly, with pauses between sentences.

📜 4 Language Patterns for Direct Suggestions

1. Presupposition of Awareness

You assume that what you're talking about is already happening. The subconscious accepts this as fact.

"You will notice how calmness spreads through your body…"

"You will see how much easier it has become to breathe…"

"You will realize that confidence is already here…"

2. Presupposition of Degree

You don't ask "will it happen or not" — you ask "to what extent."

"How much more confident do you feel?"

"Notice how much easier it is to take this calmly…"

"Notice how much deeper your trance has become…"

3. The "The More… The More…" Connector

Creates a cause-and-effect relationship where one thing amplifies another.

"The deeper you relax, the more confident your inner voice becomes…"

"The more you allow, the faster the changes happen…"

"The calmer your body, the clearer your mind…"

4. "Since / As If"

Provides a logical justification or creates a metaphor.

"Since your subconscious is open to change, each breath brings new calmness…"

"As if each day you wake up more and more confident…"

"As if you have already achieved what you want, and you feel that joy…"

📜 The Law of Compounding (How to Connect Suggestions)

Each subsequent suggestion amplifies the previous one. Don't speak isolated phrases — build a chain.

Example chain:

"You have already noticed a sense of calm in your body… [presupposition of awareness]

And now notice how much deeper that calm has become compared to a moment ago… [presupposition of degree + compounding]

The deeper it becomes, the more confident you feel… [the more… the more…]

Since your subconscious has already chosen confidence, every action you take becomes steady and conscious… [since]

As if you are already living this new reality — and it feels natural for you…" [as if]

Each phrase refers back to the previous one, amplifying the overall effect.

📜 Template Scripts for Common Requests

Template: Confidence (for interviews, presentations, social situations)

And now, in this deep, calm state, you begin to notice… how a new confidence emerges within you…

The more deeply relaxed you are, the more confident your inner voice becomes…

Notice how much calmer and clearer your speech becomes…

Since your subconscious has already accepted this confidence, every word you speak sounds steady and clear…

As if you are already where you want to be — and you feel absolutely at ease…

And with each breath, this confidence grows even stronger… even deeper… even more natural…

Template: Peaceful Sleep and Recovery

When night comes, your body naturally relaxes…

Notice how easily and deeply you drift into sleep…

The deeper you sleep, the more fully your strength is restored…

Since your subconscious takes care of your rest, each sleep cycle brings deep recovery…

As if you wake up in the morning fresh, full of energy and ready for a new day…

And with each night, this sleep becomes deeper… calmer… more restorative…

Template: Calm in Stressful Situations

In any situation that used to cause tension, you now notice calmness…

The more tense the situation around you becomes, the deeper your inner calm grows…

Notice how steady and deep your breathing becomes…

Since your subconscious has chosen calmness, each exhale releases all tension…

As if there is a source of stillness inside you, always with you…

And with each day, this source grows stronger… calmness becomes your natural response…

📜 The Rule of Positive Imagery

The subconscious does not understand negatives. If you say "don't be anxious" — the subconscious hears "be anxious." It moves toward images, not away from them.

❌ Negative wording (avoid):

"You won't be afraid" → subconscious creates fear

"Stop being nervous" → amplifies nervousness

"You won't wake up at night anymore" → subconscious fixates on waking up

✅ Positive wording (use):

"You feel calm and confident" → subconscious creates calmness

"You relax deeper and deeper" → amplifies relaxation

"You sleep deeply and peacefully all night" → subconscious creates deep sleep

Always formulate the suggestion as something that appears, not something that disappears.

💡 Important

  • Use the client's words. If they said "confidence" — use "confidence." If "ease" — "ease." Someone else's words don't resonate.
  • Build chains, not single suggestions. One suggestion is good. Five connected ones is a breakthrough. Ten is transformation.
  • Don't rush. Between each suggestion, pause 3–5 seconds to let the subconscious "digest" the information.
  • The law of compounding works both ways. If you start doubting your own voice, each subsequent suggestion will be weaker than the last. Be confident.
  • Don't try to solve everything in one session. 10–15 minutes of suggestions is optimal. More — the subconscious becomes overloaded.

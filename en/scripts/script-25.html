25. Post-Hypnotic Window and Session Structure

🌀 Module 2 — Deepening Trance

When to Use

This script is the closing part of every hypnosis session. Use it after delivering suggestions (script 24). The Post-Hypnotic Window (PHW) is the 30–90 seconds after exiting trance when the subconscious is still open to suggestions.

The session structure (diagnosis, pre-talk, induction, tests, suggestions, exit, PHW) is universal for any work with a client.

⏱ Duration: 2 minutes for exit + PHW. Full session: 25–30 minutes.

📜 Full Session Structure (7 Steps)

Step 1 — Diagnosis (Information Gathering)

Three questions from lesson 2.4. Write down the client's words — you will use them in suggestions.

  • "What do you want?" — only positive wording, without the word "not."
  • "How will you know when you have it?" — criteria for achievement.
  • "Why do you want it?" — deep motivation.

Step 2 — Pre-talk (Dispelling Fears and Myths)

Explain that hypnosis is safe, that the person is always in control, that it is a natural state. Dispel myths. Convince them that hypnosis is not loss of consciousness but hyper-focus.

⏱ Diagnosis + pre-talk: ~5 minutes.

Step 3 — Full Induction (Elman)

From progressive relaxation to deep trance. Elman protocol (Module 1) + count 10→1 with shoulder press.

Step 4 — Eyelid Catalepsy and Tests

Eyelids glued → hand catalepsy → hand levitation. Confirm the person is in trance. If needed — fractionation test (script 22).

⏱ Induction + tests: ~10 minutes.

Step 5 — Positive Suggestions (10–15 minutes)

Built on the client's words, using the 4 language patterns and the law of compounding (script 24).

⏱ Suggestions: ~10 minutes.

Step 6 — Exit from Trance

Calm, smooth exit. Script below.

Step 7 — Post-Hypnotic Window (30–90 seconds)

After exit, keep talking. The subconscious is still open. Script below.

⏱ Exit + PHW: ~2 minutes.

📜 Full Exit from Trance Script

Transition to Exit

Now I will begin to bring you out of this state… I will count from 1 to 3… and on the count of "3" you will open your eyes…

You will return completely… rested… refreshed… full of energy…

Everything we did today will stay with you… your subconscious will continue its work…

Exit Count

1 — slowly return… feel your body… feel the energy coming back to every cell…

2 — begin to wiggle your fingers and toes… take a deep breath… feel refreshment filling you…

3 — eyes open! You're back. Fully here. Notice how good you feel.

[The person opens their eyes. Do not stop talking — the PHW is open.]

📜 Post-Hypnotic Window (PHW) — What to Say After Exit

In the first 30–90 seconds after opening the eyes, the subconscious is still open. The critical factor closes gradually. Use this time for reinforcement.

Four directions for PHW suggestions:

1. Feeling good:

"Notice how good you feel… fresh… rested… full of energy…"

2. Positive attitude toward hypnosis:

"You enjoyed this state… and next time, entering it will be even easier… even deeper…"

3. Reinforcing results:

"What we did today stays with you… your subconscious continues its work… changes have already begun…"

4. Anticipation of the next session:

"You look forward to the next session… because you know — each time it gets deeper… more effective…"

[Speak calmly, without rushing. As soon as the person starts talking, looking around, analyzing aloud — the window has closed. You can wrap up.]

📜 Full Exit + PHW Script (Single Block)

Now I will bring you out of this state…

1 — slowly return… everything we did stays with you forever…

2 — begin to move… deep breath…

3 — eyes open. You're back.

[PHW — keep talking until the client speaks first]

Notice how good you feel… fresh and rested…

You enjoyed this state… and next time it will be even deeper…

What we did today is already working within you…

Your subconscious continues to process the changes…

With each day, it gets easier… calmer…

You look forward to the next session… because you know — it works…

[Pause. If the client is silent, you can add:]

Take your time… just sit quietly… feel your body… when you're ready, you can open your eyes if they're still closed…

[As soon as the client starts talking or actively moving — the window has closed. Wrap up the session.]

📜 Preparing for the Next Session (Regression)

Regression (returning to a traumatic event) is a powerful tool but requires preparation. Do not start regression on the first session.

Script for ending the first session with a regression announcement:

Today we did important work… your subconscious has already begun the changes…

At the next session, we will go even deeper… to find what lies at the root… and let it go forever…

You're ready for this… and when the time comes, your subconscious will show you exactly what you need…

And until our next meeting — just notice how your state changes… how it gets easier… how a new calmness emerges within you…

💡 Important

  • Don't stop talking after exit. PHW is 30–90 seconds of precious time. Use every second.
  • As soon as the client speaks — the window has closed. Don't try to continue suggestions after they've started analyzing.
  • The first session is not about "healing." It's about demonstration. The client should leave thinking: "Wow, this works. I want more."
  • Speed induces trance. A professional works fast, without unnecessary pauses. Aim for 25–30 minutes for a full session.
  • Regression from the second session onward, not earlier. You need somnambulism, trust, and at least 2–3 sessions of direct suggestions as warm-up.

26. Suggestibility Test

🌀 Module 0 — Introduction (Lesson 0.3)

When to Use

Suggestibility tests are the first step in any work with hypnosis. They are used for:

  • Diagnosis — determining the subject's level of suggestibility.
  • Demonstration — showing the person that hypnosis is real and works.
  • Selection — for stage hypnosis or finding the best somnambulists.
  • Warm-up — building compliance before a full induction.

⏱ Time per test: 2–5 minutes.

📜 Test 1 — Finger Lock

The simplest and most revealing test. Used for primary suggestibility assessment and as a warm-up before induction.

Position and Preparation

"Sit comfortably, straighten your back. Raise your hands in front of you and interlock your fingers together — like this. Good."

Main Script

"Close your eyes. Imagine that your fingers are the roots of trees that have intertwined deep underground. They are growing together, intertwining tighter and tighter…"

"With every word I speak, your fingers lock together more firmly. Stronger and stronger. They intertwine, merge together, become one. Can you feel them squeezing? As if an invisible force is pulling them together…"

"Now, silently say to yourself: 'My fingers are locked so tightly that I cannot separate them, no matter how hard I try.' Repeat this to yourself. Feel how this statement becomes true…"

"On the count of three, open your eyes and try to separate your fingers. One… two… THREE! Try to separate them!"

Evaluating the Result

  • Fingers did not separate — high suggestibility, subject is ready for induction.
  • Fingers separated with effort — medium suggestibility, warm-up needed.
  • Fingers separated easily — low suggestibility or resistance. Repeat with a more confident voice and detailed imagery.

[After the test — remove the suggestion:] "Now relax your fingers. Everything is normal, your hands are free. Excellent."

📜 Test 2 — Arm Drop

A test for passive compliance and the ability to let go of control. Helps assess how ready a person is to "surrender" control over their body.

Position and Preparation

"Stand up straight, feet shoulder-width apart. Extend your right arm out to the side, parallel to the floor. Palm facing up. Close your eyes."

Main Script

"Imagine that a heavy stone is resting on your palm. A large, heavy, granite boulder. Feel its weight…"

"The stone is getting heavier and heavier. Your arm begins to lower under its weight. Slowly, smoothly… Dropping down… Lower and lower…"

"The stone presses down on your palm, your arm goes lower. You can't hold it up. It's dropping, dropping, dropping…"

"Complete relaxation… your arm falls down… by itself…"

Evaluating the Result

  • Fast drop — strong ideomotor response, ready for movement suggestions.
  • Slow drop — moderate response, needs more time and imagery.
  • Arm doesn't drop — conscious control is resisting. Release the tension: "Just allow your arm to rest."

📜 Test 3 — Body Sway

The most dramatic and physiological test. Used as an independent suggestibility assessment and as preparation for a standing induction.

Position and Preparation

"Stand up straight, feet together, arms relaxed at your sides. Close your eyes. I am going to place my hand on your forehead."

Variant A — Falling Backward (with support)

"I place my hand on your forehead. Feel my palm. All your attention is on this touch. You are standing steadily… very steadily…"

"But now you will feel something pulling you backward… a light, gentle pull… as if a breeze is blowing on your back… softly, gently…"

"You begin to sway backward… just a little… forward… backward… and now stronger backward…"

"When I remove my hand — you will fall backward right into my arms. You are safe, I've got you. Ready? One, two, three — [removes hand] — fall!"

[Catch the subject by the back. Do not let them fall.]

Variant B — Swaying Without Touch

"Stand up straight. Close your eyes. Imagine that you are standing on the edge of a tall building looking down. Your body sways slightly forward and backward, maintaining balance…"

"Now I'm going to ask you to fall backward. Don't be afraid — I'll catch you. Just allow your body to fall backward. Fall!"

Evaluating the Result

  • Falls immediately and completely — extremely high suggestibility, somnambulistic potential.
  • Falls, but with delay — good suggestibility, but some residual control remains.
  • Doesn't fall, opens eyes — low suggestibility or strong fear. Work on pre-talk.

⚠️ Always reassure: "I will catch you." Fear of falling blocks this test in 80% of people.

💡 Important

  • Tests are already hypnosis. When a person performs a test, they are already in a light trance. Use this: after a successful test, you can transition into an induction by saying "...now just continue relaxing…".
  • Don't insist. If a test doesn't work — smile and say: "Great, you have a strong will. Let's try a different approach." Never say "you're not trying hard enough."
  • Every test is a suggestion. Phrase words as facts, not wishes: "Your fingers are locking" instead of "try to make your fingers lock."
  • Body Sway requires a spotter. Never perform a fall test without being ready to catch the person.
  • After tests — remove the suggestions. Even if the test "didn't work," say: "Everything is back to normal, your hands are free, your body is relaxed."

27. Hand Drop Rapid Induction (Kayfabe)

🌀 Module 3 — Instant and Rapid Inductions (lesson 3.2)

When to Use

This is the "gold standard" of Kayfabe instant inductions. It works on most people and forgives mistakes. Use it:

  • From the 3rd session onward with a client — trust is already established.
  • For demonstrating rapid trance induction.
  • As a re-induction if the client comes out of trance.
  • Before deep therapeutic work.

⏱ Duration: 10–15 seconds for induction + 15 seconds deepening.

📜 Complete Hand Induction Script (3 Phases)

Phase 1 — Preparation and Explanation

"Extend your arm forward, palm up. Like this. I place my hand on top of yours — palm to palm. Feel my hand on yours."

"Now I'm going to pull my hand away. At that moment, your hand will drop sharply. When I say 'sleep' — you will allow your nervous system to completely relax."

"This is not sleep — you will hear my voice, but your body will become deeply relaxed. Are you ready?"

Phase 2 — Focus + Shock + Command

"Look at my hand. Feel my palm on yours. Feel the pressure, the warmth, the touch… All your attention is only on this sensation…"

[Pause 2–3 seconds. Let the attention focus.]

"All attention on the sensation… warmth… pressure…"

[Sharply pulls his hand upward and backward. SIMULTANEOUSLY — the command.]

"SLEEP!"

Phase 3 — Instant Deepening (no pauses!)

Speak immediately. Not a second of pause. Continuous flow.

"Deep. Completely. Relax. You hear my voice — with every word you sink deeper. Your whole body relaxes. Arms relaxed. Legs. Shoulders. Neck. Every muscle."

"With every exhale — deeper and deeper. You're at the deepest depth. Complete relaxation. Very deep."

[Continue deepening for 10–15 seconds, then move to stabilisation.]

📜 Stabilisation After Induction

After you feel the person has "settled" at depth, move to stabilisation phrases:

"Any sounds around only help you relax deeper."

"The sound of my voice sends you deeper."

"The deeper you go, the better you feel."

"And the better you feel, the deeper you go."

After stabilisation you can move to a depth test (eyelid catalepsy) or directly to suggestions.

📜 Compact Version (for quick re-induction)

"Extend your arm. My palm on yours. Feel the warmth, the pressure. [2–3 sec] [Pull sharply] — SLEEP! Deep. Completely relax. With every word deeper. Your whole body soft, heavy, deep. Very deep."

💡 Important

  • Don't go silent after "sleep." If you go silent, the person will come out of trance in 1–2 seconds. No pauses in the first 15 seconds.
  • Sharp but controlled. The pull must be sharp but not strong — you're not trying to hit them. Your hand goes up and back, the client's hand falls naturally.
  • Command simultaneous with movement. Window — 0.25 seconds. Train the synchronisation: movement + word = one action.
  • Explain beforehand. If you don't explain "sleep," the person might get scared or confused. Always explain before the induction.
  • Don't repeat on failure. If it didn't work — smile, say "Great, let's try something different," and use another technique.

Balance Standing Instant

28. Standing Induction and Balance

🌀 Module 3 — Instant and Rapid Inductions (lesson 3.3)

When to Use

The standing induction is one of the most impressive methods of instant hypnosis. It uses the natural physiological response to loss of balance to create a spike and entry into trance.

  • For demonstrations and shows — maximally spectacular.
  • With clients from the 3rd session onward — when trust is established.
  • For quick re-induction.

⏱ Duration: 30–60 seconds full cycle.

📜 Complete Standing Induction Script (5 Steps)

Step 1 — Setup and Creating Focus

"Stand up straight, feet shoulder-width apart. Arms at your sides. Close your eyes."

"Feel your feet on the floor. You're standing steady. Very steady. Your body is like a rock. Nothing can move you."

[Places hand on the client's forehead.]

"Now I place my hand on your forehead. Feel my palm. All your attention — only on this touch. You feel the warmth of my hand. The pressure. You're steady, but all your attention is on my hand."

Step 2 — Head Rocking

"Your head is becoming light… very light… it begins to rock gently… forward… back… [gentle hand movement] easily and freely… by itself…"

"Allow your head to move… it knows how… forward… back… with each movement you relax more and more…"

[15–20 seconds of gentle rocking. Amplitude 5–10 degrees.]

Step 3 — Loss of Balance and "Sleep" Command

"…head tilting back… further and further… and when I say 'sleep' — you will completely relax… further back…"

[The shock moment — sharply remove your hand from the forehead.]

"SLEEP!"

Step 4 — Catch and Deepen While Suspended

[Second hand catches them by the back. Person is half-lying on your arm.]

"I've got you. You're safe. Complete relaxation. You hear my voice — with every word deeper. Relax even more. Your whole body soft, heavy, relaxed. Good. Very deep."

"Feel how my arm is holding you? You're completely safe. With every word — deeper. Relaxation flows from your head to your toes. You're at the deepest depth."

Step 5 — Seating

"Now I'll help you sit down. Keep relaxing."

[Seat them on a chair prepared behind the client.]

"There. Deeper. With every exhale deeper. Complete peace. You hear only my voice. Everything else is just background."

📜 Compact Version (without body sway)

"Stand straight. Hand on forehead. Feel the warmth. Look at me. Head getting light… rocking… tilting back… [removes hand] — SLEEP! [Catch] I've got you. Deep. Relax. With every word deeper. [Seating.]"

📜 Version B — Body Sway (deeper version)

Instead of Step 2 — Body Sway

[Hand on chest or shoulder.]

"You feel your body beginning to sway… like a branch in the wind… forward… back… stronger now… [amplitude grows]… you can't stop… your body keeps moving on its own…"

"With each sway, you relax more and more… deeper and deeper… your body becomes soft, pliable, obedient…"

"…and now, as you sway back — [sharply removes hand] — SLEEP!"

Then Steps 4–5 as in the main script.

💡 Important

  • Safety is priority #1. Always have your second hand ready to catch. A chair behind — mandatory.
  • Rocking for at least 15–20 seconds. If you move to shock too quickly, the nervous system won't have time to enter automatic movement mode.
  • The catch must be confident. If you catch hesitantly — the person will feel fear of falling and the trance will break.
  • Do not work with: vestibular system issues, epilepsy, late-term pregnancy, elderly with fragile bones.
  • The entire process is continuous. Any pause breaks the trance. Every action leads to the next without stopping.

Basic Levitation Demonstrative

3. Hand Levitation

🧘 Basic scripts

When to Use

Hand levitation is perhaps the most elegant induction in hypnosis. The hand rises not because you lift it, but because the subconscious itself decides to lift it. It's the perfect demonstration that hypnosis is not sleep or loss of consciousness, but a special state of attention.

This script is especially effective when:

  • You need to impress skeptics — the hand rises "by itself"
  • The subject is creative with a good imagination — visualization of the balloon comes easily
  • You want a gentle but deep induction without physical contact
  • A demonstration of the subconscious at work is needed for educational purposes

⏱ Duration: 10–15 minutes. The key is believing that the hand will actually rise. Speak confidently, without doubt.

📜 Induction Script

Preparation

Sit comfortably. Place your hands on your knees, palms up — as if you're ready to receive something. Feet on the floor, don't cross them. Close your eyes. Take a deep breath...

And as you exhale, let your whole body begin to relax. Good. Another deep breath... and exhale... with each exhale, you release tension, sinking deeper into a state of calm.

Body Relaxation

Direct your attention to the crown of your head. Relax your scalp... forehead... eyes... Let your eyelids become heavy, very heavy. Relax your jaw, lips, neck.

Relax your shoulders. Just let them drop down. Your arms become heavy... warm... relaxed. Your entire upper body is resting.

Relax your chest, abdomen. With each breath in and out, your breathing becomes steadier, deeper, calmer. Relax your back, lower back. Your whole body is resting.

Relax your thighs, legs, feet. From head to toe — complete relaxation. You are safe. You are calm.

Visualization of the Balloon

And now imagine... right above your right hand... a light balloon is floating. It's a bright, pleasant color — maybe red, or blue, or gold. What color do you see?

This balloon is filled with warm air, with helium. It is very light, it wants to rise up. And from it to your hand stretches a thin but strong string.

The string is tied to your wrist. And you feel a gentle pull of the string transmitting to your hand. The balloon wants to rise higher, and it pulls your hand along with it.

You may notice that your hand is becoming lighter... as if it's losing weight... as if it's being filled with the same gas as the balloon. Lightness in your hand is growing.

Beginning of Levitation

The balloon begins to slowly rise. And your hand... begins to repeat its movement. Barely noticeable. The first millimeters. Your fingers lift slightly from your knee.

The higher the balloon rises, the lighter your hand becomes. The less effort it needs to follow the balloon. Your hand is rising by itself — you are just allowing it to happen.

Good. The balloon rises higher... and your hand rises higher. Higher and higher. Without your conscious effort. Simply because your subconscious knows how to do it.

Your hand rises... and with every centimeter, you sink deeper into trance. Hand levitation deepens your state. The higher the hand — the deeper you go.

Strengthening the Levitation

Your subconscious is controlling the movement of your hand. It knows how to lift it exactly as much as needed. And with every moment, your hand rises higher.

You may feel a slight tingling in your fingers, warmth in your palm — these are signs that the subconscious is actively working. Good. Very good.

Your hand continues to rise. Higher and higher. Toward your face. The closer your hand gets to your face, the deeper your trance becomes.

Face Touch — Deepening

When your hand touches your face... at that moment, you will fall into the deepest, most complete trance possible. The touch of the face is the signal for the subconscious: complete letting go.

Your hand approaches your face... slowly but surely... higher... even higher... a little more... and — touch.

Deep breath. And complete letting go. You are in the deepest trance. You are here. You are safe. You hear my voice, and every word I speak penetrates deep into your subconscious.

Allow your hand to slowly return to your knee. With every centimeter down, the state solidifies, becomes stable, enduring. Your hand is on your knee. You are in a deep, peaceful trance.

Consolidation

Now I will count from one to five, anchoring this state. One — deep relaxation throughout your body. Two — you are calm and balanced. Three — every word I speak resonates with your subconscious. Four — this state is available to you at any moment by your own desire. Five — you are in an ideal, harmonious state of hypnosis.

Enjoy this moment. Everything is good. You are in a deep trance.

💡 Tip

If the hand doesn't rise — don't insist. Say: "That's normal, some people feel lightness differently. Just notice how much lighter your hand has become." Then you can switch to the other hand or use a different anchor (for example, imagine a magnet pulling the hand upward). Don't say "try to lift your hand" — that's conscious effort. Say "allow your hand to rise" — that's an appeal to the subconscious.

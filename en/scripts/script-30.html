30. Phenomena in Rapid Hypnosis

🌀 Module 3 — Instant and Rapid Inductions (lesson 3.5)

When to Use

After rapid trance induction (hand drop, standing, shock) you can immediately demonstrate phenomena. They confirm trance depth and strengthen the subject's belief in hypnosis.

  • Immediately after rapid induction — to test somnambulism.
  • To demonstrate the power of hypnosis to skeptics.
  • As a warm-up before deep therapeutic work.

⏱ Duration: 2–5 minutes per phenomenon.

📜 Phenomenon 1 — Number Amnesia

In trance — setting the suggestion

"Now your subconscious will receive an interesting task… It will learn to skip one number… Just one number out of ten."

"When you open your eyes and I ask you to count from 1 to 10… you will count easily and freely… 1… 2… 3… 4… 5… … 7… 8… 9… 10…"

"The number 6 simply won't be there. It will vanish. You will try to remember what number is missing — and you won't be able to. The harder you try, the further it slips away."

"Even if someone hints — you won't believe them. For you, the number 6 doesn't exist. You'll just skip it — that's all."

"Good. Now I'll count to three, and you will open your eyes, feeling alert and refreshed. One… two… three — eyes open, full awakening."

After Exit — Testing

"Count aloud from 1 to 10, just to wake up a bit."

Removing the Suggestion

"Now I'll snap my fingers, and the number 6 will return to its place. Snap! There, now all numbers are back. Count again."

📜 Phenomenon 2 — Name Amnesia

In trance — setting the suggestion

"In a few seconds I'll count to three. On the count of three you will open your eyes. You won't remember your name."

"The harder you try to remember — the more your name slips away. Just for a little while. Want it to happen."

Exit and Testing

"One, two, three. Eyes open. What's your name?.. Can't remember? That's normal."

Removing the Suggestion

"Now I'll snap my fingers — and your name will come back." [Snap] "What's your name? Remembered. Great."

⚠️ Caution: do not prolong name amnesia for more than 10–15 seconds. It may cause anxiety. Remove the suggestion quickly and confidently.

📜 Phenomenon 3 — Eyelid Catalepsy

Immediately after rapid induction (after 2–3 seconds)

"Your eyes are tightly closed. You cannot open your eyes. Try — eyes won't open." [2–3 seconds]

If they don't open — catalepsy worked. Confirmation of somnambulism.

"Good. Stop trying. Relax even deeper."

Removal

"And now your eyes open easily. One… two… three — open your eyes."

🧠 Nuance: In some people, eyelid catalepsy doesn't work even in deep trance — this is an individual trait. Don't insist, move on to other phenomena.

📜 Phenomenon 4 — Post-Hypnotic Reinduction

In trance — setting the trigger

"On the count of three you will open your eyes. You will feel alert and refreshed."

"But as soon as I tug my earlobe — you will instantly sink into trance, even deeper than now. Even deeper. Even more completely."

"This will happen automatically. You won't be able to stop it. Your subconscious will hear the signal and immediately return you to this deep state."

Exit by Count

"One… two… three — eyes open. You're back. Feeling great."

Reinduction After 5–10 Seconds

[Tugs earlobe.]

"…and sleep! Deeper than before. Even deeper. Complete relaxation. With every word deeper."

Other Trigger Options

  • Shoulder touch: "When I touch your shoulder…"
  • Finger snap: "As soon as I snap my fingers…"
  • Keyword: "When I say 'deep'…"
  • Eye contact: "When I hold your gaze for 3 seconds…"

💡 Important

  • Phenomena only work in somnambulism. If there's no trance — the person will just "play along." Distinguish between a real phenomenon and social compliance.
  • Conditions for success: absence of fear, willingness to participate, trust, somnambulism, desire to "play."
  • If it didn't work — don't insist. Say: "Great, next time it'll work. For now, we'll just work with relaxation."
  • Always remove suggestions after demonstration. Amnesia, catalepsy, reinduction — every phenomenon must be cancelled.
  • Reinduction is a powerful tool. Allows deepening trance multiple times in one session. Cycle: induction → exit → trigger → even deeper induction.

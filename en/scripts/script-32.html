Regression Affective Bridge

32. Introduction to Regression

🌀 Module 4 — Demonstrating Phenomena (lesson 4.6)

When to Use

Regression is needed when the problem has roots in a past experience. The main marker: the person tells a story in response to a question about the problem. Regression is not remembering — it is reliving.

  • Regression needed: specific past trauma, a story, a connection to an event.
  • Direct suggestion suffices: no story, "don't know where it comes from," surface-level problem.

⚠️ Regression is an advanced technique. Requires somnambulism, trust, and at least 2–3 warm-up sessions.

📜 Affective Bridge — Finding the First Incident

Principle

We don't ask "remember what happened." We bring up the feeling and allow that feeling to lead us back to the very first time it arose.

Step 1 — Entering Somnambulism

Perform a full induction. Confirm the subject is in deep trance (eyelid catalepsy, ideomotor signals).

Step 2 — Eliciting the Affect (Feeling)

"Now I want you to recall a situation where you feel [problematic feeling: anxiety / fear / hurt]. Just recall a hint of that feeling…"

"Allow that feeling to arise… don't resist it… just notice where in your body it resonates… in your chest? in your stomach? in your throat?"

"Good. Just hold that feeling. It is familiar to you. It is a part of you."

Step 3 — Affective Bridge (Going Back in Time)

"And now allow that feeling to take you back… into the past… to the very first time you felt this…"

"It knows the way. Just follow the feeling. You are getting younger… and younger… time is going backward… 5 years ago… 10 years… 15 years…"

"Let this feeling guide you… to the very first time… when this feeling first appeared…"

"I will be silent for 10 seconds. Just follow the feeling. [Pause 10 seconds]"

"Where are you now? How old are you? What do you see around you?"

Step 4 — Working with the Found Incident

"Look at the situation from the outside. You see yourself at that age. What is happening? Who is there? What do you feel?"

"And now — your future self, who has already lived through this… who has grown, become stronger, wiser… Approach your child-self. What do you want to tell them? What do they need to hear?"

Step 5 — Neutralization and Return

"Now that you have given your past self what was needed, allow this event to leave… it no longer affects you… it simply stays in the past…"

"And you slowly return here… to the present… growing older with each of my words… you are now [client's age] years old… you are here, in a safe place…"

"I will count from 1 to 3. On the count of 3 you will open your eyes, feeling calm and at ease. One… two… three — eyes open."

📜 Safety Protocol for Regression

Before Regression

  • Check somnambulism — level 5+ on Davis-Husband is mandatory.
  • Set a safety anchor — a word or touch that instantly returns to calm: "When I touch your shoulder, you will feel complete calm and safety."
  • Get consent — explain what you will do, ask permission.
  • Prepare a resource state — ensure the subject has a positive resource for "rewriting" the trauma.

During Regression

  • Watch for non-verbal cues — rapid breathing, tears, muscle tension are signals.
  • Do not let the subject drown in emotion — if emotion is too strong, use the anchor or shift attention to dissociation.
  • Do not ask leading questions — do not suggest false memories. Ask: "What do you see?" not "Do you see a man in a red hat?"
  • Do not leave the subject in trauma — always end regression with neutralization and return to resource state.

After Regression

  • Verify the trauma is neutralized — ask them to recall the trigger situation again. If there is no emotion — regression succeeded.
  • Give time for integration — after regression, don't send the client straight into their day. Let them sit quietly for 5–10 minutes.
  • Do not leave without a post-hypnotic suggestion — "Every day it gets easier and easier for you."

📜 When NOT to Do Regression

1. On yourself. Absolutely not allowed. You cannot be both operator and subject. If you go under — you lose control.

2. On the first session. Regression requires trust, somnambulism, and at least 2–3 warm-up sessions.

3. Without somnambulism. In light trance the person does not regress — they just remember. That is not regression, it is conversation under hypnosis.

4. With severe psychotic disorders. Regression may trigger a psychotic episode. If the client has diagnosed schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, or PTSD — refer to a specialist.

5. If the client does not consent. Regression is collaboration. Without explicit consent, do not begin.

6. Without a safety anchor. You must have an anchor that instantly brings them out of traumatic reliving.

💡 Important

  • Regression is not magic. It is a skill that has its own dedicated 9-hour masterclass. This script is only an introduction to understanding the principles.
  • Direct suggestion vs regression: direct suggestion works on the symptom, regression works on the cause. Analogy: painkiller vs removing a splinter.
  • The most important thing in regression is safety. Incorrect practice can re-traumatize the client or create false memories.
  • After neutralization — progression. Suggest how the client will feel in a month, a year, 5 years.

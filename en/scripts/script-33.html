33. Stage Hypnosis (Show Script)

🌀 Module 6 — Advanced Topics (lesson 6.1)

When to Use

This show script is designed for stage demonstration. It is important to remember: stage hypnosis is entertainment built on trust. On stage, people do enter genuine trance, but the goal is the show, not therapy.

  • Stage performances, parties, events.
  • Demonstrating the possibilities of hypnosis to the general public.
  • Practicing rapid induction skills on a group.

⏱ Show duration: 15–30 minutes.

📜 Phase 1 — Invitation and Volunteer Selection

Addressing the Audience

"Ladies and gentlemen, I now need 5–10 volunteers for a demonstration. Don't be afraid — hypnosis is completely safe. You will hear everything and remain in control. If you want to experience an unusual state of consciousness — come up on stage!"

[Important: those who come on stage have already "given consent." They are ready to play.]

Quick Suggestibility Test on Stage

"Stand in a line, facing me. Raise both arms in front of you, palms facing each other, about 30 centimeters apart. Look at the gap between your palms."

"Imagine there is a strong magnet between your palms. The palms are drawn together… closer and closer… and closer…"

[Assess whose palms came together quickly — these people are the most suggestible. They are your "show stars."]

[For those who didn't respond — gently send them back:] "Thank you, you did great. Please take a seat."

📜 Phase 2 — Rapid Group Induction

Group Induction (Hand Drop — One by One)

Approach each remaining volunteer in turn.

"Extend your arm forward, palm up. My hand on top. Feel my palm. All attention on the touch. [2–3 sec] — SLEEP!"

[Immediately after the command — help the subject sit on a chair. Move to the next.]

[When all volunteers are seated — do a group deepening:]

"You hear my voice — with every word deeper. All of you are at the deepest depth. Complete relaxation. Deep, deep down."

📜 Phase 3 — Stage Phenomena

Phenomenon 1 — Arm Catalepsy (Demonstration)

"Raise your right arm up. Your arm is becoming rigid, like an iron bar. Hard. Unbendable. You cannot bend it. Try — it won't bend."

[Test the arm of 1–2 volunteers — show the audience the arm won't bend.]

"Now your arm relaxes. Lower your arm. Deep. Even deeper."

Phenomenon 2 — Name Amnesia

[Approaches one volunteer]

"On the count of three you will open your eyes. You won't remember your name. One, two, three — eyes open." [Pause] "What's your name?"

[Volunteer cannot remember. After 5–10 seconds — removal:]

"Snap — your name is back. What's your name? [They answer] Great. Close your eyes again. Deeper."

Phenomenon 3 — Post-Hypnotic Reinduction

[Select one volunteer for demonstration.]

"On the count of three you will open your eyes. You will feel great. But when I snap my fingers — you will go back into trance, even deeper than before."

"One, two, three. Eyes open." [Pause 5 seconds, talk to the volunteer] "How do you feel? Great?"

[Snap fingers]

"SLEEP! Even deeper than before. Complete relaxation."

[Volunteer instantly goes into trance — audience is impressed.]

Phenomenon 4 — Body Catalepsy (Human Bridge)

⚠️ For experienced hypnotists only. Requires physical strength and backup.

"Now your body will become absolutely rigid, like a board. You won't be able to bend it. [Positions volunteer between two chairs] Your body is hard as steel. I lay you down… [lowers] …and you are suspended between the chairs. Absolutely straight."

[Show the audience — then return the volunteer to a seated position.]

📜 Phase 4 — Show Closing (Ethical Wrap-Up)

Group Exit from Trance

"Now I will bring all of you out of this state. You will return completely, feeling fresh, alert, rested."

"All suggestions I gave during the show are now cancelled. All special effects are over. You are returning to your normal state."

"I will count from 1 to 3. On the count of 3 — open your eyes."

"One — slowly return… feel your body…"

"Two — wiggle your fingers, take a deep breath…"

"Three — eyes open! Welcome back. How do you feel?"

Verification of Suggestion Removal (Critically Important!)

Check each volunteer:

  • "What's your name?" — name must return.
  • "Bend your arm" — catalepsy must be removed.
  • "Count from 1 to 10" — all numbers in place.

If anyone has residual suggestions — do individual removal:

"Everything I said to you on stage is cancelled. You are completely free. All effects are gone. You are back to normal."

Final Words

"Ladies and gentlemen, you have just witnessed a demonstration of hypnotic phenomena. All volunteers are perfectly fine. Hypnosis is not magic and not loss of control. It is a natural state of the body that we used today for entertainment. Thank you for your attention! Please applaud our brave volunteers!"

💡 Important — Ethics of Stage Hypnosis

  • Do not humiliate volunteers. Quacking like a duck — fine. Mimicking intimate movements — not allowed. The boundary is human dignity.
  • No sexual suggestions. Absolutely forbidden. After the show, the volunteer may remember and feel discomfort.
  • Remove ALL suggestions after the show. Every post-hypnotic suggestion must be cancelled. The volunteer must not leave with a "hanging" command.
  • Do not work with drunk/high individuals. Not only unethical but dangerous.
  • Check everyone. After the show, ensure every volunteer has fully returned to normal.

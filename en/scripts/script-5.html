5. Visual Induction (Gaze Fixation)

🧘 Basic scripts

When to Use

Gaze fixation is the oldest hypnotic technique, described in ancient texts. The modern standard of the method traces back to James Esdaile, a British surgeon of the 19th century who performed over 300 operations under hypnosis.

This method is especially good when:

  • The subject is visual — holds attention better with the eyes than with body sensations
  • You want to demonstrate "classic" hypnosis in the 19th-century style
  • You need to induce trance without touch — works remotely
  • The person is intellectual, analytical — eye fatigue shuts down the conscious mind

⏱ Duration: 10–20 minutes. For fixation, use a point on the wall at eye level (a sticker, pen, or your own finger). Eyes stay open until the signal to close.

📜 Induction Script

Preparation for Fixation

Sit comfortably, back straight but not tense. Hands on your knees. Now I'm going to show you a point for gaze fixation. Look at it carefully. Don't look away. Your task is simply to look and listen to my voice.

Find this point with your eyes. Fix your gaze on it. Don't blink longer than usual, but try not to look away. This point is the center of your world for the next few minutes.

Eye Fatigue

Look at the point. Carefully. Your eyes are beginning to tire. Your eyelids are becoming heavy. You feel the muscles around your eyes tiring from the strain.

The point may begin to change. It may grow larger, smaller, blur, double. This is normal. It's a sign that your eyes are tired and ready to close.

Perhaps you notice that your field of vision is darkening at the edges. As if a tunnel is narrowing, and in the center only this point remains... and my voice.

Your breathing becomes deeper. With each inhale, you fill with calmness. With each exhale, tension leaves. Only the point and your breathing.

Suggestion of Eyelid Heaviness

Your eyelids are becoming very heavy. Leaden. They want to close. They would feel so good closed... to stop straining... to just drop down and rest.

But you continue to look. Because I ask you to look. And you will look until your eyes close by themselves — when they are ready. You are not closing them consciously. They will close on their own when the time comes.

The point is still there. But it's becoming blurry. As if a fog is enveloping it. Your eyes are finding it harder and harder to focus. They are tired. Your eyes are very, very tired.

Command to Close Eyes

Now I will count from five to one. With each number, your eyes will become heavier. And when I say "one" — you will close your eyes and fall into a deep, peaceful trance.

Five... your eyes are tiring. Eyelids heavy. The point is blurring.

Four... heaviness grows. Breathing deeper. Relaxation stronger.

Three... almost ready. Your eyes want to close. You feel this desire.

Two... one last look at the point. Remember this feeling. Soon you will close your eyes and enter trance instantly — just because your subconscious remembered this signal.

One... close your eyes. Deep breath in. And with the exhale — fall into the deepest relaxation possible.

Deepening

Good. Eyes are closed. Now you can relax them completely. Relax your eyelids — they are no longer tense. Relax your eyes inside — as if they are softly sinking into their sockets.

Relax your face — forehead, cheeks, jaw. Relax your neck and shoulders. Let your whole body follow your eyes into a state of deep peace.

Now I will count again — this time from ten to one. And with each number, you will go twice as deep as you are now.

Ten... deep relaxation.

Nine... deeper.

Eight... all outside sounds only help you sink deeper.

Seven... you are completely safe.

Six... deeper with every word.

Five... you are halfway to the deepest point.

Four... three... deep and calm.

Two... almost at the bottom.

One... complete, absolute, deepest state of trance.

Consolidation

You are in a deep trance. You hear my voice, and it penetrates directly into your subconscious, bypassing your conscious mind. Every word is like a pebble thrown into smooth water. Ripples spreading deep inside you.

Remember this moment. Remember the path: gaze on the point, eye fatigue, the command to close your eyes — and instant immersion. In the future, when you hear the command "Sleep" or simply want to relax — you can return to this state in seconds.

You are in complete harmony. You are resting. Enjoy this moment of deep, restorative peace.

When I count to three, you will open your eyes, feeling rested, clear, full of energy. One... slowly return... two... feel your body, wiggle your fingers... three — eyes open. You are here. You are wonderful.

💡 Tip

To strengthen the effect, hold the point slightly above eye level — so the subject rolls their eyes slightly upward. In hypnosis physiology, this eye position (upward convergence) correlates with activation of the parasympathetic nervous system. You can use your finger, a coin on the wall, or even a shiny object — Esdaile used a metallic disc. If the eyes haven't closed after 3–4 minutes, say: "Good. Now close your eyes and fall into trance three times deeper" — don't wait for complete fatigue.

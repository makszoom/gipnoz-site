Age Regression — Corridor Technique

From Adv4 — Marketing Your Practice & Age Regression

When to Use

The Corridor Technique is a powerful visualization method where the subject walks down a corridor of doors, each representing a year of their life. When they stop at a door, they enter the room and re-experience the significant events stored there. This technique works best in deep somnambulism.

  • Accessing and processing childhood memories
  • Releasing stored emotional trauma from specific life periods
  • Finding the root cause of recurring patterns

⏱ Time: ~20-30 minutes

📜 Script

Corridor Induction

"You are standing at the entrance of a long corridor. As you look down it, you can see doors on both sides — left and right — stretching far into the distance. Each door represents a year of your life. The ones closest to you are recent years. As you walk further down, the doors become younger, taking you back through your past. Begin walking slowly now. Feel your feet on the floor. Notice the lighting — soft and gentle. As you walk, pay attention to any door that draws your attention — a strange feeling, a pull, a curiosity. When you feel that pull, stop. Raise your finger to let me know. We will enter that room together. There is no rush. Take your time."

Memory Processing (Green/Red Button)

"You are now inside the room. In the center of the room there is a pedestal, and on it are two buttons — one green, one red. Press the green button now. As you do, a screen lights up on the wall. On that screen, a significant memory from this year begins to play. You are an observer — safe, detached, watching. Describe what you see. What is happening? Who is there? What do you feel as you watch? Take your time. There is no need to rush. When you have seen enough, when you have felt the emotion and understood it, press the red button. Watch the image on the screen begin to fade. Watch it dissolve, becoming dimmer and dimmer until it is gone. The memory remains, but it no longer controls you. The emotion has been released. You are free."

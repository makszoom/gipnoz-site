Emergency Anesthesia Protocol

From B03 — Bypassing the Critical Factor & Emergency Anesthesia

When to Use

The three-step emergency anesthesia protocol works by redirecting attention. The mind can only focus on one thing at a time — if you fully engage the subject's attention, pain signals cannot reach conscious awareness. In emergency settings, the critical factor is already weakened by shock, making it easier to bypass.

  • Dental procedures without chemical anesthesia
  • Minor medical procedures and wound care
  • Crisis situations where chemical anesthesia is unavailable

⏱ Time: ~3-5 minutes for the full protocol

📜 Script

Step 1: Bypass the Critical Factor (Pretending Game)

"Close your eyes. Now pretend — pretend as hard as you can — that your eyelids are glued shut. Imagine someone poured superglue on them and they are sealed tight. Your eyelids are fixed, sealed, locked together. Now try to open them. Try. You will find that the harder you try, the tighter they stay shut. That is it — your eyes are locked. Good. Now stop trying and just relax."

Step 2: Install Acceptable Selective Thinking

"Nothing I do here will bother or disturb you in any way. Nothing will cause you any discomfort. Everything that happens will be perfectly acceptable to your mind. Your subconscious will accept each suggestion easily and naturally, because it knows you are safe and in control."

Step 3: Redirect Awareness (Create Numbness)

"Now focus all your attention on your [left/right] hand. Notice the exact sensation of the air against your skin. That feeling of pressure, of warmth, of your hand being exactly where it is. Now that same feeling is spreading to your [target area]. Not sharpness — just pressure. Only pressure. Like someone pressing through a thick leather glove. Tell me when the numbness is complete. Just nod when it feels completely different from the rest of your body."

Variant: Hand Anesthesia (Practice Version)

"Now pretend your hand is wrapped in thick leather. Thick, heavy leather. You can feel pressure, but nothing sharp. Nothing sharp at all. I am going to touch your hand, and you will notice only pressure — like someone pressing through a thick glove. No sharpness, no pain. Just pressure. Just the sensation of touch without any discomfort. Let me know when that feels right."

Variant: Quick Dental Anesthesia

"Fix your mind on this suggestion: as I count to three, all feeling in your jaw will disappear completely. Like novocaine spreading, but you control it — your mind controls it completely. One — the numbness begins, spreading through your jaw like a gentle wave. Two — deeper now, spreading further, the numbness grows. Three — complete. Your jaw is completely numb. Test it with your fingernail — press gently. You will feel pressure but no sharp sensation at all."

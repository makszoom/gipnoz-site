Arm Pull Induction

From Adv1 — Rapid & Instant Inductions

When to Use

The falling sensation occupies the conscious mind, preventing resistance. As the subject falls forward, you catch them and guide them into trance. This induction works well for physically responsive subjects and those who benefit from a direct, physical approach to trance.

  • Returning clients who already know they are hypnotizable
  • Physical/hands-on learners who respond to body cues
  • When you want a fast, dramatic induction

⏱ Time: ~5 seconds for the induction, ~30 seconds for deepening

📜 Script

Full Arm Pull Induction

"I would like you to stand up for me, please. [wait for them to stand] Extend your right arm straight out in front of you, palm facing up. Good. Now I am going to pull you forward. When I pull, I want you to close your eyes, let your mind go completely blank, and let yourself fall forward into a deep, deep trance. Do not think — just let go. Ready? Close your eyes. [grip their wrist firmly and pull straight toward you — as they start to topple forward] Sleep now! [catch them with your other arm, guide them into the chair, and continue in a calm voice] That is it. Let yourself sink deep. Deeper and deeper with every breath. Every muscle letting go. Every thought fading away. You are in a wonderful, peaceful state of hypnosis."

Alternative Version

"Stand up please. Extend your arm straight out. When I pull, close your eyes, let go completely, and fall into a deep trance. Ready? [pull firmly toward you] Sleep now! [catch them with your other arm] Relax completely. Let every muscle soften. Sink deep into the chair."

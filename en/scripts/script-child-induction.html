Children Play Quick

Child Induction — Pretending Game

From B12 — Working with Children & The Coma State

When to Use

Working with children is simpler than working with adults because children have less critical factor and more imagination. Use imagination, not relaxation: "Let's play a pretending game." Keep inductions short — children have short attention spans. Children respond to authority and playfulness. They don't fear hypnosis, so they go deep fast.

  • Children ages 4-12 who respond to imaginative play
  • When a playful, non-threatening induction is needed
  • As a quick bypass technique for any age (the pretending game works for adults too)

⏱ Time: ~30-60 seconds

📜 Script

Child Induction (Pretending Game)

"Let's play a game. Close your eyes. Now pretend they're stuck — like someone put glue on them. Try to open them — you can't! Good. Now pretend your arm is made of rubber — it's so heavy and loose. Keep pretending. You're doing great."

Key Principles for Working with Children

(1) Use imagination, not relaxation: "Let's play a pretending game."
(2) Keep inductions short — children have short attention spans.
(3) Use the "Pretending Game": close their eyes and pretend they're glued shut.
(4) Children respond to authority and playfulness. They don't fear hypnosis, so they go deep fast.

Ethical Note

Always maintain appropriate boundaries. Child abuse must be reported by law. Use the pretending game, not physical contact.

Coma Deep Advanced

Coma State — Three-Level Deepening

From B04, B12 — Trance Levels & Working with Children

When to Use

The coma state is the deepest level of hypnosis, characterized by complete physical catalepsy, spontaneous anesthesia, and time distortion. Three specific tests confirm coma. Understanding this state is essential for anyone doing deep therapeutic work.

  • Deep therapeutic work requiring the most profound trance state
  • When a subject spontaneously drops into deep trance
  • Advanced practitioners working with deep-seated trauma

⏱ Time: ~5-10 minutes to achieve, varies by subject

📜 Script

Three-Level Deepening (A → B → C)

"I want you to double your relaxation — that's Level A. Now double it again — Level B. Now double it once more — Level C, the basement. You're in the deepest possible state."

Coma State Induction

"You're going deeper now. Deeper than you've ever been. Your body is completely relaxed — so relaxed that it no longer needs to respond. Your arms, your legs — they'll stay exactly where I place them. Your body is resting so deeply that it has shut down all unnecessary functions. You are in a state of complete healing rest. Nothing will disturb you. Only my voice reaches you."

Safe Emergence from Coma (Step 1: Reassurance)

"You are safe. You are in a very deep state of relaxation, and that is perfectly fine. There is no rush. I am going to help you come back slowly and comfortably. Just listen to my voice and follow along."

Safe Emergence (Step 2: Progressive Emergence)

"On 3 you will rise to a lighter state — somnambulism — while staying deeply relaxed. One ... two ... three — that is it, you can hear my voice clearly now. On 5 you will be fully awake and alert. One ... two ... three ... four ... five — open your eyes now, fully awake and refreshed."

Safe Emergence (Step 3: Physical Anchor)

"Feel the pressure on your shoulder. That is the real world. Focus on that sensation — it is solid, it is real. Come back toward it. As I count to 5, follow that feeling back to full awareness. One — feeling the pressure. Two — becoming more aware. Three — taking a deep breath. Four — energy returning. Five — eyes open, fully awake."

Fright Command (Last Resort Only)

"Open your eyes now! Take a deep breath. You are fully awake and alert. Look around the room. You are safe and completely in control."

Standard Emergence from Deep Trance

"In a moment I will count from 1 to 5. On 5, your eyes will open, and you will feel refreshed and alert. One — beginning to surface, becoming aware of the room around you. Two — more aware, feeling sensation return to your body. Three — take a deep breath, filling your lungs with energy. Four — feeling energy return throughout your entire body. Five — eyes open, wide awake, feeling wonderful."

Post-Induction Deepening (Pyramid Technique)

"I'm going to count to three. On three, you'll double your relaxation. One... deeper. Two... even deeper. Three — double it. Good. Now I'll do it again — on three, double it again. One... deeper still. Two... sinking further. Three — double it completely. Every time I count to three, your relaxation doubles, and you go deeper than ever before."

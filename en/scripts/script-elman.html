★ Full Elman Induction

From B08 — Elman Induction: Live Demo & Step-by-Step

When to Use

The Elman induction is the core induction of this course. It is designed to produce somnambulism in 4-5 minutes. It follows a precise sequence: physical relaxation (eye catalepsy, hand drop), mental relaxation (reverse counting), and deepening (fractionation). Use for first sessions, anxious clients, and anyone who needs gradual trust-building.

  • First session with a new subject
  • Anxious or nervous clients who need trust-building
  • When deep somnambulism is required for therapeutic work

⏱ Time: ~4-5 minutes

📜 Script

Step 1: Eye Catalepsy

"Close your eyes. Now focus all your attention on your eyelids. Relax them completely. Let them feel so heavy, so relaxed, that they stop working. They just won't work. In a moment, I'm going to ask you to try to open them. If they don't open, they're locked. If they do open, just stop trying and go deeper. Try to open your eyes now — just try. They don't work. Good. That's proof that your body is already responding to suggestion. Let that relaxation spread from your eyes throughout your entire body."

Step 2: Hand Drop Test

"I'm going to lift your hand. Don't help me — let it be completely limp. Don't hold it up, don't assist me. Just let it be dead weight. I will lift it, and when I drop it, it will fall like a wet rag. And when it falls, you will go twice as deep. [Lift the hand gently.] Now I'm dropping it. [Let it fall.] Deeper. Twice as deep. Every muscle, every fiber of your body is letting go completely."

Step 3: Reverse Counting (Mind Relaxation)

"Now I want you to count backward from 100. Say each number out loud. With each number, your mind becomes twice as relaxed. 100 — more relaxed. 99 — more relaxed. 98 — if you lose count, just make up numbers and keep going. It doesn't matter if they're right — what matters is that your mind is letting go. [Wait as the subject counts. As their voice slows...] That's right. Your mind is becoming quiet. Let the numbers fade. Let the thoughts drift away. Deeper and deeper with every passing moment."

Step 4: Fractionation

"Open your eyes. Close them again and go twice as deep. [Pause.] Open your eyes. Close them — twice as deep as before. [Pause.] Open your eyes. Close them — deepest yet. Three times deeper than when you started. Now stay there. Let that deep relaxation settle into every cell of your body. You are in the perfect state for therapeutic work."

Complete Elman Induction (Full Script)

"Close your eyes. Relax your eyelids until they stop working. Focus all your attention on them — relax them completely. Now test them. Try to open them. They don't work. Good. Now let that relaxation spread through your whole body. I'm going to lift your hand. Don't help me — let it be completely limp. When I drop it, go twice as deep. [Lift and drop the hand.] Good. Now count backward from 100. With each number, your mind becomes twice as relaxed. 100 — more relaxed. 99 — more relaxed. 98 — deeper. [Continue until the subject's voice slows around 93-95.] Now stop counting. Open your eyes. Close them — twice as deep. Open — close — deeper. Open — close — deepest. Now stay there. Completely relaxed. Perfectly at peace."

Modified Elman (for anxious subjects)

"Your eyes are locked. That's enough to tell me you're in trance. Now let's relax your mind. Count backward from 100. With each number, your mind becomes twice as relaxed. 100 — more relaxed. 99 — more relaxed. 98 — if you lose count, just make up numbers. The counting isn't important — the relaxation is. [After counting settles...] Now open your eyes. Close them — twice as deep. Open — close — even deeper. Open — close — deepest. You are now in a perfect state of trance."

Emotional Release & Spontaneous Regression

From Adv3 — Emotional Release & Regression

When to Use

When a subject shows strong emotion during a session — anger, tears, fear — that emotion is a doorway to the root cause. Emotion is not the enemy of therapy — it is the signal that points to the root cause. Train yourself to notice emotional shifts and use them as entry points.

  • When the subject spontaneously shows emotion during a session
  • Unresolved grief with a deceased loved one (Deathbed Therapy)
  • Unfinished business with a living person (Empty Chair Technique)

⏱ Time: ~15-25 minutes

📜 Script

Spontaneous Regression Protocol

"That feeling you are experiencing right now — that is important. Close your eyes. Let that feeling grow. Do not push it away. Let it become stronger. [pause five seconds] Now tell me: where in your body do you feel it most? In your chest? Your throat? Your stomach? Good. Now I want you to let your mind go back. Go back to the very first time you felt this exact feeling. You do not need to know what it is. You do not need to figure it out. Just let it come. Let the memory rise naturally. [pause] When you are ready, describe what you see. Describe what is happening. I am right here with you. You are safe."

Deathbed Therapy Protocol

"I want you to go back to the last time you were with [name]. See them clearly. Notice the details — the light, the sounds, the feeling in the room. You are with them again. And now I want you to say everything you never got to say. Tell them what is in your heart. Tell them you love them. Tell them you forgive them. Tell them you are sorry. Take all the time you need. [long pause — thirty seconds or more] Now hear their response. Listen. They are telling you that they are at peace. They are telling you that they love you. They are telling you that it is okay to let go. [pause] It is time to say goodbye. Say it now. [pause] Now watch them fade gently, like a beautiful sunset. They are at peace. And you are allowed to continue living. You carry their love with you always, but the pain is gone. You are free."

Empty Chair Technique

"I am going to place this empty chair in front of you. In that chair I want you to imagine your [father/mother/partner/boss]. See them sitting there. Notice how they look. Notice their expression. [pause] Now I want you to tell them what you never told them. Say it out loud. Tell them how you felt. Tell them what you needed from them. Tell them what hurt you. Do not hold back. Use your real voice. [long pause — let them speak] Good. Now I want you to stand up and switch chairs. Sit in that chair. [wait for them to move] Now you are [name]. You have heard everything your [son/daughter/partner] just said. What do you say back? What do you want them to know? [pause] Keep going until it feels complete. [let the dialogue continue until resolution] Now return to your original seat. [wait] Take a deep breath. Notice how much lighter you feel. You said what needed to be said. You heard what you needed to hear. It is done."

Somatic Emotional Release

"Focus on the tension in your chest. Breathe deeply into that tightness. On the exhale, let a little of it go. Just a little. Good. Again — breathe into it, and on the exhale release more. Each breath releases more tension, more emotion, more weight. Keep going until there is nothing left but empty, peaceful space. You are safe. You are lighter. You are free."

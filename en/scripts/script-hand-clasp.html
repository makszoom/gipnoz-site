Hand Clasp Induction

From Adv1 — Rapid & Instant Inductions

When to Use

The subject clasps their own hands and imagines them glued together. The harder they try to separate them, the tighter they lock. The critical factor is bypassed before the "Sleep!" command — the subject is already in trance from the hand clasp alone. The command just formalizes it.

  • Stage demonstrations — highly visual, audience sees the struggle
  • Analytical clients — they prove to themselves they're hypnotized
  • First-time subjects — self-generated catalepsy builds confidence

⏱ Time: ~15-20 seconds clasp, ~3 seconds to trance after "Sleep!"

📜 Script

Full Hand Clasp Induction (Standard)

"Clasp your hands together like this. I want you to imagine that your hands are pressed tightly against each other. They are pressed so tightly that for a few moments you won't even be able to separate them. I'm going to count to three, and just imagine them squeezing tighter with each count. One — feel them pressing tightly together, imagine it. Two — they are completely pressed together, very tightly. Three — completely fused solid, as if carved from a single piece of wood. Try to separate them — and you'll find the harder you try, the tighter they squeeze. Very good. Now do me a favor… Sleep! That's right… body relaxed, limp, and free… your hands release freely, and you go very deep into hypnosis."

Gentle Variant ("Maternal" Style)

"Clasp your hands together. Even better than before, imagine they are incredibly tightly locked. On the count of three they will become as if carved from a single piece of wood. One — feel them really squeezing together. Two — they squeeze tighter and tighter. Three — they are fused, carved from a single piece of wood. Try to separate them — and the harder you try, the tighter they squeeze. Very good… now do me a favor… Sleep… deeper still… allow yourself to relax much more… while I gently rotate your head, your legs become springy and you can stand, keeping perfect balance, and you'll relax even deeper…"

🔧 Technique Breakdown

  1. Clasp: Have the subject clasp their own hands together, fingers interlocked.
  2. Imagination: "Imagine your hands are pressed so tightly you can't separate them." The critical factor doesn't recognize imagination as a threat — it steps aside to watch.
  3. Count 1-2-3: Each number builds the expectation. "One — feel them pressing… Two — completely pressed… Three — fused solid, like one piece of wood."
  4. Test: "Try to separate them." The harder they try, the tighter they lock. This is self-generated catalepsy — the subject is already hypnotized.
  5. Fire "Sleep!": A light clap on the hands + "Sleep!" — the hands release and the subject drops into deep trance. The clap is a tap, not a hit.
  6. Deepen: Immediately begin head rotation or other deepening. The subject is already in trance — you're just formalizing and deepening.

💡 Key Insight

The critical factor is bypassed during the hand clasp, not during the "Sleep!" command. By the time you say "Sleep!", the subject is already hypnotized. The clasp test proves it to them: "I can't separate my hands — something is happening." This self-proof eliminates resistance and makes the formal induction nearly effortless.

The "Sleep!" + hand clap is just the formalization. You can make it as gentle or as sharp as the situation requires. Kein demonstrates both — the sharp version for stage, the "maternal" version for therapy.

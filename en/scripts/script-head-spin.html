Rapid Standing Head Spin

Head Spin Induction

From Adv1 — Rapid & Instant Inductions

When to Use

A standing rapid induction combining breath, voice command, and head rotation. The sharp "Sleep!" bypasses the critical factor; the head spin deepens and disorients. The subject remains standing in perfect balance throughout — no chair needed.

  • Stage demonstrations and group inductions
  • Analytical clients (voice shock bypasses analysis)
  • Any situation where a chair is unavailable

⏱ Time: ~5-7 seconds to trance, ~30 seconds deepening

⚠️ Safety & Setup

  • Ask about neck problems first. Never spin the head of someone with cervical spine issues.
  • Remove glasses. They will fly off during the head push.
  • Check your stance. Feet shoulder-width, balanced. The subject may fall toward you — be ready to catch.
  • Pre-talk the word "sleep." Explain: "When I say 'sleep,' I don't mean natural nighttime sleep. I mean instant, rapid relaxation of the nervous system."
  • Feel the neck muscles. Place your hand on the back of the neck. Neck muscles relax last — when they go soft, you've reached deep trance.

📜 Script

Full Head Spin Induction

"Take a good deep breath, fill your lungs with air, hold it… now exhale and… Sleep! That's right… every muscle relaxes, every nerve, every tissue of your body… your legs are solid and you can stand on them and sleep… while I rotate your head, just allow yourself to go much deeper… and the deeper you go, the better you feel… the better you feel, the more and more you relax… pleasant sensations spread through your body… pleasant thoughts flow through your mind… you just stand and relax in perfect balance…"

🔧 Technique Breakdown

  1. Position: Stand slightly to the side. Place one hand on the back of the subject's neck (upper shoulders area).
  2. Breath command: "Take a good deep breath, fill your lungs with air, hold it…" — this builds expectation.
  3. Fire "Sleep!": On the exhale, snap your fingers and fire the word "Sleep!" with sharp, commanding voice. Simultaneously give a small head push forward.
  4. Head spin: Immediately begin rotating the head gently side to side. The snap bypasses the critical factor; the head spin deepens through disorientation.
  5. Deepening: Continue the deepening patter while rotating. Feel the neck muscles through your hand — when they go completely soft, you're in deep somnambulism.
  6. If neck stays tense: Continue rotating with: "Allow yourself to relax even more with each gentle exhale…" Work the relaxation until the muscles release.

Short Version (for experienced subjects)

"Deep breath in… hold it… exhale and… Sleep! While I rotate your head, relax every muscle, every nerve, every tissue… deeper and deeper… your legs hold you firm, you stand in perfect balance… deeper still… very good…"

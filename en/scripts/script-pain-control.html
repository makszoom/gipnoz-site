Pain Control & Self-Anesthesia

From B11 — Pain Control & Anesthesia

When to Use

Hypnotic anesthesia works by redirecting attention. The mind can only focus on one thing at a time. If you fully engage the subject's attention on eye catalepsy or another sensation, pain signals cannot reach conscious awareness simultaneously. This technique was used by the Mayo brothers to reduce surgical anesthetic by up to 75%.

  • Chronic pain management with medical supervision
  • Minor medical and dental procedures
  • Teaching self-anesthesia for ongoing pain conditions

⏱ Time: ~5-10 minutes for full protocol

📜 Script

Anesthesia Induction Protocol

"Close your eyes. Relax your eyelids until they stop working. Test them. They're locked. Now send that feeling of relaxation down through your entire body... but especially into your right hand. Your right hand is going numb. Like Novocaine spreading through it. It feels thick, heavy, distant. When I touch it, you'll feel pressure but no discomfort. Your hand is completely protected."

Redirecting Numbness to Target Area

"Now send that relaxation into your [target area]. It's going numb. Like novocaine spreading. You'll feel pressure but no discomfort."

Post-Hypnotic Suggestion for Anesthesia

"This feeling will last as long as needed."

The Pinch Method (Self-Control)

"Now you control the discomfort — it doesn't control you."

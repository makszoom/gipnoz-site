Pendulum Induction & Ideomotor Signals

From B05 — Chevreul Pendulum & Ideomotor Responses

When to Use

The Chevreul pendulum demonstrates the subconscious mind's ability to produce physical movement without conscious effort. It serves as both a teaching tool and an induction method. When the pendulum moves, the subject receives undeniable proof that their subconscious is active and responding — and the critical factor is bypassed before formal hypnosis begins.

  • To demonstrate that the subconscious exists and can be influenced
  • As a gentle induction for skeptical or analytical subjects
  • To establish ideomotor finger signals for trance communication

⏱ Time: ~3-5 minutes

📜 Script

Pendulum Demonstration & Induction

"Hold the pendulum steady at eye level. Now, just imagine it swinging forward and back. Don't make it move with your muscles — just imagine the movement. Watch it... and as you imagine it, notice what happens. That's right, it's starting to move. Your subconscious is responding. Now make it go in a circle. Good. Your conscious mind isn't doing this — something else is. That 'something else' is your subconscious. And it's ready to work with you."

Transition to Deep Trance

"Your subconscious has just proven it can produce movement all by itself. That same force is now going to take you deeper. Let it happen. Every swing of the pendulum is bringing you closer to a state of complete relaxation. Soon your eyes will feel heavy, your body will feel still, and you'll be ready for the deepest level of trance."

From Pendulum to Formal Hypnosis

"Close your eyes. That same force that moved the pendulum is now going to take you deeper. Let it happen. Every muscle in your body is letting go. Your breathing is slowing down. Sleep — complete relaxation. And as you relax, that force that moved the pendulum is now moving through your entire body, carrying you deeper and deeper into a wonderful state of peace."

Self-Pendulum Practice

"Hold the pendulum steady. Imagine it swinging forward and back. Don't force it — just imagine. When it moves, mentally command it to stop. Now imagine a circle. Speed it up. Stop it again. You've just communicated with your subconscious."

Finger Signals Setup

"In a moment, your right index finger will lift when your subconscious wants to say 'yes.' Your left index finger will lift when it wants to say 'no.' A thumb twitch means you don't want to answer. Just let it happen — don't force it. Let's test: is your name [correct name]?" — watch for the yes signal. "Good. Is your name [wrong name]?" — watch for the no signal. "Your subconscious is now ready to communicate directly."

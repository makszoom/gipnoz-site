Bypass Catalepsy Quick

The Pretending Game (Eye Catalepsy)

From B03 — Bypassing the Critical Factor

When to Use

The pretending game is one of the fastest and most reliable methods for bypassing the critical factor. It works because the instruction to "pretend" is not recognized as a hypnotic suggestion — it is recognized as a game. The critical factor steps aside to witness what happens next, and in that moment, the bypass is complete.

  • First session with skeptical or analytical subjects
  • As a standalone demonstration of hypnotic phenomena
  • As the first step in the emergency anesthesia protocol

⏱ Time: ~30-60 seconds

📜 Script

Full Pretending Game Script

"Close your eyes. Now pretend — pretend as hard as you can — that your eyelids are glued shut. Imagine someone poured superglue on them and they are sealed tight. Pretend the way you did as a child when you played make-believe and it felt completely real. Your eyelids are fixed, sealed, locked together. Now try to open them. Try. You will find that the harder you try, the tighter they stay shut. That is it — your eyes are locked. Go ahead, try again. Good. Now stop trying and just relax."

Variant 2: Shortened Version

"Close your eyes. Pretend your eyelids are glued shut. Superglue — the strongest kind. Pretend so hard that when you try to open them, they simply will not move. Keep pretending — your eyes are fixed, sealed, locked. Now try to open them. Try. You will find they will not open. The harder you try, the tighter they stay. Good. Stop trying now and just relax deeply."

After Bypass: Acceptable Selective Thinking

Use this immediately after achieving eye catalepsy to install a protective frame:

"Nothing I do here will bother or disturb you in any way. Nothing will cause you any discomfort. Everything that happens will be perfectly acceptable to your mind. Your subconscious will accept each suggestion easily and naturally, because it knows you are safe and in control."

Self Finger Drop Practice

Finger Drop Self-Hypnosis

From B10 — Self-Hypnosis

When to Use

Self-hypnosis allows clients to reinforce suggestions between sessions and gives practitioners a tool for their own mental programming. The critical rule: do not mentally repeat the suggestion while in trance. Mental repetition re-engages the conscious mind and the critical factor. Read the suggestion, enter trance, and let the subconscious absorb it passively.

  • Daily self-improvement and habit change
  • Reinforcing therapeutic suggestions between sessions
  • Practitioner's own mental programming

⏱ Time: ~2-3 minutes per session

📜 Script

The Finger Drop Method — Full Protocol

Step 1: Write a single-sentence suggestion on a card. Example: "I am calm and focused." Step 2: Sit comfortably. Read the suggestion five times slowly, concentrating on each word. Step 3: Raise your right index finger. Step 4: Drop your finger — and immediately close your eyes. You are now in deep hypnosis. Step 5: Do NOT repeat the suggestion mentally. Simply relax and let the subconscious absorb it. Step 6: Stay in trance for approximately 1 minute. Step 7: Count from 1 to 3 and open your eyes, fully refreshed.

Self-Hypnosis Script

"Write your suggestion on a card. Sit comfortably. Read it five times slowly. Raise your right index finger. Drop it — and close your eyes. You are now in deep hypnosis. Do not repeat the suggestion. Simply relax. Let your subconscious receive it. After one minute, count from 1 to 3 and open your eyes, fully refreshed."

The One-Minute Rule

Suggestions in self-hypnosis should be 1 minute or less. Longer is not more effective — it engages the analytical mind. One concise, well-formed suggestion absorbed in deep trance is more powerful than 10 minutes of rambling.

Common Mistakes

(1) Repeating the suggestion during trance — don't. (2) Trying too hard to relax — relaxation comes from letting go, not forcing. (3) Setting time limits consciously — instead, trust the recording or your internal clock.

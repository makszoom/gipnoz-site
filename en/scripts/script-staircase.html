Staircase & Hallucination Deepening

From B07 — Deepening: Counting, Hallucinations & Post-Induction

When to Use

Deepening is the process of moving from light trance to the somnambulistic state required for therapeutic work. These visualization techniques bypass the critical factor through sensory immersion. Multiple methods exist because no single technique works for every subject.

  • After any induction to deepen to somnambulism
  • For creative/imaginative subjects who respond well to imagery
  • The staircase is the most reliable visualization deepening method

⏱ Time: ~2-3 minutes per technique

📜 Script

Staircase Deepening

"Imagine you're at the top of a beautiful staircase with ten steps going down. Each step takes you deeper into relaxation. Ten — step down and feel your body letting go. Nine — further down, your mind is becoming quiet. Eight — each step blocks out more of the outside world. Seven — your breathing is slowing. Six — deeper and deeper. Five — you feel peaceful. Four — completely safe. Three — almost at the bottom. Two — one more step. One — at the deepest, most peaceful place. Zero — total release."

Beach Visualization

"You're walking on a warm, beautiful beach. Feel the sand beneath your feet. The waves come in with each inhale and go out with each exhale. Breathe in... the wave rolls in. Breathe out... the wave rolls back, carrying all your tension with it. The sun is warm on your skin. The sound of the ocean is peaceful. With every wave, you sink deeper and deeper into relaxation."

Floating on Clouds

"You're floating on a soft, white cloud. The cloud is thick and comfortable, supporting your entire body. With each breath, the cloud gets thicker, softer, and you sink deeper into it. You feel weightless, peaceful, completely safe. Nothing can disturb you here. The cloud carries you higher... deeper... more relaxed than you've ever been."

Practice: Staircase (10 Steps)

"Picture a staircase with 10 steps going down. You're at the top. Ten — step down, more relaxed. Nine — further down, your body feels heavier. Eight — each step blocks out more of the outside world. Seven — your mind is becoming quiet. Six — deeper and deeper. Five — you feel peaceful. Four — completely safe. Three — almost at the bottom. Two — one more step. One — at the bottom, completely at peace. Zero — resting in the deepest relaxation."

Float Technique (Practice)

"Imagine you're floating on a soft, white cloud. The cloud is thick and comfortable, supporting every part of your body. With each exhale, the cloud gets thicker, softer, and you sink deeper into it. You feel completely weightless. You're safe, supported, and every breath takes you further into this state of peace. Nothing can disturb you here. Just floating... drifting... deeper and deeper."

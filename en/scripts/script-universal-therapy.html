Universal Therapy — Guilt & Shame Release

From Adv2 — Universal Therapy Protocol

When to Use

Universal Therapy is a directive suggestion technique that treats a range of conditions: fears, phobias, depression, anxiety, nail-biting, repressed anger, low self-esteem, guilt, self-criticism, envy, stress, fear of failure, fear of success. It works in deep somnambulism by addressing the root pattern rather than individual symptoms.

  • Guilt, shame, and low self-worth
  • Repressed anger and resentment
  • Fear and anxiety disorders rooted in past experience

⏱ Time: ~15-20 minutes

📜 Script

Guilt Release Protocol

"The guilt you carry does not belong to you. It was never yours to hold. Imagine it now as a heavy gray stone sitting in your chest. Feel its weight. Feel how long you have been carrying it. Now I want you to place that stone down. Put it on the ground beside you. Leave it here. [pause five seconds] Notice how much lighter you feel without it. The guilt is gone. It has no power over you anymore. You are free. Every breath you take fills you with lightness. With release. With peace."

Self-Worth Installation

"You are worthy. You have always been worthy. Nothing that happened to you, nothing that was done to you, nothing that was said to you can change that truth. From this moment forward, you see yourself clearly — not through the filter of what others did to you, not through the lens of their mistakes or their cruelty, but through your own eyes. And what you see is someone strong. Someone valuable. Someone deserving of love, of respect, of happiness. You are not what happened to you. You are who you chose to become. And from today, you choose to see yourself as worthy."

Comprehensive Protocol (Full Script)

"Nothing I say will disturb you. You are in complete control at all times. You will only accept what is right for you. [pause] Now I want you to go deep inside yourself to the part of you that holds the past. That part that has been carrying guilt. And I want you to forgive yourself. Not because what happened was your fault, but because holding the guilt only hurts you. Let it dissolve. [pause] Now go deeper to the part that holds shame. Shame is a lie that someone else taught you. It is not yours. Release it. Let it go with each exhale. [pause] Now go to the part that holds anger. The anger protected you once. Thank it. And let it go. You do not need it anymore. [pause] Now fill that empty space with self-worth. You are valuable. You matter. Your needs matter. Your feelings matter. [pause] From today, you respond to life differently. Where you once felt guilt, you now feel understanding. Where you once felt shame, you now feel dignity. Where you once felt fear, you now feel curiosity. This is your new pattern. It is natural. It is permanent. And it grows stronger every single day."

Practice: Guilt Release (Alternative)

"You are not responsible for what happened to you. The guilt does not belong to you. Imagine it as a dark gray cloud leaving your body with each exhale. Breathe out the guilt. Lighter and lighter. Let it drift away until there is nothing left but calm, peaceful lightness. You are free."

Waking Hypnosis Pre-talk

From B06 — Waking Hypnosis & Emotional Triggers

When to Use

Waking hypnosis is the art of delivering hypnotic suggestions while the subject believes they are in their normal conscious state. It allows you to begin therapeutic work during the pre-talk, before any formal induction. The primary mechanism: emotional engagement bypasses the critical factor while logic engages it.

  • During the pre-talk to begin bypassing the critical factor before formal induction
  • When you want to plant therapeutic seeds without formal trance
  • As a rapid trance technique using the eye contact method

⏱ Time: ~1-3 minutes

📜 Script

The Eye Contact Technique

"Look at me. Hold my gaze. Notice how your eyes want to look away — that's natural. Keep looking. Your breathing is already slowing down. Your eyes want to relax. That's good. Let them soften. You're already beginning to experience what hypnosis feels like."

Emotion-Based Suggestion (Example: Smoking)

"Imagine how good it will feel to breathe deeply without coughing. Imagine the pride when you tell someone 'I don't smoke anymore.' Feel that feeling for a moment — that relief, that freedom. That feeling is already yours. We're just going to make it permanent."

Emotion-Based Suggestion (General)

"Close your eyes and picture yourself in a month from now. You wake up feeling rested. You feel lighter, healthier, stronger. That image is real — your subconscious can see it. And whatever your subconscious can see, it can achieve. Let that feeling grow."

Waking Trance Pre-talk (Full)

"Look at me for a moment. You might feel a little uncomfortable — that's normal. But notice: your breathing is already slowing down. Your eyes want to relax. You're already beginning to experience what hypnosis feels like. Just keep looking at me. Let your eyes soften. Let your breathing slow. That's it. You're doing perfectly."

Emotional Frame for Weight Loss

"Imagine stepping on the scale and seeing the number you've been waiting for. Feel that feeling for a moment — that pride, that relief. That feeling is already yours. We're just going to make it permanent. Every time you make a healthy choice, that feeling grows stronger. Your body knows what to do. Your subconscious has already begun the transformation."

Rapid Fixation (Breath-Hold)

"Take a deep breath and hold it. Close your eyes. Now exhale — and let your eyes stay closed. Notice how your body instantly relaxes. That's waking hypnosis. You didn't need an induction — you just followed an instruction and your body responded. Take another deep breath, and this time when you exhale, let go of every muscle. Deeper and deeper with each breath."

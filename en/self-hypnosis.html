Self Hypnosis: Complete Guide & Free Techniques

A practical introduction to self-induced hypnosis based on Gerald Kein's Omni Hypnosis Training methodology.

What Is Self Hypnosis?

Self hypnosis is the ability to enter a state of focused attention and heightened suggestibility without an external hypnotist. It combines relaxation, concentration, and intentional suggestion to achieve goals such as stress relief, habit change, pain management, and performance enhancement.

Unlike stage hypnosis or clinical sessions led by a practitioner, self hypnosis puts you in full control. You decide the depth, the suggestions, and the duration. The techniques below are derived from Gerald Kein's Omni Hypnosis Training Center curriculum — the same methods taught to professional hypnotherapists.

Step-by-Step Self Hypnosis Technique

  1. Find a quiet place. Minimize distractions. Sit or lie comfortably.
  2. Set an intention. Define what you want to achieve in this session (e.g., relaxation, confidence, sleep).
  3. Fix your gaze. Pick a spot slightly above eye level. Stare at it until your eyelids feel heavy.
  4. Allow closure. When your eyes feel heavy, let them close naturally. Say internally: "My eyes are heavy and relaxed."
  5. Deepen with counting. Count down from 5 to 1, telling yourself you go deeper with each number. "Five... deeper and deeper... four..."
  6. Deliver suggestions. In a calm internal voice, state your positive suggestions in present tense: "I am calm and confident."
  7. Emerge gradually. Count from 1 to 5: "One, feeling refreshed... two, alert and aware... five, eyes open, fully awake."

Advanced Self Hypnosis Methods

Eye Fixation + Counting Down

Used in beginner lessons of the Omni Hypnosis Training. Fixate on a point until spontaneous eye closure, then count down from 10 with progressive relaxation of each body part.

Arm Pull Self-Induction

Raise your arm to eye level, gaze at your hand, then slowly lower it while internally repeating "sleep now." The physical movement anchors the hypnotic state.

Staircase Deepening

Visualize descending a staircase. Each step doubles your relaxation. This method is covered in the Core Concepts lesson of the full course.

Common Questions About Self Hypnosis

Can anyone learn self hypnosis?

Yes. The ability to enter hypnosis is universal. What varies is the speed of induction and depth of trance. Practice improves both.

How long should a self hypnosis session last?

Beginners: 5–10 minutes. Intermediate: 15–20 minutes. Advanced practitioners may extend sessions to 30–45 minutes for deep therapeutic work.

Is self hypnosis safe?

Yes, when used for relaxation, performance, and habit change. For serious psychological conditions, consult a licensed hypnotherapist.

How is self hypnosis different from meditation?

Meditation aims for awareness without attachment. Self hypnosis uses the same relaxed state to deliver targeted suggestions for specific outcomes.

How quickly will I see results?

Relaxation effects are immediate. Habit change and confidence building typically require daily practice for 2–4 weeks.

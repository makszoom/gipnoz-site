What Is Sleep Now?

Sleep Now is a Chrome extension that helps you fall asleep quickly using progressive relaxation — a technique from clinical hypnosis where you systematically release tension from each muscle group, from toes to forehead. The extension guides you through the process step by step, right in your browser.

It offers a 60-second instant mode for quick relief and a 10-minute extended script for a full-body relaxation session. A sleep timer lets you set a bedtime reminder, and ambient rain sound helps mask distracting noise. All offline, no account needed.

How It Works

Progressive relaxation (also called progressive muscle relaxation or PMR) works by guiding you to tense and then release each muscle group in sequence. This process activates the parasympathetic nervous system — the body's rest-and-digest mode — and lowers cortisol and heart rate, preparing your body for sleep.

Step 1 — Preparation

Close your eyes. Take three slow breaths. Allow your body to settle.

Step 2 — Toes and Feet

Tense the muscles in your toes for 5 seconds, then release. Feel the tension flow out. Move up to your calves, thighs, and hips.

Step 3 — Torso and Back

Tense your abdomen and lower back. Hold. Release. Let your stomach rise and fall with each breath.

Step 4 — Arms and Hands

Clench your fists. Hold. Release. Let the relaxation spread up your arms to your shoulders.

Step 5 — Neck and Face

Tense your neck, jaw, and forehead. Hold. Release. Feel your entire body sink into the surface beneath you.

Step 6 — Deepening

Count from 5 down to 1, with each number taking you deeper into relaxation. By 1, you are fully relaxed and ready for sleep.

Why It Works

Progressive relaxation was developed by Edmund Jacobson in the 1920s and later refined in clinical hypnosis by Gerald Kein at the Omni Hypnosis Training Center. Research has shown PMR reduces sleep onset latency by an average of 10–15 minutes and improves sleep quality scores.

The technique works because insomnia is often maintained by physical tension you are not consciously aware of. By systematically releasing that tension, you remove the physiological barrier to sleep. The guided script adds hypnotic deepening — a counting technique that guides the mind from active beta waves to relaxed alpha and theta states.
